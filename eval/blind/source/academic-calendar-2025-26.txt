NOTES

1. Each semester has 13 weeks of teaching, not counting the
   mid-semester break.

2. A student who drops a course after the add/drop deadline receives a
   grade of W (withdrawn) if the course is dropped before the
   mid-semester break. After the break the course is recorded as failed.

3. Students who are entered for a supplementary examination are
   informed of the exact day and room at least seven days before the
   window opens.

4. Graduation tickets are limited to three guests per graduating
   student.

5. Doctoral students are registered for the full year and are not
   bound by the teaching and examination dates above. Their annual
   progress reviews take place between 1 and 19 June 2026.

6. The Thistlewood Library stays open until 23:00 during revision days
   and examination periods, and closes at 21:00 at other times.

7. Changes to this calendar are announced by the Registrar on the
   student portal.

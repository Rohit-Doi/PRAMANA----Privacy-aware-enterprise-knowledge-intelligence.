# Salary Bands and Pay Rules 2026

**Halvorsen Institute of Technology — Human Resources Division**
**Classification:** CONFIDENTIAL — managers with staffing responsibility | **Effective:** 1 January 2026

## 1. Coverage

1.1 Every employee of the Institute other than the seven executive officers is placed in one of ten salary bands, G1 to G10. The pay of executive officers is set separately by the Remuneration Committee of the Board.

1.2 The ranges below include the cost-of-living adjustment of 3.1% applied on 1 January 2026. They are annual base salaries in US dollars for the Brackenridge Campus.

## 2. Band ranges

## 3. Regional multipliers

3.1 For the regional campuses the Brackenridge range is multiplied by the following factor.

| Campus | Multiplier |
|--------|------------|
| Toronto | 1.00 |
| Lisbon | 0.82 |
| Pune | 0.46 |

3.2 The multipliers are reviewed every two years. The next review is due in January 2027.

## 4. Progression within a band

4.1 An employee whose performance rating is "satisfactory" or better receives an annual increment of 2.5% on the review date shown in the personnel record, until the band maximum is reached.

4.2 An employee at the band maximum receives only cost-of-living adjustments.

4.3 A new employee is normally appointed in the lower third of the band. Appointment above the midpoint needs the approval of the Director of Human Resources.

## 5. Additions to base salary

5.1 **Market supplement.** Where a post cannot be filled at the band rate, a supplement of up to 15% of base salary may be paid. It needs the joint approval of the Director of Human Resources and the Chief Financial Officer and is reviewed every 24 months.

5.2 **On-call allowance.** Staff who are rostered to be on call outside working hours receive $180 for each week on call.

5.3 **Overtime.** Employees in bands G1 to G4 are paid for authorised overtime at one and a half times the hourly rate. Employees in band G5 and above are not paid overtime.

5.4 **Acting-up allowance.** An employee who covers a post in a higher band for more than 20 consecutive working days is paid the minimum of the higher band for the period of cover.

## 6. Probation

New employees serve a probation period of six months. For academic staff in bands G5 to G9 the probation period is twelve months.

## 7. Confidentiality

Individual salaries must not be discussed with anyone other than the employee concerned, the line manager and Human Resources.

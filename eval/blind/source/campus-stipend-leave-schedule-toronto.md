# Campus Stipend and Leave Schedule 2026 — Toronto Campus

**Halvorsen Institute of Technology — Human Resources Division**
**Region:** Americas (Toronto) | **Effective:** 1 January 2026 | **Classification:** Internal

Issued under the authority of the Campus Director, Toronto, and the Director of Human Resources. This schedule applies only to staff and stipend-holders whose contract names the Toronto campus as their place of work. All amounts are in US dollars.

## 1. Campus profile

The Toronto campus has 1,150 registered students and 96 members of staff.

## 2. Monthly research stipends

| Level | Who it covers | Monthly stipend |
|-------|---------------|-----------------|
| M1 | Master's research assistant | $1,450 |
| D1 | Doctoral student before the qualifying examination | $2,100 |
| D2 | Doctoral candidate after the qualifying examination | $2,350 |
| PD | Postdoctoral fellow | $4,300 |

2.1 Stipends are paid on the 25th of each month. Where the 25th is not a working day, payment is made on the previous working day.

2.2 A housing supplement of $350 per month is paid to stipend-holders at levels D1, D2 and PD who do not live in Institute accommodation.

2.3 A doctoral stipend is paid for a maximum of 48 months.

## 3. Teaching assistant work

Stipend-holders may work as teaching assistants for up to 10 hours per week. The rate is $28 per hour.

## 4. Leave for employees

| Type of leave | Entitlement per year |
|---------------|----------------------|
| Annual leave | 22 working days |
| Sick leave on full pay | 12 working days |
| Public holidays observed | 11 days |
| Compassionate leave | 5 working days |

4.1 Up to 5 days of unused annual leave may be carried into the next calendar year. Carried-over leave must be used by 31 March.

4.2 Parental leave is 26 weeks, of which the first 16 weeks are on full pay and the remainder is on half pay.

4.3 The campus is closed from 24 December to 2 January inclusive. Three of these days are deducted from annual leave.

## 5. Leave for stipend-holders

Stipend-holders are entitled to 15 working days of leave per year, agreed in advance with the supervisor. The stipend continues during approved leave.

## 6. Requests

Leave is requested in the HR portal at least ten working days in advance for any absence longer than three days. Queries: hr-toronto@halvorsen-institute.example.

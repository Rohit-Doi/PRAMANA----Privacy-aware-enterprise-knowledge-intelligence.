# Campus Stipend and Leave Schedule 2026 — Lisbon Campus

**Halvorsen Institute of Technology — Human Resources Division**
**Region:** Europe, Middle East and Africa (Lisbon) | **Effective:** 1 January 2026 | **Classification:** Internal

Issued under the authority of the Campus Director, Lisbon, and the Director of Human Resources. This schedule applies only to staff and stipend-holders whose contract names the Lisbon campus as their place of work. All amounts are stated in US dollars and are paid in local currency at the rate fixed by the Finance Division on the first working day of each quarter.

## 1. Campus profile

The Lisbon campus has 870 registered students and 71 members of staff.

## 2. Monthly research stipends

| Level | Who it covers | Monthly stipend |
|-------|---------------|-----------------|
| M1 | Master's research assistant | $1,180 |
| D1 | Doctoral student before the qualifying examination | $1,720 |
| D2 | Doctoral candidate after the qualifying examination | $1,950 |
| PD | Postdoctoral fellow | $3,400 |

2.1 Stipends are paid on the 28th of each month. Where the 28th is not a working day, payment is made on the previous working day.

2.2 A housing supplement of $260 per month is paid to stipend-holders at levels D1, D2 and PD who do not live in Institute accommodation.

2.3 A doctoral stipend is paid for a maximum of 42 months.

## 3. Teaching assistant work

Stipend-holders may work as teaching assistants for up to 8 hours per week. The rate is $21 per hour.

## 4. Leave for employees

| Type of leave | Entitlement per year |
|---------------|----------------------|
| Annual leave | 25 working days |
| Sick leave on full pay | 15 working days |
| Public holidays observed | 13 days |
| Compassionate leave | 5 working days |

4.1 Up to 7 days of unused annual leave may be carried into the next calendar year. Carried-over leave must be used by 30 April.

4.2 Parental leave is 30 weeks, of which the first 20 weeks are on full pay and the remainder is on half pay.

4.3 The campus is closed from 4 to 15 August inclusive. Five of these days are deducted from annual leave.

## 5. Leave for stipend-holders

Stipend-holders are entitled to 18 working days of leave per year, agreed in advance with the supervisor. The stipend continues during approved leave.

## 6. Requests

Leave is requested in the HR portal at least fifteen working days in advance for any absence longer than three days. Queries: hr-lisbon@halvorsen-institute.example.

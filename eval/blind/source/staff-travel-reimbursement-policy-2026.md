# Staff Travel and Expense Reimbursement Policy 2026

**Halvorsen Institute of Technology — Finance Division**
**Reference:** FIN-TRV-04 | **Effective:** 1 February 2026 | **Classification:** Internal

This policy replaces the Staff Travel and Expense Reimbursement Policy 2024 (FIN-TRV-03). Trips that were approved before 1 February 2026 are settled under the 2024 policy.

## 1. Purpose

This policy sets out what employees of the Institute may claim when they travel on Institute business, and how claims are approved and paid. It applies to academic and professional staff at all four campuses. Students are not covered by this policy.

## 2. Approval before travel

2.1 Every trip must be approved in the expense system before any booking is made. The approver depends on the estimated total cost of the trip:

| Estimated cost of trip | Approver |
|------------------------|----------|
| Up to $2,500 | Head of Department |
| Above $2,500 and up to $7,500 | Dean or Director of the division |
| Above $7,500 | Chief Financial Officer |

2.2 Nobody may approve their own travel. Travel by a Dean or Director is approved by the Provost.

2.3 The Institute funds attendance at no more than two conferences per member of staff in a fiscal year. Conferences paid for entirely from an external research grant do not count towards this limit.

## 3. Bookings

3.1 Flights and hotels must be booked through the Meridian Travel Desk at least 21 days before departure. Bookings made outside the travel desk are reimbursed only up to the fare the travel desk would have obtained.

3.2 Economy class is used for flights with a scheduled duration under six hours. Premium economy may be booked for flights of six hours or more. Business class is not permitted unless the President has approved it in writing.

3.3 Hotel accommodation is reimbursed at cost up to $185 per night for domestic travel and $260 per night for international travel.

## 4. Daily allowance

4.1 A per diem is paid for each night away to cover meals and incidental expenses. Receipts are not required for the per diem.

| Destination | Per diem |
|-------------|----------|
| Domestic | $110 |
| International, Tier 1 cities | $165 |
| International, Tier 2 cities | $120 |

4.2 The list of Tier 1 cities is maintained by the Finance Division. Any city not on the list is Tier 2.

4.3 Where a meal is provided by a conference or host, the per diem for that day is reduced by 25% for each meal provided.

## 5. Use of private vehicles

5.1 Staff who use their own vehicle are reimbursed at $0.61 per mile, up to a maximum of 400 miles per round trip. Fuel is not reimbursed separately.

## 6. Advances

6.1 An advance of up to 75% of the estimated cost of the trip may be requested once the trip is approved. The advance is settled against the claim.

## 7. Claims

7.1 A claim must be submitted within 21 days of the traveller's return.

7.2 A claim submitted more than 60 days after return is not paid.

7.3 A receipt is required for every item above $15, other than the per diem.

7.4 Approved claims are paid within ten working days by bank transfer.

## 8. Items that are not reimbursed

Alcohol, fines, personal entertainment, airline club memberships, seat-selection charges and travel costs of accompanying family members are not reimbursed.

## 9. Misuse

A claim that is found to be false is referred to the Director of Human Resources and may lead to disciplinary action.

*Approved by the Chief Financial Officer.*

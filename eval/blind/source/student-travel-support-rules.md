# Student Conference and Fieldwork Travel Support Rules

Halvorsen Institute of Technology — Office of Academic Affairs and Research

Effective 18 August 2025

## Who may apply

Any registered student of the Institute may apply for support to travel to a conference, a competition or approved fieldwork. Support for a conference is given only when the student is presenting a paper or a poster that has been accepted. Attendance without presenting is not funded.

## How much can be awarded

The amount a student can receive in one academic year depends on the level of study. An undergraduate may receive up to $600, a master's student up to $900 and a doctoral student up to $1,500. In addition, a doctoral student may receive support for one international conference of up to $2,200 once during the whole period of the doctorate. Awards cannot be carried forward to a later year.

## What the award covers

Travel is by the cheapest reasonable route. Flights are in economy class regardless of the length of the journey. Accommodation is paid at cost up to $95 per night, and students travelling together are expected to share rooms. A daily allowance of $45 is paid for travel within the country and $70 for travel abroad. Conference registration fees are covered at the student rate only.

## How to apply

Applications are made on Form ST-2 and must be received at least six weeks before the date of departure. They are decided by the Student Travel Committee, which meets on the first Tuesday of every month. A letter of support from the student's supervisor or course leader must be attached. The Committee does not consider applications made after the travel has taken place.

## Advances

Students normally pay their own costs and claim afterwards. Doctoral students may ask for an advance of up to 50% of the award. No advance is available to undergraduate or master's students.

## Claiming after the trip

A claim must be submitted within 14 days of return. An original receipt is needed for every item, however small, except the daily allowance. Claims are paid to the bank account held on the student record. A student who does not submit a claim in time loses the award.

## Reporting

Within 30 days of return the student must send a one-page report to the Committee. A student who has not reported on an earlier trip will not be considered for a new award.

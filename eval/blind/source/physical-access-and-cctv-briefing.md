# Physical Access Control and CCTV: Briefing for Wardens and Security Leads

Halvorsen Institute of Technology — Campus Security. CONFIDENTIAL. Effective 1 October 2025.

## Why this briefing

- Sets out the access standard for the Brackenridge Campus from 1 October 2025.
- Issued by the Head of Campus Security; reviewed every 12 months.
- Applies to security officers, hall wardens and building managers.

## Three access zones

- Zone 1 — public areas: reception halls, the Greaves Refectory, the ground floor of the Thistlewood Library. Open without a badge from 07:00 to 22:00.
- Zone 2 — staff and student areas: teaching rooms, laboratories, offices, halls of residence. A valid badge is needed at all times.
- Zone 3 — restricted areas: the data centre in the basement of the Vasterling Building, the examination paper vault (room B-12, Calder Examination Centre) and the chemical store of the Okonkwo-Reyes Engineering Hall.

## Rules for Zone 3

- Entry needs a badge and a six-digit PIN. PINs are changed every 90 days.
- The examination paper vault follows a two-person rule: nobody enters alone.
- Access lists are approved by the owner of the area and re-confirmed every quarter.
- Contractors enter only with an escort who stays for the whole visit.

## Badges

- A lost badge must be reported to the Security Control Room at once; it is disabled within one hour of the report.
- Replacement costs $20 for students and is free for staff on the first occasion.
- Badges of leavers are disabled at 18:00 on the last day of service.
- Sharing a badge or holding a door for an unknown person is a disciplinary matter.

## CCTV

- 318 cameras cover the campus; none is placed inside bedrooms, bathrooms or changing rooms.
- Recordings are kept for 45 days. Recordings of Zone 3 are kept for 90 days.
- Footage is released only on the written request of the Head of Campus Security or the General Counsel.
- Live viewing is limited to the Security Control Room, staffed 24 hours a day.

## Visitors

- All visitors sign in at a reception desk and wear a dated visitor badge.
- Visitor logs are kept for 12 months.
- Visitors to Zone 2 offices are collected and returned to reception by their host.

## Reporting an incident

- Call the Security Control Room on extension 4400.
- Any breach of Zone 3 is reported to the Head of Campus Security within 30 minutes.
- A written incident form follows within 24 hours.
- Night patrols run every two hours between 22:00 and 06:00.

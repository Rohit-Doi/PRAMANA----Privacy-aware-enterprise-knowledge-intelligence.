# Staff Travel and Expense Reimbursement Policy 2024

**Halvorsen Institute of Technology — Finance Division**
**Reference:** FIN-TRV-03 | **Effective:** 1 July 2024 | **Classification:** Internal

## 1. Purpose

This policy sets out what employees of the Institute may claim when they travel on Institute business, and how claims are approved and paid. It applies to academic and professional staff at all four campuses. It does not apply to students, whose travel support is governed by separate rules.

## 2. Approval before travel

2.1 Every trip must be approved before any booking is made. The approver depends on the estimated total cost of the trip:

| Estimated cost of trip | Approver |
|------------------------|----------|
| Up to $2,000 | Head of Department |
| Above $2,000 and up to $5,000 | Dean or Director of the division |
| Above $5,000 | Chief Financial Officer |

2.2 Nobody may approve their own travel.

## 3. Bookings

3.1 Flights and hotels must be booked through the Meridian Travel Desk at least 14 days before departure.

3.2 Economy class is used for flights with a scheduled duration under eight hours. Premium economy may be booked for flights of eight hours or more.

3.3 Hotel accommodation is reimbursed at cost up to $160 per night for domestic travel and $230 per night for international travel.

## 4. Daily allowance

4.1 A per diem is paid for each night away to cover meals and incidental expenses. Receipts are not required for the per diem.

| Destination | Per diem |
|-------------|----------|
| Domestic | $85 |
| International, Tier 1 cities | $140 |
| International, Tier 2 cities | $105 |

4.2 The list of Tier 1 cities is maintained by the Finance Division. Any city not on the list is Tier 2.

4.3 Where a meal is provided by a conference or host, the per diem for that day is reduced by 20% for each meal provided.

## 5. Use of private vehicles

5.1 Staff who use their own vehicle are reimbursed at $0.52 per mile. Fuel is not reimbursed separately.

## 6. Advances

6.1 An advance of up to 60% of the estimated cost of the trip may be requested once the trip is approved. The advance is settled against the claim.

## 7. Claims

7.1 A claim must be submitted within 30 days of the traveller's return.

7.2 A receipt is required for every item above $25, other than the per diem.

7.3 Approved claims are paid with the next monthly payroll.

## 8. Items that are not reimbursed

Alcohol, fines, personal entertainment, airline club memberships and travel costs of accompanying family members are not reimbursed.

*Approved by the Chief Financial Officer.*

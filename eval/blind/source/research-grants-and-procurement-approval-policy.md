# Research Grants and Procurement Approval Policy

**Halvorsen Institute of Technology — Research Office**
**Reference:** RES-POL-11 | **Effective:** 1 May 2025 | **Classification:** Internal

## 1. Purpose and scope

1.1 This policy governs how research funding is applied for, how it is spent, and who must approve purchases made from research budgets. It applies to every principal investigator and research group at all four campuses.

1.2 Purchases made from teaching or administrative budgets are outside this policy.

## 2. Applying for external funding

2.1 A proposal to an external funder must be lodged with the Research Office at least ten working days before the funder's deadline, so that the budget and the Institute's commitments can be checked.

2.2 The Institute charges overhead at 22% of direct costs on externally funded projects. Where the funder is a registered charity the rate is 10%.

2.3 A proposal that commits the Institute to matching funds needs the written agreement of the Chief Financial Officer before it is submitted.

## 3. Internal seed grants

3.1 The Research Office awards seed grants of up to $15,000 to help staff prepare larger external bids.

3.2 There are two rounds each year, with deadlines of 15 March and 15 October. A seed grant must be spent within 12 months of the award letter.

3.3 A member of staff may hold only one seed grant at a time.

## 4. Approval of purchases from research budgets

4.1 The approver depends on the value of the purchase, excluding tax.

| Value of purchase | Approval required |
|-------------------|-------------------|
| Up to $5,000 | Principal investigator |
| Above $5,000 and up to $40,000 | Head of Department |
| Above $40,000 | Dean of Research |
| Above $150,000 | Dean of Research and Chief Financial Officer |
| Above $500,000 | Finance and Audit Committee of the Board, on the recommendation of the Dean of Research |

4.2 Where a purchase needs the approval of the Dean of Research, the request is made on Form RP-4 through the Research Office, which checks that funds are available before passing it on. Approval must be obtained before the order is placed. Approval given afterwards is not valid.

4.3 A purchase must not be divided into smaller orders in order to bring it below a threshold.

## 5. Quotations and tenders

5.1 Three written quotations are required for any purchase above $10,000.

5.2 An open tender run by the Procurement Office is required for any purchase above $100,000.

5.3 A single supplier may be used without competition only where the Head of Procurement certifies that no alternative exists.

## 6. Conflicts of interest

Anyone who requests or approves a purchase must declare any personal or financial connection with the supplier. Declarations are renewed every year.

## 7. Equipment

7.1 Any item of equipment costing more than $2,500 is recorded in the asset register and tagged within 30 days of delivery.

7.2 Equipment bought with research funds belongs to the Institute unless the funder's conditions say otherwise.

## 8. Ethics

No research funds are released for a project involving human participants, personal data or animals until the Research Ethics Committee has given clearance.

## 9. End of a project

9.1 A request to extend a project without additional cost must be made at least 60 days before the end date.

9.2 Unspent internal funds return to the Research Office.

*Approved by the Academic Council on the recommendation of the Dean of Research.*

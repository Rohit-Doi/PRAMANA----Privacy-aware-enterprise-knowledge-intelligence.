# Register of Senior Officers and Committee Chairs 2025–26

**Halvorsen Institute of Technology — Office of the Secretary to the Board**
**Effective:** 1 September 2025 | **Classification:** Public

This register records who holds each senior office of the Institute and who chairs each standing committee for the academic year 2025–26. It is updated when an appointment changes.

## 1. The Institute at a glance

The Institute has about 6,400 students and 540 members of staff. The main campus is the Brackenridge Campus in Port Aldane, with 2,960 students and 255 staff. The three regional campuses are in Toronto, Lisbon and Pune.

# Executive Remuneration Schedule 2026

**Halvorsen Institute of Technology — Remuneration Committee of the Board**
**Classification:** RESTRICTED — Remuneration Committee, Chair of the Board and Director of Human Resources only
**Decided:** 12 February 2026 | **Effective:** 1 July 2026

## 1. Basis

1.1 This schedule records the decisions of the Remuneration Committee at its meeting of 12 February 2026 on the pay of the seven executive officers who are outside the general salary bands.

1.2 Base salaries rise by 3.5% compared with the schedule in force since 1 July 2025. Amounts are annual and in US dollars.

## 2. Base salary and performance award

| Office | Base salary from 1 July 2026 | Maximum performance award |
|--------|------------------------------|---------------------------|
| President | $412,000 | 20% of base salary |
| Provost | $298,000 | 15% of base salary |
| Chief Financial Officer | $264,000 | 15% of base salary |
| General Counsel | $231,000 | 10% of base salary |
| Chief Information Security Officer | $182,000 | 10% of base salary |
| Registrar | $176,000 | 10% of base salary |
| Director of Human Resources | $171,000 | 10% of base salary |

## 3. Performance award

3.1 The performance award is decided by the Committee each September for the fiscal year that ended on 30 June.

3.2 For the President the award is weighted as follows: financial result against budget 30%, student enrolment and retention 25%, research income 20%, staff engagement survey 10%, and the Committee's overall judgement 15%.

3.3 For the other executive officers, half of the award depends on Institute-wide measures and half on objectives agreed with the President.

3.4 An award may be recovered by the Institute for up to three years after payment if the results on which it was based are later found to have been misstated.

## 4. Allowances

4.1 The President receives a housing allowance of $36,000 per year in place of an official residence.

4.2 The Provost and the Chief Financial Officer each receive a car allowance of $9,600 per year.

4.3 No other allowances are paid to executive officers. Executive officers are not eligible for staff housing, the on-call allowance or market supplements.

## 5. Notice and severance

5.1 The notice period is six months on either side for the President and three months for the other executive officers.

5.2 Where an appointment is ended by the Institute other than for misconduct, severance equal to six months of base salary is paid. No severance is paid on the expiry of a fixed term.

## 6. President's reappointment

The Committee recommends to the Board that the President be reappointed for a further term of four years from 1 July 2026 on the terms in this schedule.

## 7. Disclosure

Only the total remuneration of the President is disclosed in the published annual report. The remainder of this schedule is not disclosed.

*Signed by the Chair of the Remuneration Committee.*

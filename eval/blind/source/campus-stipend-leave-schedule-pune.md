# Campus Stipend and Leave Schedule 2026 — Pune Campus

**Halvorsen Institute of Technology — Human Resources Division**
**Region:** Asia-Pacific (Pune) | **Effective:** 1 January 2026 | **Classification:** Internal

Issued under the authority of the Campus Director, Pune, and the Director of Human Resources. This schedule applies only to staff and stipend-holders whose contract names the Pune campus as their place of work. All amounts are stated in US dollars and are paid in local currency at the rate fixed by the Finance Division on the first working day of each quarter.

## 1. Campus profile

The Pune campus has 1,420 registered students and 118 members of staff. It is the largest of the three regional campuses.

## 2. Monthly research stipends

| Level | Who it covers | Monthly stipend |
|-------|---------------|-----------------|
| M1 | Master's research assistant | $620 |
| D1 | Doctoral student before the qualifying examination | $940 |
| D2 | Doctoral candidate after the qualifying examination | $1,090 |
| PD | Postdoctoral fellow | $1,850 |

2.1 Stipends are paid on the last working day of each month.

2.2 A housing supplement of $140 per month is paid to stipend-holders at levels D1, D2 and PD who do not live in Institute accommodation.

2.3 A doctoral stipend is paid for a maximum of 54 months.

## 3. Teaching assistant work

Stipend-holders may work as teaching assistants for up to 12 hours per week. The rate is $9 per hour.

## 4. Leave for employees

| Type of leave | Entitlement per year |
|---------------|----------------------|
| Annual leave | 20 working days |
| Sick leave on full pay | 10 working days |
| Public holidays observed | 16 days |
| Compassionate leave | 7 working days |

4.1 Up to 10 days of unused annual leave may be carried into the next calendar year. Carried-over leave must be used by 30 June.

4.2 Parental leave is 26 weeks, all of it on full pay.

4.3 The campus is closed for five working days during the week of Diwali. These days are in addition to annual leave and to the public holidays listed above.

## 5. Leave for stipend-holders

Stipend-holders are entitled to 14 working days of leave per year, agreed in advance with the supervisor. The stipend continues during approved leave.

## 6. Requests

Leave is requested in the HR portal at least seven working days in advance for any absence longer than three days. Queries: hr-pune@halvorsen-institute.example.

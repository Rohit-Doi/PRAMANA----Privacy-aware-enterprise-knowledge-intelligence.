Tuition and Fee Schedule 2025–26

Halvorsen Institute of Technology — Finance Division. This schedule applies to the academic year that begins on 1 September 2025 and takes effect on 1 August 2025. All amounts are in US dollars.

Tuition

Tuition is charged per academic year. The same rates apply at the Brackenridge, Toronto, Lisbon and Pune campuses. Rates for 2025–26 are 3.8% higher than in the previous year.

Other academic fees

The application fee is $65 and is not refundable. Every student pays a registration fee of $250 each year. Students on programmes with laboratory courses pay a laboratory fee of $380 per semester. The graduation fee is $120 and is charged in the final semester.

Residence fees

Rooms in the student halls on the Brackenridge Campus are charged per semester according to the residence band of the hall.

  • Band 1: $2,480 per semester
  • Band 2: $3,150 per semester
  • Band 3: $3,920 per semester
  • Band 4: $4,600 per semester

Meal plans are charged separately and are described in the residence handbook.

When to pay

Tuition for the year may be paid in three equal instalments, due on 15 August 2025, 15 November 2025 and 15 February 2026. A student who pays the whole year's tuition by 31 July 2025 receives a discount of 2% on tuition. The discount does not apply to residence fees or other fees.

Interest of 1.5% per month is charged on any amount that is overdue. A student whose account is more than 60 days overdue may not register for the following semester.

Refunds

A student who withdraws before the first day of teaching receives a full refund of tuition paid. A student who withdraws during the first three weeks of teaching receives a refund of 50% of that semester's tuition. No refund is given after the end of the third week. The registration fee is not refundable.

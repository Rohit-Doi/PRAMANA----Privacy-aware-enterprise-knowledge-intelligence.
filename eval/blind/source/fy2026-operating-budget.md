# Operating Budget for Fiscal Year 2026

**Halvorsen Institute of Technology — Finance Division**
**Classification:** CONFIDENTIAL — budget holders and members of the Finance and Audit Committee
**Fiscal year:** 1 July 2025 to 30 June 2026 | **Approved by the Board:** 19 June 2025 | **Effective:** 1 July 2025

## 1. Introduction

1.1 This document sets out the approved operating budget of the Institute for fiscal year 2026 (FY2026). It covers all four campuses. All figures are in millions of US dollars unless stated otherwise.

1.2 The budget was prepared by the Chief Financial Officer, reviewed by the Finance and Audit Committee on 4 June 2025 and approved by the Board of Governors on 19 June 2025.

## 2. Planning assumptions

2.1 Total enrolment of 6,400 students across the four campuses.

2.2 A tuition increase of 3.8% for the 2025–26 academic year.

2.3 A cost-of-living adjustment to salaries of 3.1%, applied on 1 January 2026, and an annual increment of 2.5% for eligible staff.

2.4 Occupancy of the student halls on the Brackenridge Campus at 96% of the 1,200 beds.

2.5 A draw of 4.0% from the endowment.

4.5 **Staffing establishment.** The salary budget funds 540 posts: 298 academic posts and 242 professional and support posts. By campus the establishment is 255 at Brackenridge, 96 at Toronto, 71 at Lisbon and 118 at Pune. The budget assumes that 3% of posts are vacant at any time; a department that fills every post for the whole year will therefore overspend its salary line unless the Finance Division is told in advance.

## 6. Reserves

6.1 The Capital Reserve Fund held $41.2 million on 1 July 2025. It may be used only for the purchase or construction of land, buildings and major equipment, and only by resolution of the Board.

6.2 The operating contingency is set at 3% of budgeted expenditure, which is $6.3 million. The Chief Financial Officer may release up to $500,000 from the contingency for a single purpose; a larger release needs the approval of the Finance and Audit Committee.

6.3 The Board's policy is that unrestricted reserves should not fall below the equivalent of 60 days of operating expenditure.

## 8. Rules for budget holders

8.1 **Virement.** A budget holder may move up to 10% of a budget line to another line within the same cost centre without further approval. Any movement between cost centres needs the approval of the Chief Financial Officer. Any single movement above $250,000 needs the approval of the Finance and Audit Committee.

8.2 **Pay and non-pay.** Savings on salary lines may not be used for non-pay spending.

8.3 **Overspending.** A cost centre that is forecast to overspend by more than 2% must submit a recovery plan to the Finance Division within 15 working days of the forecast.

8.4 **Carry-forward.** Unspent non-pay budgets lapse on 30 June 2026. Research grant balances are carried forward under the terms of the grant.

8.5 **New posts.** No new permanent post may be advertised unless it appears in the approved establishment list or has been approved by the President.

## 10. Principal risks to the budget

10.1 **Enrolment.** Each shortfall of 100 students against the assumption of 6,400 reduces tuition revenue by about $2.3 million.

10.2 **Hall occupancy.** Each percentage point of occupancy below the 96% assumption reduces residence and dining income by about $0.16 million.

10.3 **Research income.** Grant income is recognised as the related spending occurs, so delays in starting projects move revenue into the following year.

10.4 **Energy prices.** Utilities are budgeted at $5.9 million within estates. A 10% rise in prices would add about $0.6 million.

10.5 **Exchange rates.** The Lisbon and Pune campuses spend in local currency. The budget was set at the rates of 1 May 2025, and a movement of 5% against the dollar changes the cost of those two campuses by about $2.3 million in total. The Finance Division does not hedge this exposure.

## 11. Budget calendar for the following year

11.2 The Chief Financial Officer meets each Dean and Director during March and April 2026 to agree the drafts.

11.3 The consolidated budget goes to the Finance and Audit Committee on 3 June 2026 and to the Board of Governors on 18 June 2026.

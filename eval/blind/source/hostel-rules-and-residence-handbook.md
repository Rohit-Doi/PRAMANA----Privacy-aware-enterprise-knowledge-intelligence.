# Student Hostel Rules and Residence Handbook 2025–26

**Halvorsen Institute of Technology — Residence Office, Brackenridge Campus**
**Effective:** 15 July 2025 | **Classification:** Public

## 1. Welcome

This handbook explains how the student residences on the Brackenridge Campus are run and what is expected of everyone who lives in them. By accepting a room you agree to follow these rules. The residences at the regional campuses publish their own local rules.

## 2. The halls

The Brackenridge Campus has four halls with 1,200 beds in total.

| Hall | Beds | Room type | Residence band | Mainly for |
|------|------|-----------|----------------|------------|
| Fenwick House | 356 | Twin room, shared bathroom | Band 1 | First-year undergraduates |
| Aldermoor Hall | 412 | Single room, shared bathroom | Band 2 | Undergraduates |
| Lindgate Court | 288 | Single room, en-suite | Band 3 | Final-year and master's students |
| Tamsin Lodge | 144 | Studio | Band 4 | Doctoral students |

The fee for each residence band is published in the Institute's Tuition and Fee Schedule for the year. Residence fees are charged per semester and are due on the same dates as tuition instalments.

## 3. Applying for and accepting a room

3.1 Applications for the autumn semester open on 1 April and close on 15 June. Rooms are offered first to first-year students and to students whose home is more than 60 miles from the campus.

3.2 An offer must be accepted within ten days. Acceptance requires a refundable deposit of $400. The deposit is returned within 30 days of final check-out, less any charges for damage or unpaid fines.

3.3 A resident who withdraws after the start of the semester remains liable for the residence fee for that semester unless the Residence Office is able to re-let the room.

## 4. Arrival and departure

4.1 Check-in for the 2025–26 academic year takes place on 23 and 24 August 2025 between 09:00 and 18:00. Keys and access cards are collected from the porter's desk of the hall concerned.

4.2 Rooms must be vacated by 12:00 on the day after the resident's last examination of the spring semester.

4.3 Residents who need to stay during a vacation may do so in Lindgate Court only, at $22 per night, and must apply at least 14 days in advance.

4.4 Late check-out without permission is charged at $50 per day.

## 5. Daily life in hall

5.1 **Quiet hours** are from 22:30 to 07:00 every day. During examination periods quiet hours begin at 21:00.

5.2 **Gates.** Hall gates close at 23:30 from Sunday to Thursday and at 00:30 on Friday and Saturday. Residents returning later must sign the late register at the porter's desk. A first-year resident who signs the late register more than four times in a month will be asked to meet the hall warden.

5.3 **Visitors.** Visitors are welcome between 09:00 and 21:00. Every visitor must sign in at the porter's desk and leave an identity document, which is returned on departure. The resident is responsible for the conduct of the visitor.

5.4 **Electrical appliances.** Kettles rated at up to 1,500 watts, desk lamps, laptop computers and phone chargers are allowed. Hotplates, toasters, room heaters, irons and extension leads without a fuse are not allowed in rooms. Each floor has a pantry with a microwave oven and an ironing point.

5.5 **Smoking** and vaping are prohibited inside all halls and within ten metres of any entrance.

5.6 **Room inspections** take place once a month. Residents receive 48 hours' notice. Staff may enter without notice only in an emergency or where there is an immediate risk to safety.

5.7 **Room changes** may be requested from the third week of the semester. An approved change costs $40.

## 6. Meals and laundry

6.1 Meals are served in the Greaves Refectory. Breakfast is from 07:00 to 09:30, lunch from 12:00 to 14:30 and dinner from 18:30 to 21:00.

6.2 Two meal plans are offered. Residents of Fenwick House must take one of them; for other halls the plan is optional.

| Meal plan | Meals per week | Price per semester |
|-----------|----------------|--------------------|
| Standard | 14 | $1,480 |
| Full | 19 | $1,820 |

6.3 Each hall has a laundry room open from 06:00 to 22:00. A wash costs $2.50 and a drying cycle costs $1.50, paid with the access card.

6.4 A resident with a medical or religious dietary need should tell the refectory manager in the first week of the semester. A resident who misses a meal because of a timetabled class may collect a packed meal if it is ordered by 20:00 on the previous evening.

## 7. Cleaning, repairs and services

7.1 **Cleaning.** Corridors, bathrooms and pantries are cleaned every day. Residents clean their own rooms. In Lindgate Court the en-suite bathrooms are cleaned by staff once a fortnight.

7.2 **Repairs.** Faults are reported through the residence page of the student portal. Urgent faults, such as a water leak or a door that will not lock, are attended to within four hours. Other repairs are completed within three working days. Residents are charged for damage they cause, at cost.

7.3 **Internet.** Every room has wireless coverage and one wired network socket. Residents may not connect their own wireless routers.

7.4 **Post and parcels.** Letters and parcels are delivered to the porter's desk and are held for 14 days. The resident is told by e-mail when a parcel arrives. Uncollected items are returned to the sender.

7.5 **Bicycles.** Covered bicycle racks are provided beside each hall. Bicycles must be registered with the porter, who issues a numbered tag free of charge. Bicycles may not be kept in rooms or corridors.

7.6 **Storage.** Over the summer vacation a returning resident may leave up to two boxes in the hall store for $15 per box.

7.7 **Common rooms.** Each hall has a common room open from 07:00 to 23:00. Common rooms may be booked for a private event through the warden at least five days ahead; an event must end by 22:00.

## 8. Safety

8.1 Fire drills are held twice each semester. Everyone in the building must leave when the alarm sounds and gather at the assembly point shown on the notice inside the room door.

8.2 Tampering with a smoke detector, extinguisher or fire door is treated as a serious offence.

8.3 The Health Centre is in the Harcastle Wing and is open from 08:00 to 20:00 every day during the semester. Outside those hours the duty warden can be reached on +1 416 555 0190, and the Security Control Room on extension 4400.

8.4 Residents should lock their doors when they leave. The Institute does not insure residents' personal belongings.

## 9. Charges and fines

| Item | Charge |
|------|--------|
| Lost room key | $35 |
| Lost access card | $20 |
| Late check-out, per day | $50 |
| Smoking or vaping in hall | $150 |
| Tampering with fire equipment | $300 |
| Prohibited appliance found in room | $60 and removal of the appliance |

Charges are added to the student account and must be paid within 30 days.

## 10. Discipline

10.1 Each hall has a warden who lives on site. Wardens may issue written warnings for breaches of these rules.

10.2 A resident who receives three written warnings in one semester is referred to the Residence Committee. The Committee may impose a fine, move the resident to another hall or end the resident's licence to occupy the room.

10.3 Where a licence is ended the resident is given seven days' notice to leave. In a case involving violence or a serious risk to others the Chief Warden may require the resident to leave within 24 hours.

10.4 A resident may appeal a decision of the Residence Committee to the Dean of Students within five working days.

## 11. Contact

The Residence Office is in Aldermoor Hall, room G-04, and is open on weekdays from 08:30 to 17:00. The office of the Chief Warden is in the same corridor. E-mail: residences@halvorsen-institute.example.

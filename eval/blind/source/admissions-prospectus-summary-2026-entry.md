# Admissions Prospectus Summary — 2026 Entry

**Halvorsen Institute of Technology — Office of Admissions and Outreach**
**Published:** 1 October 2025 | **Classification:** Public

## 1. About the Institute

Halvorsen Institute of Technology is a private technical university with about 6,400 students and 540 staff. Teaching takes place at the Brackenridge Campus in Port Aldane and at regional campuses in Toronto, Lisbon and Pune. Students come from 38 countries. In the most recent graduate survey, 91% of graduates were in work or further study within six months of finishing.

## 3. Entry requirements

3.1 **Undergraduate programmes.** Applicants need a school-leaving qualification with an overall average of at least 70%, including mathematics. All undergraduate applicants sit the Halvorsen Aptitude Test (HAT), a two-hour online test of mathematics and reasoning. The minimum HAT score for admission is 60 out of 100.

3.2 **Master's programmes.** Applicants need a bachelor's degree in a related field with a grade point average of at least 3.0 on a four-point scale. The HAT is not required.

3.3 **Doctoral programme.** Applicants need a master's degree and a research proposal of no more than 2,000 words, and must have identified a supervisor who agrees to take them.

3.4 **English.** Teaching at every campus is in English. Applicants whose earlier education was not in English must provide a recognised test result.

An offer must be accepted within 21 days, with a deposit of $500 that is credited against the first tuition instalment.

## 5. Scholarships

- **Halvorsen Founders Scholarship** — covers 50% of tuition for every year of the programme. Awarded on merit to undergraduate applicants in the early and main rounds.
- **Brackenridge Access Bursary** — $4,000 per year for undergraduate students from lower-income households.
- **Regional Campus Excellence Award** — $2,500 in the first year for the strongest entrants at the Toronto, Lisbon and Pune campuses.

Applicants are considered for the Founders Scholarship automatically. The Access Bursary needs a separate form, due on 28 February 2026.

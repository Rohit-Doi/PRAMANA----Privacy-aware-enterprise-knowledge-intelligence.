# Staff Housing Regulations

Halvorsen Institute of Technology — Human Resources Division

Effective 1 April 2025. Internal.

## The accommodation

The Institute owns two groups of staff housing next to the Brackenridge Campus. Rowanfield Staff Residences has 64 apartments and Harrow Terrace has 22 houses. These regulations apply to both. They do not apply to the student halls.

## Who may apply

Housing is open to full-time employees who have completed at least 12 months of service. Hall wardens and staff who are required to be on call at night are given priority and need not have completed 12 months. Applications are considered by the Staff Housing Allocation Panel, which meets once every quarter and ranks applicants on a points list held by Human Resources.

## Rent and other costs

Rent is taken from salary each month. Employees in salary bands G1 to G4 pay a flat rent of $420 per month. Employees in band G5 and above pay 12% of their base monthly salary. A security deposit equal to one month's rent is collected with the first deduction and returned when the property is handed back in good order. Water is included in the rent. Electricity is metered and billed to the occupant every month.

## How long an occupant may stay

A tenancy lasts for a maximum of five years. The Director of Human Resources may extend it once, by up to two years, where no other eligible applicant is waiting. An occupant who leaves the Institute's employment must vacate within 60 days of the last day of service. An occupant who retires has 90 days.

## Living in staff housing

Quiet hours are from 23:00 to 06:00. Guests of an occupant may stay for up to 14 nights in any period of 90 days; a longer stay needs the written agreement of Human Resources. Sub-letting any part of the property is forbidden, and an occupant found to have sub-let will have the tenancy ended on 30 days' notice. Occupants may not make structural changes. Minor repairs are requested through the Estates help desk and are normally completed within five working days.

## Inspections

Each property is inspected once a year, with seven days' notice to the occupant.

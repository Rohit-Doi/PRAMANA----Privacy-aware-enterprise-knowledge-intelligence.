# Case File LC-2025-031 — Status Note

Halvorsen Institute of Technology — Office of the General Counsel

RESTRICTED. Legally privileged. Prepared 5 February 2026 by Ottilie Ramaswamy, General Counsel, for the President.

## Background

Dr. Leopold Ashgrove-Malik was an Associate Professor in the Department of Civil Engineering. In September 2025 the Finance Division noticed that several of his travel claims contained hotel invoices whose numbers did not match the hotels' own records. An internal review found eleven claims, submitted between March and September 2025, in which receipts had been altered or duplicated. The amount overpaid was $18,640.

## Disciplinary process

Dr. Ashgrove-Malik was suspended on full pay on 6 October 2025. A disciplinary hearing took place on 25 November 2025 before a panel of three chaired by the Director of Human Resources. He attended with a colleague as companion and said that the errors had been made by a research assistant who prepared his claims. The panel did not accept that explanation, because he had signed each claim personally. He was dismissed for gross misconduct with effect from 12 December 2025.

He appealed on 19 December 2025. The appeal was heard by the Provost and was rejected on 22 January 2026.

## The claim against the Institute

On 30 January 2026 his lawyers served a claim for wrongful dismissal. He seeks $310,000, made up of lost salary, loss of pension rights and damage to reputation. His main arguments are that the panel refused to hear the research assistant as a witness and that other staff with claim errors were only asked to repay.

## Our position

The Institute is represented by Brightwell Sato LLP. Their view is that the dismissal was fair in substance but that the refusal to hear the witness is a weakness. They estimate the chance of defending the claim in full at about 65%. Legal costs to trial are estimated at $140,000.

## Settlement

Mediation has been arranged for 14 May 2026. The President has authorised settlement of up to $75,000, inclusive of the other side's costs, on condition that the overpaid $18,640 is set off against any payment and that both sides keep the terms confidential. Any offer above that figure needs the approval of the Chair of the Board.

## Preservation of records

A legal hold was issued on 2 February 2026. Finance, Human Resources and the Department of Civil Engineering must keep all records relating to his claims, including e-mail, until the hold is lifted in writing by this office.

## Next step

Witness statements from the three panel members are due to Brightwell Sato LLP by 27 March 2026.

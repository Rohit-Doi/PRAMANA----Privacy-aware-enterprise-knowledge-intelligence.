# Faculty Promotion Evaluation Rubric — 2026 Cycle

**Halvorsen Institute of Technology — Office of the Provost**
**Classification:** CONFIDENTIAL — department review panels and members of the Faculty Promotions Committee
**Effective:** 1 June 2026

## 1. How the rubric is used

1.1 Each application for promotion is scored out of 100 by a department review panel of three senior colleagues. Applicants are not shown their scores or the panel's notes.

1.2 An application that reaches the threshold for the rank applied for is forwarded to the Faculty Promotions Committee, which makes the final recommendation to the President. An application below the threshold is returned with written feedback and goes no further in that cycle.

## 2. Criteria and weights

| Criterion | Maximum points | What the panel looks at |
|-----------|----------------|-------------------------|
| Research output | 35 | Peer-reviewed publications, patents, quality of venues |
| Teaching | 30 | Student evaluations, course design, supervision |
| Grant income | 15 | External funding won as principal investigator |
| Service | 10 | Committee work, administration, outreach |
| External referees | 10 | Letters from referees outside the Institute |
| **Total** | **100** | |

## 3. Thresholds and minimum requirements

| Promotion to | Minimum total score | Years in current rank | Peer-reviewed publications in last five years | Grant income as principal investigator | External referees |
|--------------|---------------------|-----------------------|-----------------------------------------------|----------------------------------------|-------------------|
| Senior Lecturer | 65 | 3 | 3 | None required | 2 |
| Associate Professor | 72 | 4 | 6 | $120,000 | 3 |
| Professor | 80 | 5 | 12 | $400,000 | 5 |

3.1 For every rank, the applicant's average student evaluation score over the last three years must be at least 3.8 on the five-point scale. An applicant below 3.8 cannot be forwarded whatever the total score.

3.2 Referees are chosen by the panel. The applicant may propose names, but no more than one proposed name may be used.

## 4. Scoring guidance

4.1 **Research output.** A panel should award 28 points or more only where at least half of the publications are in venues the department lists as first tier.

4.2 **Teaching.** Up to 5 of the 30 points are reserved for the supervision of doctoral students to completion.

4.3 **Grant income.** Income as co-investigator counts at half its value.

4.4 **Service.** Chairing an Institute-level committee for a full year earns at least 6 points.

4.5 Where two panel members differ by more than 10 points in total, the panel must meet and record the reason for its final score.

## 5. Timetable for the 2026 cycle

| Step | Date |
|------|------|
| Applications close | 15 September 2026 |
| Department panels complete scoring | 30 October 2026 |
| Faculty Promotions Committee meets | 24 November 2026 |
| Applicants informed of outcome | 15 December 2026 |

## 6. After the decision

6.1 A successful applicant moves to the minimum of the new salary band or receives a 6% increase in base salary, whichever is greater.

6.2 An unsuccessful applicant may apply again after one full cycle has passed.

6.3 Panel score sheets are kept by the Office of the Provost for five years.

# Scholarship Award Scoring Rubric — 2026 Entry

**Halvorsen Institute of Technology — Office of Admissions and Outreach**
**Classification:** CONFIDENTIAL — scorers and members of the Scholarships Committee | **Effective:** 1 December 2025

## 1. Purpose

This rubric tells scorers how to assess undergraduate applicants for the three scholarships offered for 2026 entry. It must not be shared with applicants, schools or agents.

## 2. Scoring

2.1 Every eligible applicant is given a score out of 100, built from five components.

| Component | Maximum points | Source |
|-----------|----------------|--------|
| Academic record | 40 | School results, converted by the admissions conversion table |
| Halvorsen Aptitude Test | 25 | HAT score divided by four |
| Personal statement | 15 | Scored by two readers |
| Interview | 15 | 20-minute video interview |
| Community contribution | 5 | Evidence in the application |
| **Total** | **100** | |

2.2 The personal statement and the interview are each scored independently by two scorers. Where the two totals for an applicant differ by more than 8 points, a third scorer assesses the applicant and the two closest totals are averaged.

2.3 A scorer who knows an applicant or the applicant's family must withdraw from that file.

## 3. Award rules

| Scholarship | Minimum score | Other condition | Awards available |
|-------------|---------------|-----------------|------------------|
| Halvorsen Founders Scholarship | 82 | None | 60 |
| Brackenridge Access Bursary | 65 | Household income below $48,000 per year | 140 |
| Regional Campus Excellence Award | None | Top 5% of the intake at each regional campus by score | As determined by intake size |

3.1 Where more applicants reach the minimum score than there are awards, awards go to the highest scores. Ties are broken by the academic record component, then by the HAT component.

3.2 An applicant may hold the Access Bursary together with either of the other two awards. The Founders Scholarship and the Regional Campus Excellence Award cannot be held together.

3.3 Interviews are held only for applicants whose score on the other four components is 68 or more.

## 4. Keeping an award

4.1 A Founders Scholar must maintain a cumulative grade point average of 3.2 or higher. A bursary holder must maintain 2.7 or higher.

4.2 A holder who falls below the requirement is given one semester to recover before the award is withdrawn.

4.3 Household income for bursary holders is re-checked every year.

## 5. Timetable

5.1 Scoring of early-round applicants is completed by 9 January 2026 and of main-round applicants by 3 April 2026.

5.2 The Scholarships Committee meets on 14 April 2026 to confirm the awards.

5.3 Award letters are sent by 30 April 2026. An award lapses if it is not accepted within 21 days.

## 6. Records

Score sheets are kept for two years after the end of the admissions cycle and are then destroyed.

# Examination Regulations 2024

**Halvorsen Institute of Technology — Office of the Controller of Examinations**
**Document reference:** ER-2024 | **Effective:** 1 August 2024 | **Classification:** Internal

## 1. Scope

1.1 These regulations govern all taught undergraduate and postgraduate examinations at the Brackenridge Campus and at the Toronto, Lisbon and Pune campuses.

1.2 They do not apply to the examination of doctoral theses, which is covered by the Research Degrees Code.

## 2. Eligibility to sit an examination

2.1 A student must have attended at least 75% of the scheduled teaching sessions of a course in order to sit its final examination.

2.2 A student whose fees are in arrears on the first day of the examination period may sit the examination, but the result will be withheld until the account is cleared.

## 3. Assessment structure

3.1 Unless a course outline approved by the Dean of Academic Affairs states otherwise, continuous assessment carries 40% of the course mark and the final examination carries 60%.

3.2 The overall pass mark for a course is 40%. In addition, a student must obtain at least 35% in the final examination component.

## 4. Grade boundaries

| Grade | Mark range (%) | Grade points |
|-------|----------------|--------------|
| A | 80–100 | 4.0 |
| B | 65–79 | 3.0 |
| C | 50–64 | 2.0 |
| D | 40–49 | 1.0 |
| F | 0–39 | 0.0 |

## 5. Conduct of examinations

5.1 Candidates must be seated ten minutes before the published start time. A candidate who arrives late is admitted up to 30 minutes after the start; no extra time is given.

5.2 Mobile phones, smart watches and any device capable of storing text are not permitted at the desk. A candidate found with such a device will have the paper cancelled and the matter will be referred to the Controller of Examinations.

5.3 Candidates with an approved accommodation receive 25% additional time. Applications must reach the Disability Support Office at least four weeks before the first day of the examination period.

## 6. Publication of results

6.1 Results are published on the student portal within 21 working days of the last day of the examination period.

## 7. Resit examinations

7.1 A student who fails a course may resit the final examination. A course may be attempted up to three times in total, including the first attempt.

7.2 The resit fee is $60 per paper.

7.3 The grade awarded after a successful resit is capped at D.

## 8. Re-evaluation of scripts

8.1 A student may apply for re-evaluation of a final examination script within 14 calendar days of the publication of results.

8.2 The re-evaluation fee is $35 per paper. The fee is refunded if the mark changes by five marks or more in either direction.

8.3 Re-evaluation is carried out by an examiner who did not mark the original script.

## 9. Appeals

9.1 A student who remains dissatisfied after re-evaluation may appeal to the Examination Appeals Board within 10 working days of receiving the re-evaluation outcome.

9.2 The Board will issue a written decision within 30 calendar days of receiving a complete appeal. The decision of the Board is final.

## 10. Custody of scripts

10.1 Marked scripts are retained for 12 months after the publication of results and are then destroyed under the supervision of the Controller of Examinations.

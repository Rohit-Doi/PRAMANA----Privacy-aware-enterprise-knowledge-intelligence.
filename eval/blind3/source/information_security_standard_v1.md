# Information Security Standard — Version 1

**Marrowgate Quill Mutual**
**Reference:** SEC-STD-04
**Version:** 1
**Effective from:** 2 September 2024
**Owner:** Head of Information Security

## 1. Purpose and scope

This standard sets the minimum security controls for every system, device and person that handles the Society's information. It applies to all staff, directors, contractors and temporary workers in all areas.

## 2. Information classification

All documents and records carry one of four labels.

| Label | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| PUBLIC | May be given to anyone | Schedule of fees, branch directory |
| INTERNAL | For staff only | Staff policies, procedures |
| CONFIDENTIAL | Limited to those with a business need | Audit reports, budgets, tender evaluations |
| RESTRICTED | Named individuals only | Closed-session minutes, payroll data |

Unlabelled documents are treated as INTERNAL.

## 3. Access controls

| Control | Requirement under this version |
|---|---|
| Minimum password length | 12 characters |
| Password change, standard accounts | Every 95 days |
| Password change, privileged accounts | Every 45 days |
| Account lockout | After 6 failed attempts |
| Lockout duration | 20 minutes |
| Multi-factor authentication | Required for remote access only |
| Screen lock after inactivity | 15 minutes |
| Review of access rights | Every 6 months |

## 4. Devices and media

1. All laptops must use full-disk encryption.
2. Removable media (USB sticks, external drives) may be used only with the written approval of the Chief Information Officer.
3. Lost or stolen devices must be reported within 4 hours.
4. Personal devices may connect only to the guest network.

## 5. Patching and logging

| Item | Requirement under this version |
|---|---|
| Critical security patches | Applied within 14 days of release |
| High-severity patches | Applied within 30 days of release |
| Retention of security logs | 13 months |
| Vulnerability scan frequency | Every 8 weeks |
| External penetration test | Once every 18 months |

## 6. Incident reporting

1. Any suspected security incident must be reported to the Head of Information Security within 4 hours of discovery, using the address soc@marrowgatequill.example or the duty phone.
2. Incidents involving member data are also notified to the Data Protection Lead.
3. A post-incident review is completed within 15 working days.

## 7. Training

All staff complete security awareness training once a year. The pass mark is 80%. Staff who fail twice are referred to their line manager.

## 8. Exceptions

The Head of Information Security may grant a temporary exception to this standard for up to 60 days. Longer exceptions require the approval of the Risk Committee.

## 9. Review

This standard is reviewed every two years by the Head of Information Security and approved by the Risk Committee.

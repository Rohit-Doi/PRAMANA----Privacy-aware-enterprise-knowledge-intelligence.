# The Quill — Members' Newsletter, Summer 2026

**Marrowgate Quill Mutual**
**Published:** 15 June 2026

## 1. A word from your Society

Thank you to everyone who came to this year's Annual General Meeting at the Tanners' Exchange Hall, and to the many more who voted by post or online. Your Society now has 61,480 members, 2,935 more than a year ago. This issue brings you the AGM results, news from the branches and a reminder of what your membership is worth.

## 2. AGM results

Members cast 4,217 valid votes. All resolutions were passed.

| Resolution | Votes for | Votes against |
|---|---|---|
| To receive the annual report and accounts | 4,102 | 115 |
| To approve the directors' remuneration report | 3,764 | 453 |
| To re-appoint Pellow Garrick & Co as auditors | 4,031 | 186 |
| To re-elect two directors retiring by rotation | 3,958 | 259 |
| To raise the limit on charitable giving | 3,871 | 346 |

## 3. Your share dividend

The Board declared a dividend of 1.15% on Everyday Share Account balances for the 2025 financial year, up from 0.95% the year before. It was paid into accounts on 5 May 2026.

## 4. A new branch at Kirra Point

Our ninth branch opened at 140 Headland Parade, Kirra Point, on 9 March 2026. In its first 12 weeks the branch welcomed 1,164 new members. The branch has a community room that local groups can book free of charge.

## 5. Community Fund

In the year to 31 March 2026 the Community Fund made 37 grants worth $86,950 in total. Grants went to, among others:

- Saltmere Tidal Rowing Club, for two training boats;
- Oudeveld Repair Café, for tools and a van hire fund;
- Bandar Teluk Reading Barge, for 900 children's books;
- Thornby Cross Allotment Society, for a rainwater system.

Applications for the next round close on 31 August 2026. Any member may nominate a group. The largest single grant we make is $6,500.

## 6. Branch improvements

We are refurbishing six branches over the next two years, starting with Thornby Cross. While a branch is closed, the Quill Van will park outside so that you can still pay in, withdraw and talk to us.

## 7. Fees and rates reminder

Our 2026 Schedule of Fees and Charges came into force in February. Some fees went up and some came down; the unpaid direct debit fee is now lower. Copies are in every branch.

## 8. Member Council

The Member Council is your voice between AGMs. It has 18 seats, six for each area. Elections for nine of the seats take place this autumn; nominations close on 14 August 2026. The Council's autumn meeting is open to all members as observers. To stand, write to the Chair of the Member Council care of head office.

## 9. Stay safe from scams

We will never ask you to move money to a "safe account" and we will never ask for your full password. If a caller pressures you, hang up and call 0800 555 0164.

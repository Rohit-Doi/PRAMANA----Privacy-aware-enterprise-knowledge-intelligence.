# Pay Scale and Allowance Bands 2026

**Marrowgate Quill Mutual**
**Reference:** HR-PAY-2026
**Effective from:** 1 April 2026
**Owner:** Head of People
**Classification:** CONFIDENTIAL — people team, line managers and payroll only

## 1. Purpose

This document gives the money value of each pay grade and each allowance band used in contracts, in the three area staff terms supplements and in payroll. Grades and bands are the same across all areas; which band applies to a member of staff depends on the area supplement.

## 2. Pay grades

All figures are annual base salary for a full-time member of staff. The 2026 scale includes the general pay award of 3.15% applied on 1 April 2026. (The award applied on 1 April 2025 was 2.65%.)

| Grade | Typical roles | Minimum | Midpoint | Maximum |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Q1 | Member Service Assistant | $31,460 | $34,120 | $36,780 |
| Q2 | Senior Member Service Adviser | $37,215 | $40,890 | $44,565 |
| Q3 | Lending Officer, Analyst, IT Engineer | $45,330 | $50,470 | $55,610 |
| Q4 | Team Leader, Senior Analyst, Deputy Branch Manager | $56,840 | $63,250 | $69,660 |
| Q5 | Branch Manager, Specialist Manager | $71,120 | $79,480 | $87,840 |
| Q6 | Head of function | $96,350 | $108,900 | $121,450 |
| Q7 | Area Director | $127,600 | $141,300 | $155,000 |

Executive offices (Chief Executive and the chief officers) are outside the grade structure; their pay is set by the Remuneration Committee.

## 3. Converting annual salary

1. The **daily rate** is the annual base salary divided by 260.
2. The **monthly salary** is the annual base salary divided by 12.
3. The **hourly rate** is the daily rate divided by one fifth of the standard weekly hours in the staff member's area supplement.

## 4. Progression

1. New staff normally start at the minimum of the grade.
2. Staff move to the midpoint after 30 months of satisfactory service in the grade.
3. Movement above the midpoint depends on the annual review and needs the approval of the Head of People.
4. A starting salary above the midpoint needs the approval of the Chief Financial Officer.

## 5. On-call allowance bands

On-call bands are named in each area supplement. The rate is paid for each night a member of staff is on the call rota, whether or not they are called.

| Band | Rate per weeknight | Rate per weekend night or public holiday |
|---|---|---|
| Band A | $46.50 | $69.75 |
| Band B | $71.25 | $104.60 |
| Band C | $118.40 | $162.90 |
| Band D (major incident standby) | $145.00 | $201.50 |

Band D is not tied to any area. It is paid only when the Chief Information Officer declares a major incident standby.

## 7. Overtime

Overtime for grades Q1 to Q4 is paid at 1.4 times the hourly rate on weekdays. Saturday rates are set in the area supplements. Grades Q5 and above do not receive overtime pay and instead take time off in lieu.

## 8. Review

The pay scale is reviewed each year. The Head of People proposes the general award, the Chief Financial Officer confirms it can be afforded, and the Remuneration Committee notes it at its first meeting of the year.

Savings Account Terms and Conditions

Marrowgate Quill Mutual — in force from 1 February 2026

1. About these terms

These terms apply to every savings account you hold with Marrowgate Quill Mutual ("the Society", "we"). By opening an account you become a member of the Society and agree to its Rules. These terms should be read with our current Schedule of Fees and Charges, which sets the amount of every fee and charge mentioned here.

3. Interest

We work out interest each day on the cleared balance and add it to your account once a year on 5 April. On fixed-term accounts interest is added on the day the term ends. We pay interest without deducting tax; you are responsible for any tax you owe.

4. Limits

The most any one member may hold with us across all savings accounts is $185,000. Joint accounts count half towards each holder's limit. We may refuse a deposit that would take you above the limit.

5. Withdrawals and closing your account

You may withdraw up to $750 in cash a day at a branch without notice. Larger cash withdrawals need 2 working days' notice. If you make a fifth withdrawal from a Tarnside Saver in one year, the account earns the Everyday Share Account rate for the rest of that year.

If you close a fixed-term account before the end of the term, we make the early closure charge set out in the current Schedule of Fees and Charges. If you give less than the full notice on a Quill Notice 45 account, you lose 45 days' interest on the amount withdrawn.

6. Changing your mind

You may cancel a new account within 16 days of opening it. We will return your money with any interest earned and make no charge.

7. Changes to rates and terms

If we lower a variable rate we will tell you at least 21 days before the change. If we raise a rate we may do so at once. We will give at least 60 days' notice of any other change that is to your disadvantage.

8. Protection of your savings

Your eligible deposits are protected up to $112,000 per member by the Mutual Depositors' Guarantee Fund. The Society is supervised by the Cooperative Prudential Board.

9. If something goes wrong

If you are unhappy with any part of our service, please see our leaflet "How We Handle Complaints" or ask at any branch.

10. Governing rules

These terms are subject to the Rules of the Society. If these terms and the Rules disagree, the Rules prevail.

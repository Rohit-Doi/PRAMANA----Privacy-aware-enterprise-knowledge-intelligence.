# Anti-Money-Laundering and Member Due Diligence Procedure

**Marrowgate Quill Mutual**
**Reference:** COM-AML-03
**Effective from:** 19 January 2026
**Owner:** Head of Compliance
**Classification:** CONFIDENTIAL — thresholds in this document must not be disclosed to members

## 1. Purpose

This procedure tells staff how to identify members, how to monitor transactions and how to report suspicion. It applies to all branches, the central lending team and the contact centre.

## 2. Roles

1. The Money Laundering Reporting Officer (MLRO) receives all internal suspicion reports and decides whether to report to the authorities. The office of MLRO is held ex officio by the Head of Compliance.
2. Branch Managers make sure their staff follow this procedure and complete the training.
3. The Chief Risk Officer approves the opening or continuation of any relationship with a politically exposed person.

## 3. Identifying members

Before an account is opened, staff must verify the member's identity using one photographic document and one proof of address dated within the last 4 months. Where a member cannot provide standard documents, the Branch Manager may accept alternatives listed in Annex B, and must record why.

## 4. Risk tiers and review frequency

Every member is given a risk tier when they join. The tier sets how often their due diligence is refreshed.

| Risk tier | Description | Review of due diligence |
|---|---|---|
| R1 | Standard member, local, salary or pension income | Every 48 months |
| R2 | Self-employed or cash-based income | Every 30 months |
| R3 | Complex ownership, overseas links or unusual activity | Every 14 months |
| R4 | Politically exposed person or close associate | Every 5 months |
| R5 | Relationship under MLRO restriction | Continuous; no transactions without MLRO consent |

## 5. Transaction thresholds

| Trigger | Threshold | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Single cash deposit | Over $7,400 | Enhanced review by branch |
| Cash deposits in any rolling 7 days | Over $11,900 in total | Enhanced review by branch |
| Source-of-funds evidence | Any single credit over $23,500 | Documentary evidence before funds are released |
| International wire to a higher-risk country | Any amount | Referral to compliance team before sending |
| Third-party cash deposit | Over $1,150 | Identify the depositor |
| Early repayment of a loan in cash | Over $4,800 | Enhanced review by branch |

Staff must not tell a member that a threshold has been triggered.

## 6. Reporting suspicion

1. Any member of staff who knows or suspects that a transaction involves the proceeds of crime must send an internal suspicion report to the MLRO within 1 working day, using form AML-7 in the Halyard system.
2. The MLRO decides within 4 working days whether to make an external report.
3. While a report is being considered, the member must not be told ("tipping off" is a criminal offence).
4. External reports must be filed with the authorities within 9 working days of the internal report being received.

## 7. Records

Identity records and transaction records are kept for 76 months after the relationship ends. Internal suspicion reports are kept for the same period whether or not an external report was made.

## 8. Training

All staff complete anti-money-laundering training within 20 working days of joining and once a year after that. The pass mark is 82%. This is a separate course from the security awareness training.

## 9. Reporting to the Board

The MLRO presents an annual report to the Risk Committee at its last meeting of each year. The report covers the number of internal and external reports, training completion and any breaches of the time limits in section 6.

## 10. Breaches

A failure to follow this procedure may be treated as a disciplinary matter. Late reporting must itself be reported to the Chief Risk Officer within 2 working days of discovery.

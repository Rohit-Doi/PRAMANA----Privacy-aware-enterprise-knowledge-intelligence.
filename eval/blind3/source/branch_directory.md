## 4. Accessibility

All branches have step-free entry except Thornby Cross, where a ramp is available on request until the branch is refurbished. Hearing loops are fitted at every counter. Large-print and audio versions of our leaflets are free.

## 5. The Quill Van

Our mobile branch, the Quill Van, visits villages without a branch. It calls at Upper Pellow on the first Tuesday of the month, Fenwardine on the second Thursday and Selat Kecil on the third Wednesday. It also provides cover when a branch is closed for building work.

## 6. Who to speak to

Each branch is led by a Branch Manager, who can help with anything our counter staff cannot. If you want to speak to the manager, ask at the counter or call the branch number above. Each area also has an Area Director, who can be reached through head office on 0800 555 0164.

## 7. Cash machines

Every branch has a cash machine available 24 hours a day, free to members. The daily machine withdrawal limit is $620.

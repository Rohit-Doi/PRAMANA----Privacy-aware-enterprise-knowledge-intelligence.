# Schedule of Fees and Charges 2026

**Marrowgate Quill Mutual**
**Applies from:** 2 February 2026
**Replaces:** Schedule of Fees and Charges 2025

This schedule lists the fees we charge members. It applies at every branch, by phone and through QuillOnline. The 2025 schedule no longer applies from the date above.

## 1. Everyday accounts

| Fee | Amount |
|---|---|
| Monthly account maintenance | $3.65 |
| Paper statement (each) | $2.40 |
| Cash withdrawal at another provider's machine | $2.15 |
| Unpaid direct debit | $9.75 |
| Replacement card | $7.50 |
| Bank cheque | $8.25 |

The monthly maintenance fee is not charged in any month in which the balance stays at or above $1,400. Members aged under 21 or over 70 do not pay the maintenance fee.

## 2. Payments

| Fee | Amount |
|---|---|
| Outbound domestic wire | $18.40 |
| Outbound international wire | $36.75 |
| Inbound wire | Free |
| Stopped cheque | $5.40 |
| Same-day priority payment | $11.20 |

## 3. Savings and loans

| Fee | Amount |
|---|---|
| Early closure of a fixed-term account | 80 days' interest |
| Dormant account fee (per quarter) | $4.10 |
| Mortgage application fee | $315 |
| Mortgage valuation (standard property) | $265 |
| Arranged overdraft interest | 12.9% a year |
| Unarranged overdraft interest | 19.85% a year |

An account is dormant after 30 months without a transaction made by the member. We write to the member twice before any dormant fee is charged.

## 4. Membership

A one-off membership share of $7 is payable on joining. It is returned when the member leaves.

## 5. Waivers and refunds

1. A Branch Manager may waive fees of up to $40 for a member in any calendar year.
2. A larger waiver needs the agreement of the Head of Member Services.
3. If we charge a fee in error we refund it within 4 working days and add interest at the arranged overdraft rate.

## 6. What changed from 2025

- Maintenance, statement, cash machine, card and wire fees have risen.
- The unpaid direct debit fee has fallen from $11.50 to $9.75.
- Both overdraft interest rates have fallen.
- The early closure charge on fixed-term accounts has fallen from 95 to 80 days' interest.
- The balance needed to avoid the maintenance fee has risen from $1,250 to $1,400.
- The bank cheque fee, dormant account fee and membership share are unchanged.

## 7. Changes

We give at least 60 days' notice before raising any fee. Members were notified of this schedule by letter in November 2025.

## 8. Questions

Ask at any branch, call 0800 555 0164, or write to Member Services, 41 Cooperage Lane, Marrowgate. If you are unhappy with a charge, our Complaints Procedure explains what to do.

# Member Lending Policy — Version 3

**Marrowgate Quill Mutual**
**Policy reference:** LEN-POL-01
**Version:** 3
**Effective from:** 1 March 2025
**Owner:** Head of Lending

## 1. Scope

This policy governs all lending by the Society to its members: residential mortgages, secured personal loans, unsecured personal loans and arranged overdrafts. It applies in all three areas and to every branch and to the central lending team.

## 2. Principles

1. The Society lends only to members and only for purposes the member can afford.
2. Every loan is assessed on the member's ability to repay, not on the value of security alone.
3. No member of staff may approve a loan to themselves, a relative or a close associate.

## 3. Eligibility

1. An applicant must have been a member for at least 9 months before applying for unsecured lending. There is no minimum membership period for a residential mortgage.
2. Applicants must be aged 18 or over.
3. The mortgage must be repaid before the borrower's 74th birthday.

## 4. Affordability and security limits

| Measure | Limit under this version |
|---|---|
| Maximum loan-to-value (residential mortgage) | 87.5% |
| Loan-to-value above which mortgage indemnity cover is required | 80% |
| Maximum debt-to-income multiple | 4.25 times gross annual income |
| Interest rate stress buffer applied in affordability test | 2.75 percentage points above the product rate |
| Maximum mortgage term | 32 years |
| Maximum unsecured loan term | 6 years |
| Maximum aggregate exposure to a single member | $1,150,000 |

## 5. Delegated lending authorities

Loans are approved at the lowest tier whose limits cover the member's total borrowing after the new loan.

| Tier | Holder of authority | Unsecured limit | Secured limit |
|---|---|---|---|
| L1 | Lending Officer | $8,500 | $95,000 |
| L2 | Senior Lending Officer or Branch Manager | $17,500 | $240,000 |
| L3 | Head of Lending | $42,000 | $610,000 |
| L4 | Credit Committee | Not applicable | $1,450,000 |
| L5 | Board | Above L4 | Above L4 |

A loan that takes a member's aggregate exposure above the single-member cap in section 4 may be approved only at tier L5.

## 6. Policy exceptions

1. An exception is any loan that does not meet a limit in section 4 other than the single-member cap.
2. Every exception requires the written approval of the Head of Lending in addition to the normal tier approval.
3. Exceptions must not exceed 6.5% of the value of new lending in any calendar quarter.
4. The Head of Lending reports all exceptions to the Risk Committee at each of its meetings.

## 7. Declined applications

A member whose application is declined may ask for a review. The review is carried out within 9 working days by the Branch Manager of a different branch in the same area.

## 8. Arrears

1. A member is contacted within 4 working days of a missed payment.
2. Accounts more than 60 days in arrears are referred to the Head of Member Services for a forbearance review.
3. Legal recovery may begin only with the approval of the General Counsel.

## 9. Review of this policy

The Credit Committee reviews this policy every year and recommends changes to the Board. The Board considers the reviewed policy at its second ordinary meeting of each year.

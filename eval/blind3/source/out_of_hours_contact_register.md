## 1. Purpose

This register lists the staff who may be called outside working hours when a branch alarm sounds, a system fails or a branch cannot open. It supports the business continuity plan. It does not list holders of named offices, whose details are in the executive call tree kept by the Society Secretary.

## 2. How to use the register

1. The alarm company calls the first keyholder for the branch. If there is no answer within 10 minutes it calls the second keyholder.
2. For a system failure, the service desk calls the on-call IT engineer shown on the weekly rota, then the Security Operations Analyst if the failure may be a security incident.
3. A keyholder who attends a branch out of hours must never enter alone; they wait for the security patrol, which should arrive within 35 minutes.
4. Every call-out is logged the next working day on form BCP-3.

## 4. Allowances

Staff listed as first or second keyholder receive the keyholder allowance. Staff on the on-call IT rota receive the on-call allowance at the band named in the staff terms supplement for their area; head office is in the Pellcross Basin area for this purpose. The amounts are set in the Pay Scale and Allowance Bands document.

## 6. Keeping the register up to date

Staff must tell the people team within 5 working days if their mobile number changes. The register is reissued on the first Monday of every second month. Old copies must be destroyed. Printed copies are kept only in the sealed continuity pack at each branch.

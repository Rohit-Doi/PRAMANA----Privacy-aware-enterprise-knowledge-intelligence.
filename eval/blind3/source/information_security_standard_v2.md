# Information Security Standard — Version 2

**Marrowgate Quill Mutual**
**Reference:** SEC-STD-04
**Version:** 2 (replaces Version 1)
**Effective from:** 6 April 2026
**Owner:** Head of Information Security
**Approved:** by the Risk Committee at its first meeting of 2026

## 1. Purpose and scope

This standard sets the minimum security controls for every system, device and person that handles the Society's information. It applies to all staff, directors, contractors and temporary workers in all areas. Version 1 is withdrawn from the effective date.

## 2. Information classification

All documents and records carry one of four labels.

| Label | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| PUBLIC | May be given to anyone | Schedule of fees, branch directory |
| INTERNAL | For staff only | Staff policies, procedures |
| CONFIDENTIAL | Limited to those with a business need | Audit reports, budgets, tender evaluations |
| RESTRICTED | Named individuals only | Closed-session minutes, payroll data |

Unlabelled documents are treated as CONFIDENTIAL until labelled. This is a change from Version 1.

## 3. Access controls

| Control | Requirement under this version |
|---|---|
| Minimum password length | 15 characters |
| Password change, standard accounts | Every 365 days, or at once on suspected compromise |
| Password change, privileged accounts | Every 60 days |
| Account lockout | After 5 failed attempts |
| Lockout duration | 35 minutes |
| Multi-factor authentication | Required for all access, on site and remote |
| Screen lock after inactivity | 9 minutes |
| Review of access rights | Every 4 months |

## 4. Devices and media

1. All laptops and all mobile phones issued by the Society must use full-disk encryption.
2. Removable media (USB sticks, external drives) may be used only with the written approval of the Chief Information Officer. Each approval lasts no more than 10 working days.
3. Lost or stolen devices must be reported within 90 minutes.
4. Personal devices may connect only to the guest network.

## 5. Patching and logging

| Item | Requirement under this version |
|---|---|
| Critical security patches | Applied within 6 days of release |
| High-severity patches | Applied within 21 days of release |
| Retention of security logs | 19 months |
| Vulnerability scan frequency | Every 3 weeks |
| External penetration test | Once every 12 months |

## 6. Incident reporting

1. Any suspected security incident must be reported to the Head of Information Security within 90 minutes of discovery, using the address soc@marrowgatequill.example or the duty phone.
2. Incidents involving member data are also notified to the Data Protection Lead.
3. A post-incident review is completed within 11 working days.
4. Incidents rated "severe" are reported to the Chair of the Risk Committee within one working day.

## 7. Training

All staff complete security awareness training once a year. The pass mark is 85%. Staff who fail twice are referred to their line manager, and their remote access is suspended until they pass.

## 8. Exceptions

The Head of Information Security may grant a temporary exception to this standard for up to 45 days. Longer exceptions require the approval of the Risk Committee.

## 9. Use of AI tools

Staff may use only AI tools listed on the approved software register. RESTRICTED information must never be entered into any AI tool. CONFIDENTIAL information may be entered only into tools hosted inside the Society's own environment.

## 10. Review

This standard is reviewed every year by the Head of Information Security and approved by the Risk Committee.

## 11. Summary of changes from Version 1

- Password length raised from 12 to 15 characters; routine rotation of standard passwords relaxed.
- Multi-factor authentication extended to all access.
- Reporting time for incidents and lost devices cut from 4 hours to 90 minutes.
- Patch windows, log retention, scanning and testing frequencies tightened.
- Training pass mark raised; exception period shortened.
- New section on AI tools.

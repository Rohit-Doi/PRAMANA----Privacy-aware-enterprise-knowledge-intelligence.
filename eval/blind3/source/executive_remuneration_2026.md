# Executive and Director Remuneration 2026

**Marrowgate Quill Mutual — Remuneration Committee paper**
**RESTRICTED — Remuneration Committee members and the Society Secretary only**
**Paper reference:** RC-2026-01
**Decided:** at the Remuneration Committee's first meeting of 2026

## 1. Purpose

This paper records the remuneration the Committee approved for holders of executive offices and for directors for the year from 1 April 2026. Offices are referred to by title. Individual figures in this paper are not published; the annual report shows them only in bands, except for the Chief Executive.

## 2. Executive base salaries

| Office | Base salary from 1 April 2026 | Base salary from 1 April 2025 | Maximum variable pay |
|---|---|---|---|
| Chief Executive | $287,400 | $276,900 | 17.5% of base salary |
| Chief Financial Officer | $214,650 | $207,300 | 12.5% of base salary |
| Chief Risk Officer | $198,300 | $192,150 | 12.5% of base salary |
| Chief Information Officer | $191,750 | $184,800 | 12.5% of base salary |
| General Counsel | $176,900 | $171,500 | 12.5% of base salary |
| Head of Lending | $148,200 | $143,650 | 10% of base salary |
| Head of People | $139,850 | $135,580 | 10% of base salary |

The Head of Lending and the Head of People sit above the maximum of grade Q6 by individual decision of this Committee and are therefore reported here.

## 3. Variable pay

1. Variable pay is earned against four measures: member satisfaction (30%), capital strength (30%), cost-to-income ratio (20%) and conduct and risk (20%).
2. No variable pay is paid to anyone if the Society's capital ratio falls below the Board's own minimum.
3. Half of any variable pay to the Chief Executive is deferred for 24 months and can be reduced or cancelled.
4. For the year to 31 March 2026 the Committee awarded variable pay at 61% of the maximum for every executive office.

## 4. Pensions and benefits

| Item | Chief Executive | Other executive offices |
|---|---|---|
| Employer pension contribution | 11.5% of base salary | 10.25% of base salary |
| Car allowance | $9,400 a year | $6,150 a year |
| Private medical cover | Family | Individual |
| Notice period from the Society | 12 months | 6 months |
| Life cover | 4 times base salary | 4 times base salary |

## 5. Pay ratio

The median base salary of all staff at 1 April 2026 is $43,950. The Chief Executive's base salary is therefore 6.54 times the median. The Committee's guideline is that the ratio should not exceed 7.25.

## 6. Directors' fees

| Role | Annual fee from 1 April 2026 |
|---|---|
| Chair of the Board | $58,300 |
| Deputy Chair and Senior Independent Director | $39,200 |
| Non-executive director (base fee) | $31,750 |
| Supplement for chairing the Audit Committee or the Risk Committee | $6,400 |
| Supplement for chairing the Remuneration Committee or the Credit Committee | $4,850 |
| Member Council Chair (honorarium) | $3,600 |

The supplement is added to the base fee. Directors receive no variable pay and no pension. Fees were last raised on 1 April 2024.

## 7. Matters noted

1. The Committee noted the general staff pay award proposed by the Head of People.
2. The Committee agreed a retention arrangement for six named engineers on the core banking programme: $8,250 each, payable on go-live.
3. The Committee will review executive salaries against the mutual-sector benchmark at its last meeting of 2026.

# Core Banking Replacement — Tender Evaluation Report

**Marrowgate Quill Mutual**
**Reference:** IT-TEN-2026-02
**Date:** 9 July 2026
**Classification:** CONFIDENTIAL — commercially sensitive
**Prepared by:** Evaluation Panel, chaired by the Chief Information Officer

## 1. Background

The Halyard core banking system has been in service since 2009 and its supplier will end support on 30 June 2028. The Society invited tenders for a replacement system on 2 February 2026 under tender reference IT-TEN-2026-02. Seven suppliers asked for the documents and five submitted compliant bids by the closing date of 17 April 2026.

## 2. Evaluation panel

The panel was chaired by the Chief Information Officer. Its other members were the Chief Financial Officer, the Head of Information Security, the Head of Lending and the Head of Facilities and Procurement, who acted as procurement lead. The Head of Internal Audit attended as an observer and did not score.

## 3. Criteria and weights

| Criterion | Weight |
|---|---|
| Functional fit | 35% |
| Whole-life cost | 25% |
| Security and resilience | 20% |
| Implementation approach | 15% |
| Social value and mutual-sector experience | 5% |

Each criterion was scored out of 100 and multiplied by its weight.

## 4. Bids and scores

| Bidder | Functional | Cost | Security | Implementation | Social value | Weighted total | Whole-life cost (7 years) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Thrumwick Software | 86 | 72 | 88 | 78 | 70 | 80.90 | $4,284,000 |
| Corvane Systems | 78 | 84 | 80 | 74 | 60 | 78.40 | $3,912,300 |
| Ledgerloom | 74 | 66 | 82 | 70 | 80 | 73.30 | $4,498,500 |
| Ambrel Holt Partners | 81 | 52 | 85 | 60 | 90 | 71.85 | $5,127,000 |
| Northpike Digital | 69 | 90 | 61 | 66 | 50 | 71.25 | $3,655,400 |

## 5. Commentary

1. **Thrumwick Software** scored highest on functional fit and security. Its lending module supports the tiered authorities in the Lending Policy without customisation. Its proposed migration takes 14 months.
2. **Corvane Systems** was the best-priced of the credible bids and came second overall, 2.50 points behind. The panel had concerns about its reliance on a subcontractor for data migration.
3. **Ledgerloom** offered strong security but a weaker implementation plan, with a 19-month migration.
4. **Ambrel Holt Partners** had the best social value response but its whole-life cost is above the programme ceiling set in the 2026 Budget, and it could not be recommended at any score.
5. **Northpike Digital** was the cheapest bid. Its security score of 61 was below the panel's minimum acceptable score of 65 for that criterion, so it was excluded from the final ranking.

## 6. Recommendation

The panel recommends that the contract be awarded to **Thrumwick Software** for a term of 7 years at a whole-life cost of $4,284,000. The cost is spread evenly across the term. The contract includes a break option at the end of year 4 on 9 months' notice.

## 7. Approval route

Under the Society's financial regulations a contract with a whole-life value above $2,750,000 can be awarded only by the Board. The panel's recommendation will be put to the Board at its fourth ordinary meeting of 2026. Until the Board decides, bidders must not be told the outcome. A standstill period of 12 days follows the Board's decision before the contract is signed.

## 8. Risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Migration overruns the end of Halyard support | 10 months of slack between planned go-live and end of support |
| Key staff leave during the programme | Retention payments for six named engineers |
| Data quality in old loan records | Cleansing project begins in September 2026 |
| Supplier failure | Source code held in escrow; parent company guarantee |
| Member disruption at cut-over | Cut-over on a public holiday weekend; Quill Van on standby |

## 9. Next steps

If the Board approves, the contract will be signed after the standstill period and the programme will start on 1 September 2026. Unsuccessful bidders will be offered a debrief within 15 working days of the decision.

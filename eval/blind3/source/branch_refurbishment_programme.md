# Branch Refurbishment Programme 2026–27

**Board paper — CONFIDENTIAL**
**Marrowgate Quill Mutual**
**Date:** 14 May 2026
**Sponsor:** Head of Facilities and Procurement

## Decision requested

- Approve the start of the Branch Refurbishment Programme
- Approve the appointment of Pellow & Strake Fit-Out as main contractor
- Decision to be taken at the Board's third ordinary meeting of 2026

## Why now

- Six of our nine branches were last refitted before 2011
- Thornby Cross has no step-free entry
- Heating systems at four branches are beyond their design life
- Member survey: 38% rated branch comfort "poor" or "fair"

## Scope

- Six branches refurbished over two years
- Total programme ceiling: $1,386,000
- Ceiling includes a contingency of $108,500
- Kirra Point, Garnet Quay and Bandar Teluk are excluded (recently fitted out)

## Sequence of works

- 1. Thornby Cross — start 8 June 2026, 9 weeks
- 2. Hallam Sluice — start 7 September 2026, 7 weeks
- 3. Lantern Wharf — start 11 January 2027, 10 weeks
- 4. Saltmere — start 5 April 2027, 6 weeks
- 5. Oudeveld — start 14 June 2027, 8 weeks
- 6. Nettlebridge — start 13 September 2027, 7 weeks

## What each branch gets

- Step-free entry and automatic doors
- Two private interview rooms
- Heat pump replacing gas boiler
- LED lighting and new insulation
- Community noticeboard and seating area

## Engineering standards

- Target energy reduction per branch: 31%
- Counter height: 760 mm, with one lowered position
- Fire compartment rating for the strong room: 120 minutes
- Minimum corridor width: 1,250 mm
- All works signed off by an independent building inspector

## Contractor selection

- Four quotations received
- Pellow & Strake Fit-Out: $1,214,600 for all six branches
- Next lowest quotation: $1,298,900
- Fixed price, 5% retention held for 12 months
- Contract value is below the level that requires a full tender

## Service during closure

- The Quill Van parks outside each closed branch
- Staff are redeployed to the nearest branch in the same area
- Cash machine stays in service where safe
- Members told by letter 5 weeks before closure

## Risks

- Asbestos found at older sites — surveys booked for all six
- Heat pump lead times — order placed 16 weeks ahead
- Overlap with core banking cut-over — no branch works in the cut-over month
- Cost overrun — contingency drawn only with approval of the Chief Financial Officer

## Governance

- Programme board chaired by the Head of Facilities and Procurement
- Each Area Director signs off designs for branches in their area
- Each Branch Manager is site contact for their own branch
- Progress reported to every ordinary Board meeting
- Final report to the Board when the sixth branch reopens

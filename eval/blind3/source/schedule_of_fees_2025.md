# Schedule of Fees and Charges 2025

**Marrowgate Quill Mutual**
**Applies from:** 6 January 2025

This schedule lists the fees we charge members. It applies at every branch and online.

## 1. Everyday accounts

| Fee | Amount |
|---|---|
| Monthly account maintenance | $3.20 |
| Paper statement (each) | $1.85 |
| Cash withdrawal at another provider's machine | $1.95 |
| Unpaid direct debit | $11.50 |
| Replacement card | $6.80 |
| Bank cheque | $8.25 |

The monthly maintenance fee is not charged in any month in which the balance stays at or above $1,250.

## 2. Payments

| Fee | Amount |
|---|---|
| Outbound domestic wire | $17.25 |
| Outbound international wire | $34.00 |
| Inbound wire | Free |
| Stopped cheque | $5.40 |

## 3. Savings and loans

| Fee | Amount |
|---|---|
| Early closure of a fixed-term account | 95 days' interest |
| Dormant account fee (per quarter) | $4.10 |
| Mortgage application fee | $285 |
| Arranged overdraft interest | 13.65% a year |
| Unarranged overdraft interest | 21.4% a year |

An account is dormant after 26 months without a transaction made by the member.

## 4. Membership

A one-off membership share of $7 is payable on joining. It is returned when the member leaves.

## 5. Waivers

A Branch Manager may waive fees of up to $30 for a member in any calendar year. Ask at your branch.

If we charge a fee in error we refund it within 6 working days.

## 6. Changes

We give at least 60 days' notice before raising any fee. Members were told about this schedule by letter in October 2024.

## 7. Members who do not pay the maintenance fee

Members aged under 18 or over 75 do not pay the monthly maintenance fee. No other fee is waived by age.

## 8. Questions

Ask at any branch, call 0800 555 0164, or write to Member Services, 41 Cooperage Lane, Marrowgate. If you are unhappy with a charge, please ask for our complaints leaflet.

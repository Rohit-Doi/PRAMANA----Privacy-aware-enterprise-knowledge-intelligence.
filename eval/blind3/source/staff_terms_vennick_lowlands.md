# Staff Terms Supplement — Vennick Lowlands Area

**Marrowgate Quill Mutual**
**Reference:** HR-TERMS-VL
**Effective from:** 1 April 2026
**Owner:** Head of People

## 1. Who this supplement covers

This supplement sets the local terms of employment for staff whose contractual place of work is a branch or office in the Vennick Lowlands area. It must be read with the staff member's contract and with the Society-wide Pay Scale and Allowance Bands document, which gives the money value of every grade and allowance band named here. Staff in other areas have their own supplements and the figures there are different.

## 2. Working time

1. The standard working week is 36 hours.
2. Branches in this area open on Saturday mornings on the first and third Saturday of each month. Saturday hours are paid at 1.5 times the normal hourly rate.
3. Staff who supervise a Saturday opening are paid for that session at the midpoint of grade Q2 if their own grade is lower.

## 3. Leave

| Entitlement | Vennick Lowlands figure |
|---|---|
| Annual leave | 27 days a year |
| Public holidays observed | 9 days a year |
| Additional leave after 5 years' service | 2 days |
| Leave that may be carried over | 6 days |
| Sick pay at full pay | 16 weeks |
| Parental leave at full pay (primary carer) | 22 weeks |
| Parental leave at full pay (second carer) | 7 weeks |
| Compassionate leave | 5 days per event |

A request for more than 10 consecutive working days of annual leave needs the approval of the Area Director as well as the line manager. The local public holiday, Dyke Wardens' Day, falls on the last Friday of May and all branches in the area are closed.

## 4. Allowances

| Allowance | Vennick Lowlands figure |
|---|---|
| Area cost-of-living allowance | $1,875 a year, paid in 12 equal monthly instalments |
| Late-shift meal allowance | $12.40 per shift ending after 19:00 |
| On-call allowance | Band A |
| Mileage for own vehicle | $0.54 per mile |
| Cycle-to-work allowance | $0.33 per mile |
| Professional subscription | One subscription, up to $325 a year |

The on-call band is a rate per night on call; its value is set in the Pay Scale and Allowance Bands document.

## 5. Pension

The Society contributes 8.5% of base salary to the staff pension plan for staff in this area, provided the staff member contributes at least 4%.

## 6. Probation and notice

1. New staff serve a probation period of 26 weeks.
2. During probation either side may end the employment on 3 weeks' notice.
3. After probation the notice period is 9 weeks from either side for grades Q1 to Q4 and 17 weeks for grades Q5 and above.

## 7. Relocation into the area

Staff who move into the Vennick Lowlands area at the Society's request receive a relocation grant of up to $4,275 against receipts. The grant is repayable in full if the staff member resigns within 18 months.

## 8. Queries and disputes

Questions about this supplement go first to the Branch Manager and then to the people team at people@marrowgatequill.example. A grievance about the application of these terms is heard by the Area Director, with a final appeal to the Head of People.

## 9. Review

These terms are reviewed each year with the Vennick Lowlands works council. The figures above were agreed with the council in January 2026.

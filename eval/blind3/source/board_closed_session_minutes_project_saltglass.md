# Minutes of the Board — Closed Session

**Marrowgate Quill Mutual**
**RESTRICTED — named recipients only. Project Saltglass insider list applies.**

**Meeting:** Special meeting of the Board in closed session
**Date and time:** 7 July 2026, 16:00
**Place:** Founders' Room, 41 Cooperage Lane, Marrowgate
**Present:** 11 directors, including the Chair of the Board (in the chair), the Deputy Chair, the Chief Executive and the Chief Financial Officer
**In attendance:** Society Secretary; General Counsel; two partners of Felgate Mornay LLP (advisers)

## 1. Declarations of interest

The Chair of the Audit Committee declared that a relative is a member of Dunmarrow Friendly Society. The Board agreed this was not a conflict.

## 2. Project Saltglass

The Chief Executive presented the proposal known as Project Saltglass. Under it, Dunmarrow Friendly Society would transfer its engagements to Marrowgate Quill Mutual, so that its members become members of this Society.

Key facts reported to the Board:

| Item | Figure |
|---|---|
| Dunmarrow members | 14,260 |
| Dunmarrow total assets | $318.4 million |
| Dunmarrow branches | 4 |
| Proposed welcome bonus per Dunmarrow member | $65 |
| Due diligence budget | $412,500 |
| Target completion date | 1 July 2027 |

The advisers said that two of Dunmarrow's four branches are within 3 miles of a Marrowgate Quill branch and would be likely to close within 18 months of completion.

## 3. Discussion

1. The Chair of the Risk Committee asked about Dunmarrow's loan book. The advisers said 6.8% of it is in arrears of more than 60 days.
2. The Deputy Chair asked that no Dunmarrow staff be made redundant for 12 months after completion. The Chief Executive agreed to put this in the heads of terms.
3. One director considered the welcome bonus too generous.

## 4. Decisions

1. The Board resolved to open exclusive talks with Dunmarrow Friendly Society and to begin due diligence within the budget above. Votes: 9 for, 1 against, 1 abstention.
2. The Board will take its final decision on whether to proceed at its last ordinary meeting of 2026.
3. Project Saltglass is embargoed. Nothing may be said to staff, members or the press until the public announcement, which is planned for the day after that meeting.
4. The Society Secretary will keep the insider list. At the date of this meeting it has 19 names.
5. The General Counsel will notify the Cooperative Prudential Board in confidence within 5 working days.

## 5. Close

The meeting closed at 17:25. These minutes are not to be circulated with the ordinary Board pack.

# Marrowgate Quill Mutual — Register of Appointments

**Maintained by:** Office of the Society Secretary
**Position as at:** 1 July 2026
**Reference:** SEC-REG-02

## 1. Purpose

This register records who holds each named office of Marrowgate Quill Mutual ("the Society"). Policies, procedures and board papers refer to offices by title only; this register is the single place where the current holder of each office is recorded. Where a policy requires the approval of a named office, the approval must come from the person shown here or from the deputy shown in section 5.

The Society was founded in 1887 as the Marrowgate Quill-Makers' Thrift Society and is owned by its members. Its head office is at 41 Cooperage Lane, Marrowgate.

## 5. Deputies

Where an office holder is absent for more than three working days, the following deputies may exercise the office's approvals:

- Chief Executive: the Chief Financial Officer.
- Chief Risk Officer: the Head of Compliance, except for matters where the Head of Compliance acts as Money Laundering Reporting Officer.
- Head of Lending: the Chair of the Credit Committee may convene the Credit Committee to decide instead; there is no individual deputy.
- Area Director: the Area Director of a neighbouring area, by arrangement with the Chief Executive.
- Branch Manager: the Deputy Branch Manager of the same branch.

## 4. What went wrong

1. When overtime entry moved into the payroll system on 1 June 2026, the bureau rebuilt its employee file.
2. For six records the routing code field was cut short by one character.
3. The fault was caught by the bureau's validation check 5 working days before the pay date. No payment was misdirected.

## 5. Actions

1. Each of the six staff confirmed their details in person or by a call-back to the mobile number held on file. Completed 21 August 2026.
2. The bureau has corrected its file and will credit the Society $1,275 under the service agreement.
3. The Chief Financial Officer has asked for a full reconciliation of all 312 payroll records before the September run.
4. The incident was reported to the Head of Information Security and to the Data Protection Lead on 19 August 2026. It was assessed as not reportable to the regulator because no data left the bureau.

# Internal Audit Report — Lending Controls

**Marrowgate Quill Mutual**
**Report reference:** IA-2026-07
**Issued:** 24 July 2026
**Classification:** CONFIDENTIAL
**Prepared by:** Internal Audit, for the Audit Committee

## 1. Summary

Internal Audit reviewed how the delegated lending authorities and exception rules in the current Member Lending Policy were applied in the quarter from 1 April to 30 June 2026, the first quarter under the current version of the policy.

**Overall rating: Needs Improvement.** Controls are designed adequately but were not applied consistently in two of the three areas.

This report will be presented to the Audit Committee at its third meeting of 2026. Management actions are due by the date of the Audit Committee's last meeting of 2026 unless a different date is shown.

## 2. Scope and method

1. We sampled 64 loans from the 1,318 approved in the quarter: 31 residential mortgages, 9 secured personal loans and 24 unsecured loans.
2. We tested each loan against the tier limits, the affordability limits and the exception rules of the policy in force.
3. We interviewed 11 lending staff and three Branch Managers.
4. We did not test arrears handling, which is scheduled for audit IA-2026-11.

## 3. Results at a glance

| Measure | Result |
|---|---|
| Loans sampled | 64 |
| Loans with no finding | 57 |
| Loans approved above the approver's tier limit | 2 |
| Exceptions without written approval of the Head of Lending | 3 |
| Decisions not recorded in Halyard on the day made | 2 |
| Policy exceptions as a share of new lending in the quarter | 7.9% |

## 4. Findings

| No. | Finding | Rating | Action owner |
|---|---|---|---|
| F1 | A mortgage of $291,400 at Hallam Sluice was approved at tier L2, above the L2 secured limit in the current policy | High | Head of Lending |
| F2 | A secured loan of $118,250 at Saltmere was approved at tier L1, above the L1 secured limit | High | Head of Lending |
| F3 | Three exceptions had no written approval from the Head of Lending on file | Medium | Head of Lending |
| F4 | Exceptions in the quarter reached 7.9% of new lending, above the quarterly cap in the current policy | High | Chief Risk Officer |
| F5 | Two decisions were entered in Halyard one and three days late | Low | Area Directors |
| F6 | Tier limits in the Halyard approval screen still showed the superseded policy values until 22 April 2026 | Medium | Chief Information Officer |

## 5. Matter reviewed and found in order

We examined the $760,000 facility for the Hengebarrow Grain Co-operative, approved by the Head of Lending on 21 May 2026. This is above the standing L3 secured limit. However, the Credit Committee granted a one-off case authority of $760,000 for this facility at its May 2026 meeting, and the approval was given within the 45 days allowed. We therefore do not regard it as a breach. We note that case authorities are not yet recorded in Halyard and recommend that they should be.

## 6. Root causes

1. The system limits were updated three weeks after the new policy took effect (F6), so staff relied on memory and on printed copies of both versions.
2. The briefing for lending staff on the new policy was delivered in Pellcross Basin and Vennick Lowlands after the effective date; only Orrin Straits staff were briefed in March.
3. There is no automatic running total of exceptions during the quarter; the figure is calculated only at quarter end.

## 7. Agreed management actions

1. The Head of Lending will re-approve or refer the two loans in F1 and F2 at the correct tier by 14 August 2026.
2. The Chief Information Officer will add a control so that Halyard blocks an approval above the user's tier limit, by 30 September 2026.
3. The Chief Risk Officer will introduce a monthly exception report to the Credit Committee starting with its September 2026 meeting.
4. The Area Directors will confirm that all lending staff have been briefed, by 28 August 2026.
5. The Head of Lending will report the breach of the exception cap to the Risk Committee at its third meeting of 2026.

## 8. Follow-up

Internal Audit will re-test a sample of 40 loans from the quarter ending 31 December 2026 and report to the Audit Committee in the first quarter of 2027.

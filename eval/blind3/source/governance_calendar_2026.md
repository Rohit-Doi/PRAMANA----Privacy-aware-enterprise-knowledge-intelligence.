# Marrowgate Quill Mutual — Governance Calendar 2026

**Issued by:** Society Secretary
**Issued:** 5 January 2026
**Reference:** SEC-CAL-2026

## 1. About this calendar

This calendar fixes the dates of all meetings of the Board, its committees and the Member Council for the 2026 calendar year. Policies and papers refer to meetings by their order in the year (for example "the Board's second ordinary meeting of 2026"); the date of each such meeting is set out here. Unless stated otherwise, meetings are held in the Founders' Room at head office, 41 Cooperage Lane, Marrowgate.

## 2. Board of Directors

The Board holds six ordinary meetings a year, each on a Thursday starting at 09:30.

| Meeting | Date | Principal standing business |
|---|---|---|
| First ordinary meeting | 29 January 2026 | Year-end results, draft annual report |
| Second ordinary meeting | 26 March 2026 | Annual policy approvals, AGM notice |
| Third ordinary meeting | 28 May 2026 | Capital programme decisions |
| Fourth ordinary meeting | 30 July 2026 | Half-year results, major contracts |
| Fifth ordinary meeting | 24 September 2026 | Strategy review, regulatory matters |
| Sixth (last) ordinary meeting | 19 November 2026 | Budget for 2027, corporate plan |

A special meeting of the Board in closed session is scheduled for 7 July 2026 at 16:00. Attendance at closed sessions is limited to directors, the Society Secretary and persons invited by the Chair of the Board.

The quorum for any Board meeting is six directors, of whom at least four must be non-executive.

## 3. Annual General Meeting

The 2026 Annual General Meeting is held on Saturday 25 April 2026 at 10:30 at the Tanners' Exchange Hall, Marrowgate. Voting forms must reach the scrutineers by 17:00 on 22 April 2026.

## 6. Paper deadlines

1. Papers for Board meetings must reach the Society Secretary nine working days before the meeting.
2. Papers for committee meetings must reach the committee's secretary six working days before the meeting.
3. Late papers are accepted only with the consent of the chair of the meeting concerned.

## 7. Changes

Any change of date is notified by the Society Secretary by reissuing this calendar. As at the date of issue no changes are planned. Dates for 2027 will be proposed to the Nominations and Governance Committee at its second meeting of 2026.

# How We Handle Complaints

**Marrowgate Quill Mutual — information for members**
**In force from:** 13 October 2025

## 1. Our promise

We are owned by our members. If we get something wrong we want to know, and we will try to put it right quickly and fairly. Making a complaint is free and will never affect the service you receive.

## 2. How to complain

You can complain in any of these ways:

- in person at any branch;
- by phone on 0800 555 0164;
- by letter to Member Services, 41 Cooperage Lane, Marrowgate;
- by email to complaints@marrowgatequill.example.

Please tell us your name, your membership number and what happened.

## 3. What happens next

| Step | What we do | Time limit |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | We try to resolve the matter on the spot | Same day where possible |
| 2 | We send a written acknowledgement | Within 3 working days |
| 3 | A complaints officer investigates | Updates every 10 working days |
| 4 | We send our final response | Within 23 working days |
| 5 | You may ask for a senior review | Within 30 days of our final response |

The senior review at step 5 is carried out by the Head of Compliance, who has had no part in the original decision. The review is completed within 12 working days.

## 4. Putting things right

If we have made a mistake we will apologise, correct it and refund any fee or lost interest. We may also offer a goodwill payment.

1. A Branch Manager may offer a goodwill payment of up to $135.
2. A payment above $135 and up to $900 needs the approval of the Head of Member Services.
3. A payment above $900 needs the approval of the Chief Executive.

## 5. If you are still unhappy

If you do not accept our final response, you may refer your complaint to the Mutual Sector Adjudicator, an independent body. You must do so within 7 months of the date of our final response. The Adjudicator's service is free. We are bound by its decisions up to $165,000.

## 6. How we learn

We record every complaint. The number and type of complaints, and how long we took to answer them, are reported to the Audit Committee at each of its meetings and published in our annual report. In 2025 we received 412 complaints and answered 94.7% of them within the time limit.

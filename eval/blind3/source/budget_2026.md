# Budget 2026 — Board Paper

**Marrowgate Quill Mutual**
**Paper reference:** BD-2025-11-04
**Approved by the Board:** 20 November 2025
**Classification:** CONFIDENTIAL
**Sponsor:** Chief Financial Officer

## 1. Decision

The Board approved the revenue budget and the capital programme set out below for the financial year 1 January to 31 December 2026.

## 2. Key assumptions

1. Membership grows from 59,800 to 62,500 over the year.
2. The average mortgage rate charged is 5.42% and the average rate paid on savings is 2.38%.
3. A general staff pay award of 3.15% from 1 April 2026.
4. Budgeted headcount of 312 full-time-equivalent staff.
5. Loan losses of 0.31% of the average loan book.

## 4. Capital programme

| Project | Approved ceiling | Period |
|---|---|---|
| Core banking replacement programme | $4,650,000 | 2026 to 2033 (whole-life) |
| Branch refurbishment programme | $1,386,000 | 2026 to 2027 |
| Kirra Point branch fit-out | $418,500 | 2026 |
| Cash machine replacement (11 machines) | $203,500 | 2026 |
| Cyber security tooling | $167,250 | 2026 |

A ceiling is the most that may be committed to the project without returning to the Board. The core banking ceiling covers the whole-life cost of the contract to be let by tender in 2026.

## 5. Financial controls during the year

1. A budget holder may move money between lines within their own budget (a virement) up to $48,000 without further approval.
2. A virement above $48,000 and up to $190,000 needs the approval of the Chief Financial Officer.
3. A virement above $190,000 needs the approval of the Board.
4. Any contract with a whole-life value above $2,750,000 can be awarded only by the Board.
5. Spending against the contingency of $385,000 needs the approval of the Chief Executive.

## 6. Risks to the budget

- A fall of 0.25 percentage points in the mortgage margin would reduce net interest income by about $1,730,000.
- Each 1% of pay award above the assumption adds about $171,000 to staff costs in the year.
- The regulator's levy for 2026 has not been confirmed; $236,500 is included.

## 7. Monitoring

The Chief Financial Officer reports actual results against budget to every ordinary meeting of the Board. A mid-year reforecast is presented to the Board at its fourth ordinary meeting of 2026. Budget holders receive monthly statements by the 7th working day of the following month.

## 8. Capital position

The budget leaves the Society's capital ratio at 17.3% at the end of 2026, against a regulatory minimum of 11.5% and the Board's own minimum of 14.75%.

BRENNHOLT REGIONAL TRANSIT AUTHORITY
SAFETY AND SECURITY

SECURITY STANDING ORDER SSO-04
DEPOT ACCESS, KEYS AND GUARDING

Effective from: 15 September 2025
Issued by: Head of Safety and Security
Classification: CONFIDENTIAL - depot managers, supervisors and security
staff only. Not to be displayed on noticeboards.


1. APPLICATION

1.1 This order applies at MLD, PLY, STM, AGV and FWD, and at the ferry
terminal at Quillon Pier outside public opening hours.

1.2 It replaces all earlier local instructions on depot access. Depot
managers may add local instructions but may not relax anything in this
order.


2. SECURITY ZONES

2.1 Every depot is divided into three zones.

Zone 1 is the staff car park, the mess room and the administration offices.
Any member of staff with a valid pass may enter Zone 1 at any time.

Zone 2 is the vehicle yard, the workshops and the wash bay. Entry is
limited to staff whose duties require it and to escorted visitors. A
high-visibility vest must be worn.

Zone 3 is the fuel store, the cash room, the traction power room, the
charging switchgear and the key safe. Entry is limited to staff named on
the depot's Zone 3 list, which the depot manager signs and reviews every
three months.

2.2 At AGV the signalling equipment room is also Zone 3. At FWD the
maintenance berth and the fuel barge mooring are Zone 3.


3. VISITORS AND CONTRACTORS

3.1 Every visitor signs in at the gatehouse, shows photo identification and
is issued a dated visitor pass. Visitors are escorted at all times in Zones
2 and 3.

3.2 A visitor arriving after 20:00 may be admitted only if the pass is
countersigned by the depot manager or, in the manager's absence, by the
depot supervisor on duty.

3.3 Contractors working in a depot for more than five days are issued a
contractor pass valid for no longer than 90 days. The sponsoring manager is
responsible for collecting the pass at the end of the work.


4. KEYS AND ALARM CODES

4.1 Vehicle keys are kept in the key safe and issued against the duty
sheet. A vehicle key must never be left in an unattended vehicle in the
yard.

4.2 Zone 3 keys are held by the depot manager and one named deputy. A
spare set is sealed and held in the Security Control Room.

4.3 Alarm codes are changed every 90 days, and immediately when anyone who
knows a code leaves the Authority or moves to another depot.

4.4 The loss of any key or pass must be reported to the Security Control
Room within one hour of discovery.


5. GUARDING

5.1 Guarding is provided under contract by Redvane Guarding. The contract
runs until 31 August 2027.

5.2 Between 22:00 and 05:00 there must be at least two guards on duty at
STM and at AGV, and at least one at each of MLD, PLY and FWD.

5.3 Guards patrol the perimeter and Zone 2 every two hours during those
hours and record each patrol at the electronic checkpoints. A missed
patrol is reported to the Security Control Room before the end of the
shift.

5.4 Guards do not enter Zone 3 except in an emergency or when accompanied
by a member of staff on the Zone 3 list.


6. CAMERAS

6.1 Recordings from depot cameras are kept for 31 days and then
overwritten. Recordings from cameras at Quillon Pier are kept for 45 days.

6.2 Recordings needed for an investigation or a claim are copied and kept
separately on the instruction of the Head of Safety and Security or the
General Counsel.

6.3 Only the Security Control Room and the depot manager may view
recordings. Requests from the police are passed to the Security Control
Room.


7. THREAT LEVELS

7.1 The Head of Safety and Security sets the threat level for each depot.
The levels are GREEN (normal), AMBER (heightened) and RED (specific
threat).

7.2 At AMBER, vehicle searches are carried out on every vehicle entering
Zone 2 and contractor passes are checked daily.

7.3 At RED, the depot is locked down: visitors are refused, a second guard
is added at depots that normally have one, and vehicles leave only on the
authority of the Duty Controller. Only the Head of Safety and Security may
declare or lift RED.


8. REPORTING

8.1 The Security Control Room at Calloway House is staffed 24 hours a day.
It is reached on extension 4400 or, from outside, on +1 312 555 0100.

8.2 Any intrusion, theft, assault or damage is reported to the Security
Control Room at once and followed by a written report on form S-12 within
24 hours.

8.3 The Head of Safety and Security reports security incidents to the
Safety Committee every quarter.


9. FUEL AND CASH

9.1 Fuel stock at MLD, PLY and FWD is dipped and reconciled every day. A
discrepancy of more than 150 gallons in a week is reported as a security
incident.

9.2 Cash in a depot cash room must not exceed $25,000 overnight. Cash
collections are made by the contracted carrier at varied times.


10. REVIEW

This order is reviewed every 12 months. Depot managers are audited against
it twice a year by the Safety and Security team.

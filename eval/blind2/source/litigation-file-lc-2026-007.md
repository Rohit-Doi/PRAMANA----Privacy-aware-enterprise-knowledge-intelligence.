# Litigation File LC-2026-007 — Status Note

**Brennholt Regional Transit Authority — Office of the General Counsel**
**Matter:** Halloran v. Brennholt Regional Transit Authority
**Status note dated:** 30 April 2026
**Author:** Mr. Cormac Yeardley, General Counsel and Authority Secretary
**Classification:** RESTRICTED — privileged and confidential. Prepared for the purpose of litigation. Do not forward.

## 1. The claim

1.1 The claimant, Ms. Deirdre Halloran, was a passenger injured in the incident recorded by Safety and Security as INC-2025-0388. She suffered a fractured wrist and a shoulder injury and was in hospital for two nights.

1.2 The claim was filed in the Brennholt County Circuit Court on 9 February 2026 and served on the Authority on 13 February 2026. It is for $410,000, made up of medical costs, loss of earnings and general damages. The claimant is a self-employed violin teacher and says she was unable to work for 19 weeks.

1.3 The claimant is represented by Garrity and Voss. The Authority is represented by Pruett Lindgren LLP; the lead partner is Ms. Saoirse Lindgren.

## 2. Liability

2.1 The Authority's position is that the collision was caused by the driver of the road vehicle and that his employer, Brisk Parcel Couriers, should bear most of the liability. A cross-claim against Brisk Parcel Couriers was filed on 6 March 2026.

2.2 Outside counsel's assessment is that a court would be likely to apportion liability roughly 60% to Brisk Parcel Couriers and 40% to the Authority. The Authority's share reflects the two contributory conditions identified in the investigation report, which the claimant's lawyers are expected to obtain in discovery.

2.3 Counsel advises that the Authority's own work order records are the most damaging documents and that the case should not go to trial if a reasonable settlement is available.

## 3. Insurance and reserve

3.1 The Authority's liability insurer is Carrowmere Mutual. The policy carries a deductible of $100,000 for each incident; the insurer pays the amount above that.

3.2 The insurer was notified on 20 November 2025 and has agreed to the appointment of Pruett Lindgren LLP.

3.3 A reserve of $240,000 has been set against this matter, covering the likely settlement and the Authority's legal costs. Legal costs to 30 April 2026 are $38,700.

## 4. Settlement authority

4.1 The General Counsel's standing authority allows him to settle a claim for up to $150,000. A settlement above that figure normally needs the approval of the Finance and Performance Committee.

4.2 For this matter only, the Board has given the General Counsel a higher authority by a resolution in closed session. The figure is recorded in the closed minutes and is not repeated in this note.

## 6. Preservation of documents

6.1 A litigation hold was issued on 16 February 2026 to Safety and Security, Engineering, the Central Division and IT. It covers camera footage, event recorder data, work orders, rosters and all e-mail mentioning the incident, the crossing or the claimant.

6.2 The hold remains in force until the General Counsel lifts it in writing. Routine deletion of the affected records is suspended.

## 7. Other claims from the same incident

7.1 Six other passengers have notified claims. All are for minor injuries and each is below $15,000. Four have been settled, for a total of $31,200.

7.2 The van driver has not made a claim against the Authority.

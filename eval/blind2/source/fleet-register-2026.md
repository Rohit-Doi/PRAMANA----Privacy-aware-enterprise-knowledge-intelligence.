# Fleet Register 2026

**Brennholt Regional Transit Authority — Engineering Directorate**
**Reference:** ENG-FR-2026
**Position as at:** 1 February 2026
**Maintained by:** Fleet Asset Manager, on behalf of the Director of Engineering
**Classification:** INTERNAL

## 1. Purpose

This register lists every passenger vehicle and vessel owned by the Authority, where it is based and when it entered service. Operating and security documents refer to depots by the three-letter codes in section 2.

3.1 The bus fleet totals 233 vehicles. The peak vehicle requirement is 196, leaving 37 spare or in maintenance.

3.2 The Halden C12 diesels at Marrow Lane are the oldest buses in the fleet. The Authority's policy is to retire a bus at 15 years, so the whole series falls due between 2027 and 2028. A tender for battery-electric replacements for part of the series is being prepared.

3.3 The Norvik E-12 has a usable battery capacity of 420 kWh and a planning range of 310 kilometres on a full charge. The E-18 has 560 kWh and a planning range of 260 kilometres.

5.2 MV Marlin is the smallest and oldest vessel. It is the only vessel besides MV Petrel with a draught shallow enough to use the tidal landing on Route F3. Design work on a replacement is included in the capital programme.

## 6. Maintenance intervals

6.1 Buses receive a safety inspection every 28 days and a full service every 20,000 kilometres.

6.2 Light rail vehicles receive a safety inspection every 14 days.

6.3 Ferry engines are serviced every 500 running hours.

## 7. Changes to the register

Changes are notified to fleet.assets@brennholt-transit.example. A vehicle may not enter or leave passenger service until the register has been updated.

# Uniform Allowance: Ferry Crew

**Brennholt Regional Transit Authority — Harbour Division**
**Standing instruction:** OPS-SI-13
**Effective from:** 1 July 2025
**Issued by:** Director of Operations
**Classification:** INTERNAL

## 1. Who this covers

This instruction covers ferry masters, ferry engineers and deckhands. Bus and light rail operators are covered by standing instruction OPS-SI-12 and the amounts here do not apply to them.

## 2. First issue

Every new member of ferry crew receives a first issue of uniform free of charge before their first sailing. The first issue is three shirts, two pairs of trousers, one fleece, one waterproof jacket and one knitted hat. The first issue is not charged against the annual allowance.

## 3. Annual allowance

3.1 Each member of ferry crew has a uniform allowance of $520 a year. It is credited on 1 July each year.

3.2 Ferry masters receive a further $75 a year for epaulettes and a cap.

3.3 Up to $100 of an unspent balance may be carried over to the following year. Anything above that is cancelled on 30 June.

3.4 Orders are placed through the Corvane Workwear portal from the marine section of the approved catalogue.

## 4. Deck boots and foul-weather gear

4.1 In addition to the allowance, each member of crew receives a voucher for non-slip deck boots worth $140 once every 12 months.

4.2 Foul-weather gear (a waterproof jacket, over-trousers and gloves) is issued by the depot and replaced every two years. It is not charged against the allowance.

4.3 Lifejackets and safety harnesses are safety equipment, not uniform. They belong to the vessel and are inspected under the Marine Operations Manual.

## 5. Cleaning

Uniform that has been soiled by fuel, oil or bilge water is cleaned at the Authority's expense through the laundry service at Fennick Wharf Depot. Ordinary laundry costs are not reimbursed.

## 6. Leavers

A member of crew who leaves within 12 months of joining must repay 50% of the value of the first issue. All leavers must return waterproof jackets, foul-weather gear and epaulettes on their last working day.

## 7. Standards of dress

Uniform must be worn at all times when on board a vessel in service or on duty at a terminal. Masters wear epaulettes whenever passengers are on board. Non-slip footwear is compulsory on deck.

## 8. Enquiries

Questions about entitlement are decided by the Marine Superintendent.

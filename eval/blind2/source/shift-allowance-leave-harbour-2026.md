# Harbour Division Shift Allowance and Leave Schedule 2026

**Brennholt Regional Transit Authority — Harbour Division**
**Reference:** HR-SCH-H-2026
**Effective from:** 1 January 2026
**Issued by:** Director of People, with the General Manager, Harbour Division
**Classification:** INTERNAL

## 1. Application

This schedule applies to operating-grade staff (OP1 to OP5) based at Fennick Wharf Depot and Quillon Pier, including ferry masters, ferry engineers, deckhands and the bus operators who work Harbour Division routes. Staff in the West and Central Divisions have their own schedules; the amounts below must not be applied outside the Harbour Division.

## 2. Shift allowances

| Allowance | When it is paid | Amount |
|---|---|---|
| Early shift | Shift starts before 05:45 | $8.75 per shift |
| Late shift | Shift finishes after 21:30 | $10.00 per shift |
| Night shift | Any part of the shift falls between 00:00 and 04:00 | $22.00 per shift |
| Split shift | Unpaid gap of more than 2 hours within one duty | $16.50 per shift |
| Sunday premium | All hours worked on a Sunday | 35% on the hourly rate |
| Tide-shift supplement | Crew rostered on Route F3 on a day when sailing times are moved for the tide | $19.00 per day |
| Sea-duty meal allowance | Crew on board for more than 6 hours without a shore break | $13.50 per day |

2.1 Only one of the early, late and night allowances is paid for any one shift. Where a shift qualifies for more than one, the highest is paid.

2.2 The split shift allowance, the tide-shift supplement and the sea-duty meal allowance are paid in addition to any other allowance.

2.3 Allowances are paid with the pay run for the month after the one in which they were earned.

## 3. Authority Day

Staff rostered to work on Authority Day, on the date fixed in the Governance Calendar, are paid at one and three-quarter times the hourly rate for the hours worked and may take a day off in lieu within 30 days.

## 4. Annual leave

4.1 The leave year runs from 1 July to 30 June.

4.2 The annual leave entitlement is 21 working days. It rises to 26 working days once an employee has completed 6 years of continuous service.

4.3 Up to 6 days of unused leave may be carried over. Carried-over leave must be taken by 30 September and is lost after that date.

4.4 Leave requests must be made at least 14 days before the first day of leave.

4.5 No more than 15% of the crew of any one vessel roster, or of the bus operators at Fennick Wharf Depot, may be on annual leave at the same time.

4.6 Ferry masters must agree their leave with the Marine Superintendent, who checks that a certificated relief master is available.

## 5. Sick leave

5.1 An employee is entitled to 9 days of sick leave on full pay in each leave year.

5.2 A medical certificate is required for any absence longer than 3 consecutive working days. Seagoing staff returning after more than 14 days of sickness must be passed fit by the Authority's marine medical examiner before going back on board.

5.3 Absence must be reported to the Duty Controller at Quillon Pier at least 2 hours before the start of the shift.

## 6. Other leave

| Type of leave | Entitlement |
|---|---|
| Bereavement leave (close family) | 4 days paid |
| Jury service | Paid for the duration, on proof of attendance |
| Study leave for approved courses, including marine certificates | Up to 8 days paid per year |
| Volunteer day | 2 days paid per year |

**Brennholt Regional Transit Authority**
**Document reference:** GOV-REG-01

**Position as at:** 12 January 2026
**Classification:** INTERNAL

## 1. Purpose

1.1 This register records who holds each senior post and each designated role named in the Authority's policies, standing orders and schemes of delegation. Where a policy assigns a duty to a post (for example "the Director of Operations"), the person named in this register is the person who must discharge it.

1.2 The register is reissued whenever an appointment changes. Enquiries go to the Authority Secretary's office at secretary@brennholt-transit.example.

## 6. Deputising arrangements

6.1 When the Chief Executive is absent for more than three consecutive working days, the Chief Financial Officer acts as Chief Executive.

6.2 When the Director of Operations is absent, the General Manager, Central Division exercises the Director's approval powers, including approvals under the Overtime and Fatigue Management Policy.

6.3 When the Marine Superintendent is absent, her duties pass to the General Manager, Harbour Division. Decisions that require a certificated master are referred to the senior ferry master on duty.

6.4 The Head of Safety and Security has no standing deputy. In her absence the Chief Executive nominates a deputy in writing for each period of absence.

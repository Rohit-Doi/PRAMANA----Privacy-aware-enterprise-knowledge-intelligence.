# Operating Grades Pay Scales 2026

**Brennholt Regional Transit Authority**
**Reference:** HR-PAY-2026
**Effective from:** 1 January 2026
**Issued by:** Director of People
**Classification:** CONFIDENTIAL — for managers, payroll and people teams only

## 1. Scope

These scales apply to all employees in operating grades OP1 to OP7 in every division and at head office. They do not cover members of the Executive Leadership Team, whose pay is set by the Remuneration Committee.

## 2. Annual salary scales

The figures below include the general uplift of 3.2% applied on 1 January 2026.

| Grade | Typical roles | Scale minimum | Scale maximum |
|---|---|---|---|
| OP1 | Depot assistant, vehicle cleaner, terminal attendant | $38,400 | $44,100 |
| OP2 | Bus operator, deckhand, ticket office clerk | $46,800 | $55,900 |
| OP3 | Light rail operator, ferry engineer, revenue protection officer | $52,300 | $62,700 |
| OP4 | Ferry master, senior technician | $61,000 | $73,400 |
| OP5 | Duty controller, depot supervisor | $68,500 | $81,200 |
| OP6 | Depot manager, lead service planner | $84,000 | $101,500 |
| OP7 | Head of function | $104,000 | $128,000 |

## 3. Progression

3.1 Progression within a grade is by an annual increment of 2.5% of salary, in addition to any general uplift. An increment can never take a salary above the scale maximum.

3.2 The increment is applied on 1 January each year, provided the employee has at least six months' service in the grade and is not subject to a live formal warning.

3.3 New starters are placed on the scale minimum unless the Director of People approves a higher starting salary. A starting salary more than 10% above the scale minimum also needs the approval of the Chief Financial Officer.

3.4 On promotion an employee moves to the lowest point in the new grade that gives an increase of at least 4% on their previous salary.

## 4. Hourly rate

The hourly rate used for overtime, premiums and deductions is the annual salary divided by 2,080.

## 5. Trainees and apprentices

5.1 Trainee bus operators are paid 85% of the OP2 scale minimum during their training period, which lasts up to 12 weeks. They move to the OP2 scale minimum on the day they pass their route assessment.

5.2 Engineering apprentices are paid 60% of the OP1 scale minimum in their first year, 75% in their second year and 90% in their third year.

## 6. Market supplements

6.1 A market supplement may be paid where a post has been advertised twice without a suitable appointment. The supplement may not exceed 8% of salary and is reviewed every 12 months.

6.2 At the date of issue, market supplements are in payment for marine senior technicians (6%) and light rail signalling technicians (8%). No other role attracts a supplement.

## 7. Long-service award

Employees receive a one-off payment of $750 on completing 10 years of continuous service, $1,500 at 20 years and $2,500 at 30 years. The award is paid in the month following the anniversary.

## 8. Pay dates

Salaries are paid on the 25th of each month, or on the last working day before it. The payroll cut-off for changes is the 8th of the month.

## 9. Confidentiality

Individual salaries are confidential to the employee, their line managers, payroll and the people team. Scale minima and maxima may be quoted in job advertisements. This document must not be forwarded outside the Authority.

## 10. Next review

The next general pay review is planned to take effect on 1 January 2027. The amount will be notified separately once negotiations with staff representatives have concluded.

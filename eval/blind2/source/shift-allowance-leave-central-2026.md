# Central Division Shift Allowance and Leave Schedule 2026

**Brennholt Regional Transit Authority — Central Division**
**Reference:** HR-SCH-C-2026
**Effective from:** 1 January 2026
**Issued by:** Director of People, with the General Manager, Central Division
**Classification:** INTERNAL

## 1. Application

This schedule applies to operating-grade staff (OP1 to OP5) based at Stanmere Depot and Ashgrove Light Rail Depot, including Copperline operators and platform staff. Staff in the West and Harbour Divisions have their own schedules; the amounts below must not be applied outside the Central Division.

## 2. Shift allowances

| Allowance | When it is paid | Amount |
|---|---|---|
| Early shift | Shift starts before 05:00 | $10.25 per shift |
| Late shift | Shift finishes after 22:30 | $12.50 per shift |
| Night shift | Any part of the shift falls between 00:30 and 04:30 | $27.50 per shift |
| Split shift | Unpaid gap of more than 2 hours within one duty | $15.00 per shift |
| Sunday premium | All hours worked on a Sunday | 30% on the hourly rate |
| Single-line working supplement | Light rail operator works a shift during planned single-line working | $18.00 per shift |

2.1 Only one of the early, late and night allowances is paid for any one shift. Where a shift qualifies for more than one, the highest is paid.

2.2 The split shift allowance and the single-line working supplement are paid in addition to any other allowance.

2.3 Allowances are paid with the pay run for the month in which they were earned.

## 3. Authority Day

Staff rostered to work on Authority Day, on the date fixed in the Governance Calendar, are paid at twice the hourly rate for the hours worked. No additional leave is given.

## 4. Annual leave

4.1 The leave year runs from 1 April to 31 March.

4.2 The annual leave entitlement is 24 working days. It rises to 28 working days once an employee has completed 10 years of continuous service.

4.3 Up to 4 days of unused leave may be carried over. Carried-over leave must be taken by 30 June and is lost after that date.

4.4 Leave requests must be made at least 28 days before the first day of leave.

4.5 No more than 10% of the operators at a depot may be on annual leave at the same time. Light rail operators and bus operators are counted separately.

4.6 Leave is not normally granted to light rail operators in the week of a timetable change.

## 5. Sick leave

5.1 An employee is entitled to 12 days of sick leave on full pay in each leave year.

5.2 A medical certificate is required for any absence longer than 4 consecutive working days.

5.3 Absence must be reported to the depot control room at least 2 hours before the start of the shift.

## 6. Other leave

| Type of leave | Entitlement |
|---|---|
| Bereavement leave (close family) | 5 days paid |
| Jury service | Paid for the duration, on proof of attendance |
| Study leave for approved courses | Up to 6 days paid per year |
| Volunteer day | 1 day paid per year |

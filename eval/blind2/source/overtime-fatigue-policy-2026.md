# Overtime and Fatigue Management Policy

**Brennholt Regional Transit Authority**
**Policy number:** HR-POL-07, version 2
**Effective from:** 1 February 2026
**Policy owner:** Director of People
**Approved by:** Executive Leadership Team, 15 December 2025
**Classification:** INTERNAL

This version replaces version 1 of HR-POL-07 (effective 1 April 2024), which is withdrawn. The changes follow the interim recommendations of a safety investigation, issued in December 2025, and consultation with staff representatives in all three divisions.

## 1. Who this policy covers

1.1 This policy covers every employee in an operating grade (OP1 to OP5) in the West, Central and Harbour Divisions.

1.2 Employees at grade OP6 and above are not paid overtime. They receive time off in lieu for extra hours worked at the request of their manager.

1.3 Agency workers placed with the Authority are subject to the limits in sections 3 and 5 but are paid by their agency.

## 2. Standard hours

The standard working week is 40 hours, averaged over a four-week roster cycle. Any hours worked beyond 40 in a week, at the request of a supervisor, are overtime.

## 3. Limits on overtime

3.1 An employee may work up to 12 hours of overtime in a week on the authority of the depot supervisor.

3.2 Overtime above 12 hours in a week requires the prior written approval of the Director of Operations. A Division General Manager can no longer approve overtime above the weekly limit for staff in their own division.

3.3 No employee may work more than 20 hours of overtime in a week in any circumstances.

3.4 Overtime is voluntary. No employee will be treated less favourably for declining it.

## 4. Overtime pay

4.1 The first 6 hours of overtime in a week are paid at one and a half times the employee's hourly rate.

4.2 Overtime beyond 6 hours in a week, and all overtime worked on a rostered rest day, is paid at twice the hourly rate.

4.3 Overtime worked on a public holiday is paid at two and a half times the hourly rate.

4.4 The hourly rate is calculated from the employee's annual salary as described in the Operating Grades Pay Scales.

4.5 An employee may choose time off in lieu instead of payment. Time off in lieu must be taken within 90 days of the week in which it was earned, after which it is paid out at the plain hourly rate.

## 5. Rest and driving limits

5.1 Every employee must have at least 11 hours of rest between the end of one shift and the start of the next.

5.2 No employee may drive, or operate a light rail vehicle, for more than 8.5 hours in a single shift.

5.3 A break of at least 45 minutes must be taken after no more than 4.5 hours of continuous driving.

5.4 No employee may work more than 6 consecutive days.

5.5 Ferry masters and deck crew are additionally bound by the hours-of-rest rules in the Harbour Division Marine Operations Manual. Where the two differ, the stricter rule applies.

## 6. Fatigue self-declaration

6.1 An employee who feels too tired to work safely must tell the Duty Controller before the shift starts, or stop at the next safe point if already on duty.

6.2 A fatigue self-declaration carries no loss of pay for the shift concerned and is not counted as sickness absence.

6.3 The Duty Controller arranges cover and records the declaration in the fatigue register. A supervisor must not ask an employee to withdraw a declaration.

6.4 An employee who makes three declarations within a rolling 13-week period is offered an appointment with occupational health within 10 working days. The appointment is supportive, not disciplinary.

## 7. Roster design

7.1 Rosters must not schedule more than four consecutive night shifts.

7.2 Rosters are published at least 21 days before they take effect. Changes inside that period need the employee's agreement.

## 8. Records and reporting

8.1 Depot supervisors keep a weekly record of overtime worked by each employee. The record is sent to the division's General Manager and to the Director of Operations every Monday and is kept for five years.

8.2 The Director of People reports overtime hours, fatigue declarations and any breach of sections 3 and 5 to the Safety Committee every quarter.

## 9. Breaches

A supervisor who rosters an employee in breach of sections 3 or 5 must report it within 24 hours to the Head of Safety and Security. Deliberate or repeated breaches are handled under the disciplinary procedure.

## 10. Review

This policy will be reviewed in February 2028, or earlier if working-time rules change.

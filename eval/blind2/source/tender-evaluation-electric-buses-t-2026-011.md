# Tender Evaluation Report: Battery-Electric Buses for Marrow Lane

**Brennholt Regional Transit Authority — Engineering Directorate and Procurement**
**Tender reference:** BRTA-T-2026-011
**Evaluation panel meeting:** 3 June 2026
**Report to:** Finance and Performance Committee and the Board
**Classification:** CONFIDENTIAL — commercially sensitive. Bidders' prices and scores must not be disclosed before the contract is awarded.

## 1. What is being bought

1.1 The tender is for 36 battery-electric buses, 12 metres long, to replace the oldest of the diesel buses based at Marrow Lane Depot. Charging equipment for the depot is being bought under a separate tender.

1.2 The tender was advertised on 9 March 2026 and closed on 11 May 2026. Seven firms asked for the documents and four submitted bids.

## 3. Evaluation method

3.1 Bids were scored out of 100 against five criteria: price (40 points), technical quality (30), whole-life cost (15), delivery programme (10) and social value (5).

3.2 The price score was calculated by formula: the lowest price received 40 points and every other bid received 40 multiplied by the lowest price and divided by its own price.

3.3 The tender documents stated that a bid scoring fewer than 18 points out of 30 for technical quality would be rejected, whatever its total.

## 5. Panel's comments on each bid

5.1 **Norvik Mobility.** The highest technical score. The bus offered is a development of the Norvik E-12 already in service with the Authority, which simplifies training and spares. Battery capacity is 480 kWh. The battery warranty is 8 years or 500,000 kilometres. Norvik offered the lowest energy consumption in the test cycle.

5.2 **Halden Vehicle Works.** A competitive price and the best delivery programme, but this would be Halden's first battery-electric bus in fleet service anywhere, and the panel marked down the technical score for the lack of operating evidence. The battery warranty is 6 years.

5.3 **Castellan eBus.** A strong technical bid at the highest price. Delivery would begin three months later than Norvik's.

5.4 **Druvia Motors.** The lowest price, but the bid did not reach the minimum technical score. The bus offered did not meet the specified range on the hill test representing Route 3, and the bid gave no evidence of a spare parts operation in the region. The bid was rejected under the rule in paragraph 3.3.

## 6. Recommendation

6.1 The panel unanimously recommends that the contract be awarded to Norvik Mobility.

6.2 The contract value is $32,832,000 for 36 buses. This is within the amount set aside for the buses in the capital programme for financial year 2027.

6.3 Because of its value, the award falls in the highest financial delegation band and must be approved by the Board. The panel asks that it be put to the Board at the meeting at which the budget is adopted.

## 7. Delivery and payment

7.1 Norvik will deliver the first 12 buses by 30 April 2027 and the remaining 24 by 30 September 2027.

7.2 Payment is 20% on order, 70% on acceptance of each bus and 10% retained for 12 months after acceptance.

7.3 Liquidated damages for late delivery are $1,500 per bus per week, capped at 10% of the price of the bus.

## 8. Standstill and next steps

8.1 After the Board's decision, all four bidders will be told the outcome on the same day. A standstill period of 10 days then applies before the contract is signed.

8.2 Unsuccessful bidders may ask for a debrief within 15 days of being told the outcome. Debriefs are given by the procurement lead.

8.3 The diesel buses being replaced will be withdrawn as the new buses are accepted. Their disposal is not part of this tender.

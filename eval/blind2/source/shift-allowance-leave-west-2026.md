# West Division Shift Allowance and Leave Schedule 2026

**Brennholt Regional Transit Authority — West Division**
**Reference:** HR-SCH-W-2026
**Effective from:** 1 January 2026
**Issued by:** Director of People, with the General Manager, West Division
**Classification:** INTERNAL

## 1. Application

This schedule applies to operating-grade staff (OP1 to OP5) based at Marrow Lane Depot and Pellham Yard. Staff in the Central and Harbour Divisions have their own schedules; the amounts below must not be applied outside the West Division.

## 2. Shift allowances

| Allowance | When it is paid | Amount |
|---|---|---|
| Early shift | Shift starts before 05:30 | $9.50 per shift |
| Late shift | Shift finishes after 22:00 | $11.00 per shift |
| Night shift | Any part of the shift falls between 00:00 and 04:30 | $24.00 per shift |
| Split shift | Unpaid gap of more than 2 hours within one duty | $14.00 per shift |
| Sunday premium | All hours worked on a Sunday | 25% on the hourly rate |
| Hill route supplement | Duty includes at least three round trips on Route 3 | $6.00 per shift |

2.1 Only one of the early, late and night allowances is paid for any one shift. Where a shift qualifies for more than one, the highest is paid.

2.2 The split shift allowance and the hill route supplement are paid in addition to any other allowance.

2.3 Allowances are paid with the pay run for the month after the one in which they were earned.

## 3. Authority Day

Staff rostered to work on Authority Day, on the date fixed in the Governance Calendar, are paid at one and a half times the hourly rate for the hours worked and receive one additional day of leave.

## 4. Annual leave

4.1 The leave year runs from 1 January to 31 December.

4.2 The annual leave entitlement is 22 working days. It rises to 27 working days once an employee has completed 8 years of continuous service.

4.3 Up to 5 days of unused leave may be carried over. Carried-over leave must be taken by 31 March and is lost after that date. Carry-over requests are made to the depot manager on form W-LV2.

4.4 Leave requests must be made at least 21 days before the first day of leave.

4.5 No more than 12% of the bus operators at a depot may be on annual leave at the same time. Requests are granted in the order received.

4.6 Between 15 June and 31 August an operator may take no more than 10 consecutive working days of leave.

## 5. Sick leave

5.1 An employee is entitled to 10 days of sick leave on full pay in each leave year.

5.2 A medical certificate is required for any absence longer than 3 consecutive working days.

5.3 Absence must be reported to the depot control room at least 90 minutes before the start of the shift.

## 6. Other leave

| Type of leave | Entitlement |
|---|---|
| Bereavement leave (close family) | 4 days paid |
| Jury service | Paid for the duration, on proof of attendance |
| Study leave for approved courses | Up to 5 days paid per year |
| Volunteer day | 1 day paid per year |

# Uniform Allowance: Bus and Light Rail Operators

**Brennholt Regional Transit Authority**
**Standing instruction:** OPS-SI-12
**Effective from:** 1 April 2025
**Issued by:** Director of Operations
**Classification:** INTERNAL

## 1. Who this covers

This instruction covers bus operators and Copperline light rail operators in all three divisions. Ferry masters, ferry engineers and deckhands are covered by a separate instruction (OPS-SI-13) and the amounts here do not apply to them.

## 2. First issue

2.1 Every new operator receives a first issue of uniform free of charge before their first day in passenger service. The first issue is three shirts, two pairs of trousers or skirts, one jacket, one tie or neck scarf and one high-visibility vest.

2.2 The first issue is not charged against the annual allowance.

## 3. Annual allowance

3.1 Each operator has a uniform allowance of $380 a year. It is credited to the operator's account on the supplier portal on 1 April each year.

3.2 Operators who join after 1 October receive half the allowance for that year.

3.3 Any balance left at 31 March is cancelled. Balances do not carry over to the next year.

3.4 The allowance may only be spent on items in the approved catalogue. The current supplier is Corvane Workwear and orders are placed through its portal using the operator's staff number.

## 4. Footwear and winter coat

4.1 In addition to the allowance, each operator receives a voucher for safety footwear worth $95 once every 18 months.

4.2 A winter coat is issued every three years. An operator may ask for an earlier replacement if the coat is damaged in the course of duty; the depot supervisor decides.

## 5. Alterations and cleaning

5.1 The Authority reimburses tailoring of uniform items up to $40 a year on production of a receipt.

5.2 Dry-cleaning and laundry costs are not reimbursed.

## 6. Leavers

6.1 An operator who leaves within 6 months of joining must repay 50% of the value of the first issue. The amount is deducted from final pay.

6.2 All leavers must return jackets, coats and high-visibility vests to their depot on their last working day. Shirts and trousers need not be returned.

## 7. Standards of dress

7.1 Uniform must be worn at all times when in passenger service. The high-visibility vest must be worn whenever an operator is on foot in a depot yard or on the light rail track.

7.2 Name badges are issued by the depot and replaced free of charge.

7.3 Operators may wear the short-sleeved shirt without a tie between 1 May and 30 September.

## 8. Enquiries

Questions about orders go to the depot administration office. Disputes about entitlement are decided by the depot manager.

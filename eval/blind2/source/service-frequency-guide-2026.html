How to read this guide

This guide shows how often each route runs. The figures are the number of minutes between departures. For exact departure times, use the timetable posted at each stop or the journey planner.

Peak hours are 06:30 to 09:30 and 16:00 to 19:00, Monday to Friday. Evening means after 19:00 on any day. On Saturdays every route runs at its off-peak frequency all day until the evening.

About the Copperline

The Copperline is 17.4 kilometres long and has 19 stops. The end-to-end journey from Ashgrove to Lindenmoor takes 41 minutes. Trams run every five minutes in peak hours, which is the most frequent service on the network.

About the ferries

Crossing times are 18 minutes to Garrow Point, 35 minutes to Sablewick Island and 50 minutes to Marlinspike Cove. Route F3 uses a tidal landing, so on some days sailings are moved earlier or later; changes are posted at Quillon Pier by 16:00 on the day before. Route F3 does not run on days when the forecast wind at Marlinspike Cove is above 30 knots.

What changed on 12 April 2026

Route 14 now runs every 6 minutes in peak hours instead of every 7.5 minutes. The last departure on Route 8 is 25 minutes later than before. Sunday sailings on Route F1 have been improved from every 60 minutes to every 40 minutes. There are no changes to Routes 3, 22, 31 and 47.

Public holidays

On public holidays all routes run to the Sunday frequency, except Route 47, which does not run. On Authority Day services run to the Saturday timetable.

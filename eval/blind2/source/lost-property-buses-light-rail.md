# Lost Property on Buses and the Copperline

**Brennholt Regional Transit Authority**
**Passenger information notice:** PIN-08
**Effective from:** 1 September 2025
**Classification:** PUBLIC

## 1. What this notice covers

This notice explains what happens to items found on the Authority's buses, on Copperline light rail vehicles, and at bus stations and light rail stops. Items found on ferries or at ferry terminals are handled differently and are covered by a separate notice.

## 2. Where found items go

2.1 Drivers and operators hand in found items at their depot at the end of their duty. Items are then taken to the Lost Property Office, which is in Room G14 on the ground floor of Brennholt Central Station, next to the ticket hall.

2.2 An item normally reaches the Lost Property Office within two working days of being found. Please allow that time before visiting.

2.3 Bicycles are not taken to the Lost Property Office. They are kept at Stanmere Depot and can be collected by appointment.

## 3. Opening hours and contact

The Lost Property Office is open Monday to Friday from 08:00 to 18:00 and on Saturday from 09:00 to 13:00. It is closed on Sundays and public holidays. You can call +1 312 555 0147 during opening hours or write to lostproperty@brennholt-transit.example. Enquiries by e-mail are answered within three working days.

## 4. How long items are kept

4.1 Most items are kept for 60 days from the date they were found.

4.2 Bicycles are kept for 30 days.

4.3 Perishable food is thrown away at the end of the day on which it is found.

4.4 Bank cards, passports, driving licences and other identity documents are kept for 7 days and then sent to the issuing organisation or handed to the police.

4.5 Cash of more than $50, whether loose or in a wallet or purse, is handed to the police within 48 hours. Smaller sums are kept with the item.

## 5. Collecting an item

5.1 You will be asked to describe the item and to show photo identification.

5.2 A handling fee is charged when an item is collected: $5 for most items and $15 for electronic devices, bags and suitcases. There is no fee for the return of medical items, mobility aids, or children's car seats and pushchairs.

5.3 Someone else can collect an item for you if they bring a signed letter from you and their own identification.

5.4 We can post an item to an address in the Brennholt region for a flat charge of $12 in addition to the handling fee. We do not post items worth more than $300 or anything fragile.

## 6. Unclaimed items

Items not claimed within the period in section 4 become the property of the Authority. Clothing, books and toys are given to the Harbourlight Trust, a local charity. Other items are sold at a public auction held twice a year, and the proceeds go to the Authority's passenger assistance fund. Data is wiped from electronic devices before they are sold.

## 7. If you find something

Please hand any item you find to the driver or to a member of station staff. Do not take it home with the intention of returning it yourself.

## 8. Suspicious items

Unattended items that appear suspicious are not treated as lost property. They are reported to the Security Control Room and dealt with under the Authority's security procedures.

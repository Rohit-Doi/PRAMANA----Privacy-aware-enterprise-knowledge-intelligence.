# Lost Property on Ferries

**Brennholt Regional Transit Authority — Harbour Division**
**Passenger information notice:** PIN-09
**Effective from:** 1 September 2025
**Classification:** PUBLIC

## 1. What this notice covers

This notice explains what happens to items found on board the Authority's ferries, at Quillon Pier Terminal and at the landings on ferry routes F1, F2 and F3. Items found on buses and on the Copperline are handled by the Lost Property Office at Brennholt Central Station and are covered by a separate notice.

## 2. Where found items go

2.1 An item found on board is kept by the crew until the vessel finishes its sailings for the day. It is then handed in at the Purser's Desk in the departure hall at Quillon Pier Terminal.

2.2 Items left at the Sablewick Island landing are kept at the island kiosk for 3 days. If nobody has claimed them by then, they are brought across to the Purser's Desk.

## 3. Opening hours and contact

The Purser's Desk is open every day from 06:30 to 21:30, including Sundays and public holidays. You can call +1 312 555 0152 or write to ferries.lostproperty@brennholt-transit.example.

## 4. How long items are kept

4.1 Most items are kept for 45 days from the date they were found.

4.2 Wet clothing, bait, fishing catch and food are thrown away after 24 hours.

4.3 Passports and other identity documents are handed to the harbour police within 24 hours.

4.4 Cash of more than $100 is handed to the harbour police within 24 hours. Smaller sums are kept with the item.

## 5. Collecting an item

5.1 You will be asked to describe the item and to show photo identification.

5.2 A handling fee is charged when an item is collected: $8 for most items and $20 for electronic devices, bags and fishing equipment. There is no fee for medical items or mobility aids.

5.3 Items must be collected in person from the Purser's Desk.

## 6. Valuable and unclaimed items

6.1 Any unclaimed item that appears to be worth more than $500 is reported to the Marine Superintendent, who decides how it is disposed of.

6.2 Other unclaimed items become the property of the Authority at the end of the 45 days. Clothing is given to the Harbourlight Trust. Remaining items are sent to the Authority's twice-yearly public auction.

## 7. Items lost overboard

The Authority does not attempt to recover items that fall into the water. If you drop something overboard, please tell a member of crew, but do not expect the vessel to stop or turn back unless a person is in danger.

## 8. If you find something

Please hand any item you find to a member of crew or to the Purser's Desk.

# Fare Tariff Schedule 2025

**Brennholt Regional Transit Authority**
**Tariff reference:** FT-2025
**Effective from:** Monday 3 February 2025
**Approved by the Board:** 28 November 2024
**Classification:** PUBLIC

## 1. Scope

This schedule sets the fares charged on all Authority bus routes, on the Copperline light rail line and on ferry routes F1, F2 and F3. It should be read with the Conditions of Carriage.

## 2. Fare table

| Product | Adult | Concession |
|---|---|---|
| Single journey, bus, paid with TapLine card | $2.40 | $1.20 |
| Single journey, bus, paid in cash on board | $2.75 | $1.40 |
| Single journey, Copperline light rail | $2.90 | $1.45 |

| 30-day pass, all modes | $92.00 | $46.00 |

## 3. Transfers

3.1 A passenger who pays a single fare with a TapLine card may transfer to another bus or to the Copperline without further charge within 75 minutes of the first tap.

3.2 Transfers to or from a ferry are not free. A passenger transferring from bus or light rail to a ferry within the 75-minute window pays the ferry fare less the fare already paid.

3.3 Cash fares do not include a transfer.

## 4. Daily cap

TapLine card users are charged no more than $7.50 in any one service day (half that for concession cards). A service day runs from 04:00 to 03:59.

## 5. Children

Children under 6 travel free when accompanied by a fare-paying passenger. No more than three children may travel free with one adult. Children aged 6 and over pay the concession fare until their 16th birthday.

## 6. Concessions

Concession fares are available to holders of a valid Brennholt Access Card. Eligibility for the Access Card is set out in the Conditions of Carriage.

## 7. TapLine card

7.1 A new TapLine card costs $4.00. The fee is not refundable.

7.2 The minimum top-up is $5.00 at ticket machines and $10.00 online. The maximum stored balance is $250.00.

7.3 Light rail platforms and ferry terminals have ticket machines that accept cash and bank cards. Cash is accepted on board buses; drivers give change up to $10.

## 8. Group and school travel

Organised school groups of ten or more pupils travelling between 09:30 and 15:00 on school days pay $1.00 per pupil per journey, with one supervising adult free for every ten pupils. Bookings must be made five working days ahead through the Customer Centre.

## 9. Park and ride

Parking at Thornaby Park and Ride is free for passengers holding a valid ticket or pass for Route 47. Vehicles left for longer than 24 hours may be removed.

## 10. Revision

This tariff replaces the schedule that took effect in February 2024. Fares are reviewed each autumn by the Director of Fares and Commercial and any change is approved by the Board.

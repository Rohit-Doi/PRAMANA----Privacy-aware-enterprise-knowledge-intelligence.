# Conditions of Carriage

**Brennholt Regional Transit Authority**
**Reference:** LEG-COC-06
**Effective from:** 2 March 2026
**Issued by:** Office of the General Counsel
**Classification:** PUBLIC

## 1. Application

1.1 These Conditions apply to every journey on a bus, light rail vehicle or ferry operated by the Brennholt Regional Transit Authority ("the Authority"), and to everyone on the Authority's stops, platforms, terminals and piers.

1.2 By boarding a vehicle or vessel a passenger accepts these Conditions. Fares are set out separately in the current Fare Tariff Schedule; these Conditions do not state fare amounts.

1.3 Staff may refuse travel to anyone who does not comply with these Conditions, and may ask a passenger to leave a vehicle, vessel or station.

## 2. Tickets and validation

2.1 Every passenger must hold a valid ticket, pass or TapLine card for the whole of the journey, and must show it to a Revenue Protection Officer when asked.

2.2 TapLine cards must be tapped on a validator when boarding a bus, before entering a Copperline platform and at the ferry terminal gate. On ferries a second tap is required when leaving the terminal at the destination.

2.3 If a validator is not working, the passenger must tell the driver or a member of crew. A passenger who has done so will not be charged a penalty fare for that journey.

2.4 Paper tickets are valid only on the day of issue unless they state otherwise. Tickets are not transferable once a journey has begun.

## 3. Concession eligibility

3.1 Concession fares are available only to holders of a valid Brennholt Access Card. The following people may apply for an Access Card:

- (a) anyone aged 65 or over;
- (b) full-time students under 23 enrolled at a school, college or university in the Brennholt region;
- (c) holders of a Disability Access Certificate issued by the regional health board, together with one companion travelling with them;
- (d) armed forces veterans resident in the Brennholt region.

3.2 An Access Card is issued free of charge the first time. A replacement for a lost or damaged card costs $8.00.

3.3 Student Access Cards expire on 31 August each year and must be renewed with proof of enrolment. All other Access Cards are valid for four years.

3.4 The Access Card must be carried when travelling on a concession fare. A passenger who cannot show it is treated as travelling without a valid ticket.

## 4. Luggage, bicycles and animals

4.1 Each passenger may bring two items of luggage. No item may weigh more than 25 kg or be longer than 90 cm in any direction. Luggage must not block aisles, doors or wheelchair spaces.

4.2 Folded bicycles are carried free on all services at all times. Non-folding bicycles are carried as follows:

- on the Copperline outside peak hours only, with no more than two bicycles per vehicle;
- on ferries at all times, up to 12 bicycles per sailing, or 8 on the MV Marlin;
- on buses only on Route 47, which has a front rack for two bicycles.

4.3 Peak hours are 06:30 to 09:30 and 16:00 to 19:00 on weekdays.

4.4 Assistance animals travel free on all services at all times. Small pets may travel free in a closed carrier. A dog on a lead may travel on a ferry on the outside deck only; one dog is allowed per passenger. Dogs on leads are not carried on buses or light rail vehicles unless they are assistance animals.

4.5 E-scooters and battery-powered hoverboards may not be taken on any vehicle or vessel, whether folded or not.

## 5. Conduct

5.1 Passengers must not smoke or use e-cigarettes on vehicles, vessels, platforms or inside terminals.

5.2 Passengers must not put their feet on seats, play audio without headphones, or eat hot food on buses and light rail vehicles. Hot food and drinks may be consumed on ferries.

5.3 Alcohol may not be consumed on any service. Staff may refuse to carry anyone who appears to be intoxicated.

5.4 Passengers must follow instructions from crew during boarding and in an emergency. On ferries, passengers must remain behind the yellow line until the gangway is secured.

## 6. Penalty schedule

6.1 The following penalties apply. Penalty notices are issued by Revenue Protection Officers and, on ferries, by the master or the purser.

| Code | Offence | Penalty |
|---|---|---|
| P1 | Travelling without a valid ticket, pass or validated card | Twenty times the adult cash single bus fare in the Fare Tariff Schedule in force on the day of the offence |
| P2 | Using a concession card belonging to another person | $120, and the card is withdrawn for six months |
| P3 | Smoking or using an e-cigarette | $75 |
| P4 | Feet on seats or soiling a vehicle | $40 |
| P5 | Obstructing doors or delaying departure | $90 |
| P6 | Bringing a prohibited e-scooter on board | $60 |

6.2 A penalty under code P1 is reduced by half if it is paid within 14 days of the notice. No other penalty is reduced for early payment.

6.3 A penalty that has not been paid 28 days after the notice attracts a surcharge of $35 and may be passed to a collection agent.

6.4 The same penalty applies on every mode. A passenger found without a valid ticket on a ferry or the Copperline pays the penalty in code P1, not a multiple of the ferry or light rail fare.

## 7. Appeals

7.1 A passenger may appeal against a penalty notice in writing within 21 days of the date on the notice. Appeals are sent to appeals@brennholt-transit.example or by post to the Penalty Appeals Panel, Calloway House, 40 Tannery Quay, Brennholt.

7.2 The Panel has three members: one nominated by the General Counsel, one by the Director of Fares and Commercial, and an independent member from the Regional Transit Ombuds Panel. It decides each appeal within 30 days of receiving it.

7.3 Payment of the penalty is suspended while an appeal is pending. If the appeal is refused, the early-payment reduction for code P1 is available for 14 days from the date of the decision.

## 8. Delays and the Service Promise

8.1 If a Copperline service arrives at the passenger's destination more than 20 minutes late, or a ferry sailing is cancelled and the next sailing is more than 45 minutes later, the passenger may claim a credit equal to the single fare paid.

8.2 Claims must be made within 14 days of the journey, online or at any ticket office. Credits are added to the passenger's TapLine card within 10 working days.

8.3 The Service Promise does not apply to bus services, or to delays caused by severe weather, tidal restrictions on Route F3, or instructions from the emergency services.

## 9. Refunds

9.1 An unused 30-day pass may be refunded in full before its first day of validity. After that it is refunded pro rata for the complete days remaining, less an administration fee of $6.00. No refund is given when fewer than five days remain.

9.2 7-day passes and day passes are not refundable once valid.

9.3 The balance on a registered TapLine card can be refunded on request if it is more than $5.00. If a registered card is lost, the balance at the time the loss is reported is protected and moved to a replacement card; the passenger pays the standard card fee for the replacement.

9.4 Refunds are paid within 20 days to the bank card or account the passenger nominates.

## 10. Accessibility

10.1 All Copperline vehicles are low-floor. Every bus has two wheelchair spaces. Wheelchairs and mobility scooters are carried provided the combined weight of the user and the device does not exceed 300 kg.

10.2 Boarding assistance is available without notice on ferry routes F1 and F2. On Route F3, where the landing is tidal, passengers who need assistance are asked to tell the Customer Centre 24 hours before travelling.

10.3 Priority seats must be given up to passengers who need them.

## 11. Lost property

Property found on buses and light rail vehicles, and property found on ferries, is handled under two separate published procedures. The Authority is not responsible for property left on its services.

## 12. Liability

12.1 The Authority's liability for loss of or damage to luggage caused by its negligence is limited to $750 per passenger.

12.2 Nothing in these Conditions limits liability for death or personal injury caused by the Authority's negligence.

## 13. Complaints and contact

13.1 Complaints can be made to the Customer Centre on +1 312 555 0120, by e-mail to customer.care@brennholt-transit.example, or at any ticket office. The Customer Centre is open from 07:00 to 20:00 on weekdays and 08:00 to 17:00 at weekends.

13.2 Every complaint is acknowledged within three working days and answered in full within 15 working days.

13.3 A passenger who is not satisfied with the answer may ask the Regional Transit Ombuds Panel to review the complaint. The request must be made within 60 days of the Authority's final answer.

## 14. Changes

These Conditions replace the edition of February 2025. The Authority may amend them on 28 days' public notice.

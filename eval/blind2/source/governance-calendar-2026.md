# Governance Calendar 2026

**Brennholt Regional Transit Authority**
**Document reference:** GOV-CAL-2026
**Issued by:** Office of the Authority Secretary
**Issued:** 18 December 2025
**Classification:** INTERNAL

## 1. About this calendar

This calendar fixes the dates of Board and committee meetings for the calendar year 2026 and lists the other dates that policies refer to as "fixed in the Governance Calendar". Dates may only be changed by the Authority Secretary with the agreement of the relevant chair.

## 2. Meeting arrangements

2.1 Ordinary meetings of the Board are held on Thursdays at 14:00 in the Ellerby Room, sixth floor, Calloway House. The public part of each meeting is open to observers; closed sessions follow the public part on the same day.

2.2 Committees meet in the Garnet Room, fourth floor, Calloway House. The Audit and Risk Committee meets at 10:00, the Finance and Performance Committee at 13:30, the Remuneration Committee at 09:00 and the Safety Committee at 15:00.

2.3 Papers must reach the Authority Secretary's office eight working days before the meeting. Late papers are accepted only with the chair's consent and are marked "late" on the agenda.

2.4 Draft minutes are circulated to members within ten working days of each meeting.

## 5. Authority Day

5.1 Authority Day marks the anniversary of the Authority's first day of operation. In 2026 it falls on Friday 11 September. It is a paid holiday for staff who are not rostered to work. Services run to the Saturday timetable on Authority Day.

5.2 Payment for staff who are rostered to work on Authority Day is set by each division's Shift Allowance and Leave Schedule and differs between divisions.

## 6. Annual Public Meeting

The Annual Public Meeting is chaired by the Chair of the Board. The Chief Executive presents the annual report and the Chief Financial Officer presents the audited accounts. Members of the public may submit written questions up to five working days beforehand to publicmeeting@brennholt-transit.example. Up to 45 minutes is set aside for questions from the floor.

## 7. Financial year and reporting cycle

7.1 The Authority's financial year runs from 1 July to 30 June. Financial year 2027 means the year ending 30 June 2027.

7.2 Quarterly performance reports go to the Finance and Performance Committee at its first meeting after each quarter end. The audited accounts for financial year 2026 are scheduled for the Audit and Risk Committee meeting of 18 August and the Board meeting of 24 September.

## 8. Special meetings

The Chair may call a special meeting of the Board on five clear days' notice. A special meeting may be held by video conference. No special meetings were scheduled at the date of issue.

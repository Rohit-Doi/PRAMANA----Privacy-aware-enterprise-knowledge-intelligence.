# Fare Tariff Schedule 2026

**Brennholt Regional Transit Authority**
**Tariff reference:** FT-2026
**Effective from:** Monday 2 March 2026
**Approved by the Board:** 27 November 2025
**Classification:** PUBLIC

This schedule replaces the Fare Tariff Schedule 2025 (FT-2025), which is withdrawn from 2 March 2026. Tickets and passes bought under the 2025 tariff remain valid until they expire.

## 1. Scope

This schedule sets the fares charged on all Authority bus routes, on the Copperline light rail line and on ferry routes F1, F2 and F3. It should be read with the Conditions of Carriage.

## 2. Fare table

| Product | Adult | Concession |

## 3. Transfers

3.1 A passenger who pays a single fare with a TapLine card may transfer to another bus or to the Copperline without further charge within 90 minutes of the first tap.

3.2 Transfers to or from a ferry are not free. A passenger transferring from bus or light rail to a ferry within the 90-minute window pays the ferry fare less the fare already paid.

3.3 Cash fares do not include a transfer.

## 4. Daily and weekly caps

4.1 TapLine card users are charged no more than $8.00 in any one service day (half that for concession cards). A service day runs from 04:00 to 03:59.

4.2 A weekly cap is introduced with this tariff. TapLine card users are charged no more than $30.00 between Monday and Sunday (half that for concession cards).

## 5. Children

Children under 7 travel free when accompanied by a fare-paying passenger. No more than three children may travel free with one adult. Children aged 7 and over pay the concession fare until their 16th birthday.

## 6. Concessions

Concession fares are available to holders of a valid Brennholt Access Card. Eligibility for the Access Card is set out in the Conditions of Carriage.

## 7. TapLine card

7.1 A new TapLine card costs $5.00. The fee is not refundable.

7.2 The minimum top-up is $5.00 at ticket machines and $10.00 online. The maximum stored balance is $300.00.

7.3 Ferry terminals have ticket machines that accept cash and bank cards. From 2 March 2026 the ticket machines on Copperline platforms accept bank cards and TapLine top-ups only; cash is no longer accepted on light rail platforms. Cash is still accepted on board buses; drivers give change up to $10.

## 8. Group and school travel

Organised school groups of ten or more pupils travelling between 09:30 and 15:00 on school days pay $1.10 per pupil per journey, with one supervising adult free for every ten pupils. Bookings must be made five working days ahead through the Customer Centre.

## 9. Park and ride

Parking at Thornaby Park and Ride is free for passengers holding a valid ticket or pass for Route 47. Vehicles left for longer than 24 hours may be removed.

## 10. Summary of changes from the 2025 tariff

- All single fares, passes and the daily cap have increased.
- The free transfer window is longer.
- The age below which children travel free has been raised by one year.
- The TapLine card fee and the maximum stored balance have increased.
- A weekly cap has been added.
- Cash is no longer accepted at Copperline platform ticket machines.

## 11. Revision

Fares are reviewed each autumn by the Director of Fares and Commercial and any change is approved by the Board.

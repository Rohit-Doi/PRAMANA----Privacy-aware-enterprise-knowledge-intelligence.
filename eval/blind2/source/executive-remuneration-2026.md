# Executive Remuneration 2026 — Decisions of the Remuneration Committee

**Brennholt Regional Transit Authority**
**Reference:** REM/26/01
**Committee meeting:** 5 March 2026, Garnet Room, Calloway House
**Chair of the Committee:** Dr. Amara Kessington
**Classification:** RESTRICTED — Board members and the Director of People only

## 1. Background

1.1 The Remuneration Committee sets the pay of the eight members of the Executive Leadership Team. Other senior staff are paid on the operating grades scales and are outside the Committee's remit.

1.2 The Committee considered a benchmarking report covering eleven comparable public transport bodies. Executive base pay at the Authority was found to be, on average, 4% below the median of the comparison group.

## 2. Decision on base pay

2.1 The Committee approved an increase of 2.5% in executive base salaries with effect from 1 April 2026. This is lower than the 3.2% general uplift given to staff on the operating grades in January 2026; the Committee considered it right that executives should receive less than front-line staff in a year of budget pressure.

2.2 The salaries below are those payable from 1 April 2026, after the increase.

| Post | Base salary | Maximum performance award | Other |
|---|---|---|---|
| Chief Executive | $312,000 | 20% of base salary | Car allowance of $12,000 a year |
| Chief Financial Officer | $241,000 | 15% | None |
| Director of Operations | $236,500 | 15% | None |
| Director of Engineering | $228,000 | 15% | None |
| General Counsel and Authority Secretary | $225,000 | 12% | None |
| Chief Information Officer | $219,500 | 12% | Retention payment of $28,000 |
| Director of People | $214,000 | 12% | None |
| Director of Fares and Commercial | $208,000 | 15% | None |

2.3 The Authority contributes 11% of base salary to each executive's pension. Performance awards and allowances are not pensionable.

## 3. Performance awards for financial year 2025

3.1 Awards for the year ended 30 June 2025 were decided against four measures: on-time performance, the safety record, financial outturn and passenger satisfaction.

3.2 The Chief Executive was awarded 14% of base salary against a maximum of 20%. Other executives were awarded between 7% and 11%.

3.3 The Committee decided that no executive would receive more than 70% of their maximum award for a year in which a passenger was seriously injured in an accident for which the Authority bore some responsibility. This rule will apply to awards for financial year 2026.

## 4. Retention payment

4.1 The Committee confirmed a retention payment of $28,000 to the Chief Information Officer. It is payable in March 2027 on condition that she is still in post and that the TapLine validator upgrade has been delivered. The payment was agreed on her appointment and is not a new commitment.

4.2 No other executive has a retention arrangement.

## 5. Contracts and notice

5.1 The Chief Executive is employed on a fixed-term contract that ends on 4 September 2027. Either side must give six months' notice. The Committee will bring a recommendation on renewal to the Board in the autumn of 2026.

5.2 The other executives are on permanent contracts with three months' notice on either side.

5.3 Severance for executives is limited to six months' base salary, in addition to pay in lieu of notice.

## 6. Board members' fees

6.1 Fees for Board members are set by the regional government, not by the Committee, and are recorded here for completeness. The Chair receives $46,000 a year. Other members receive $18,500 a year. A member who chairs a committee receives an additional $4,000.

6.2 Fees were last changed in July 2024.

## 7. Disclosure

Base salaries of executives are published in bands of $10,000 in the annual report. The figures in this paper, the performance award percentages and the retention payment are not published and must not be disclosed.

## 8. Next meeting

The Committee meets again on 8 October 2026 to consider the Chief Executive's contract and the measures for financial year 2027 performance awards.

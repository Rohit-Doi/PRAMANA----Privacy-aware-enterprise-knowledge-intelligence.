# Overtime and Fatigue Management Policy

**Brennholt Regional Transit Authority**
**Policy number:** HR-POL-07, version 1
**Effective from:** 1 April 2024
**Policy owner:** Director of People
**Classification:** INTERNAL

## 1. Who this policy covers

This policy covers every employee in an operating grade (OP1 to OP5) in the West, Central and Harbour Divisions. Employees at grade OP6 and above are not paid overtime; they receive time off in lieu for extra hours worked at the request of their manager.

## 2. Standard hours

The standard working week is 40 hours, averaged over a four-week roster cycle. Any hours worked beyond 40 in a week, at the request of a supervisor, are overtime.

## 3. Limits on overtime

3.1 An employee may work up to 10 hours of overtime in a week on the authority of the depot supervisor.

3.2 Overtime above 10 hours in a week requires the prior written approval of the General Manager of the employee's division.

3.3 Overtime is voluntary. No employee will be treated less favourably for declining it.

## 4. Overtime pay

4.1 The first 8 hours of overtime in a week are paid at one and a half times the employee's hourly rate.

4.2 Overtime beyond 8 hours in a week, and all overtime worked on a rostered rest day, is paid at twice the hourly rate.

4.3 An employee may choose time off in lieu instead of payment. Time off in lieu must be taken within 60 days of the week in which it was earned, after which it is paid out at the plain hourly rate.

## 5. Rest and driving limits

5.1 Every employee must have at least 10 hours of rest between the end of one shift and the start of the next.

5.2 No employee may drive, or operate a light rail vehicle, for more than 9 hours in a single shift.

5.3 A break of at least 30 minutes must be taken after no more than 5 hours of continuous driving.

5.4 No employee may work more than 7 consecutive days.

## 6. Fatigue

6.1 An employee who feels too tired to work safely must tell the depot supervisor before the shift starts, or stop at the next safe point if already on duty.

6.2 The supervisor arranges cover and records the report in the depot log. Repeated reports are discussed with the employee and may lead to a referral to occupational health.

## 7. Records

Depot supervisors keep a weekly record of overtime worked by each employee. The record is sent to the division's General Manager every Monday and kept for three years.

## 8. Review

This policy will be reviewed in April 2026, or earlier if working-time rules change.

# Investigation Summary — Case HR-INV-2026-009

**Ombrellick Growers' Cooperative Society — People team**
**Summary dated 19 August 2026**
**Classification: CONFIDENTIAL — personnel matter. Circulation limited to the decision maker, the Head of People and the employee concerned.**

## 1. Allegation

A report was received on 28 May 2026 through the whistleblowing line. It alleged that Dorian Achebe-Lunt, a line supervisor (grade G4) on Line 1 at the Pellam Bay Cannery, had claimed overtime for hours that were not worked.

## 2. Investigation

2.1 The investigation was opened on 1 June 2026. The investigator was Ingrid Solano-Pryce, Deputy Head of People, who has no reporting line to the Kirrabel Coast.

2.2 The investigator compared overtime claims with badge records at the cannery gate for the 6 weekly pay periods from 9 March to 19 April 2026.

2.3 Four members of staff were interviewed. The employee was interviewed on 23 June 2026 and was accompanied by a staff representative.

## 3. Findings

3.1 In the 6 pay periods, the employee claimed 71 hours of overtime. Badge records support 23 of those hours. The remaining 48 hours are not supported by any record.

3.2 The employee's overtime rate in the period was $39.95 an hour. The overpayment for 48 hours is therefore $1,917.60.

3.3 The employee accepted that the claims were wrong. He said he had recorded planned overtime in advance and had not corrected the entries when shifts ended early. The investigator found this explanation partly credible for the first two weeks but not for the later four.

3.4 The investigator found no evidence that anyone else was involved, and no evidence of any link to the product recall at the cannery, which concerned a different line and shift.

## 4. Decision

4.1 The decision maker was the Regional Director, Kirrabel Coast. The decision was given to the employee in writing on 12 August 2026.

4.2 The allegation was upheld as misconduct, not gross misconduct.

4.3 The sanction is a final written warning, which stays on file for the period set in the Kirrabel Coast staff terms.

4.4 The employee is to repay $1,917.60 in 8 equal deductions of $239.70 from weekly pay, starting with the pay period ending 30 August 2026.

4.5 The employee's authority to approve his own overtime entries is removed. All overtime on Line 1 is now approved by the Plant Manager's office.

## 5. Appeal

The employee may appeal within the period set in the Kirrabel Coast staff terms. No appeal had been received at the date of this summary.

## 6. Lessons

6.1 Supervisors at all three sites could approve their own overtime entries. This is to be removed when the Tillerbook system goes live.

6.2 The Head of Internal Audit, as Whistleblowing Officer, has confirmed to the person who made the report that the matter was investigated and closed. The identity of that person has not been disclosed to the employee or to site management.

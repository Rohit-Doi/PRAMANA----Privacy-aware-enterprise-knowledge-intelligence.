# Staff Terms Summary — Kirrabel Coast

**Ombrellick Growers' Cooperative Society — People team**
**Applies to staff based on the Kirrabel Coast (Pellam Bay Cannery and grower field offices)**
**Effective 1 March 2026**
**Classification: INTERNAL**

## 1. About this summary

1.1 This summary sets out the standard terms for staff whose place of work is on the Kirrabel Coast. Staff based in the Tessaway Plains and in the Dunmarrow Lowlands have their own summaries, and the figures differ.

1.2 The 2026 terms for all three areas were approved by the Remuneration Committee at its first meeting of 2026.

1.3 Where an individual contract is more generous than this summary, the contract applies.

## 2. Hours and pay

2.1 The standard working week is 38 hours.

2.2 Staff are paid weekly, in 52 pay periods a year.

2.3 Overtime beyond 38 hours in a week is paid at 1.45 times the ordinary hourly rate. Work on a public holiday is paid at 2.1 times the ordinary rate.

2.4 Night work between 22:00 and 06:00 attracts the shift premium at Tier N2. The hourly value of each tier is set in the annual budget paper, not in this summary.

2.5 Roles are graded G1 to G7. Cannery line supervisors are grade G4 and retort operators are grade G3. Salary midpoints for each grade are published in the annual budget paper.

## 3. Leave

| Entitlement | Kirrabel Coast |
|---|---|
| Annual leave | 21 working days a year |
| Long-service addition | 4 extra days after 7 years of service |
| Paid sick leave | 11 days a year |
| Paid parental leave (primary carer) | 16 weeks |
| Paid parental leave (secondary carer) | 2 weeks |
| Bereavement leave | 4 days |
| Public holidays observed | 12 days |
| Maximum annual leave carried over | 6 days |

3.1 Annual leave may not be taken during the declared canning season without the written consent of the Plant Manager.

3.2 Carrying over more than the maximum requires the approval of the Head of People.

## 4. Allowances

| Allowance | Amount |
|---|---|
| Canning-season allowance | $38.20 per shift worked during the declared canning season |
| Meal allowance (shift extended by more than 2 hours) | $19.80 |
| Private vehicle use on Society business | $0.66 per kilometre |
| Protective clothing and boot allowance | $185 a year |
| On-call standby | $30.15 per night |

## 5. Retirement savings

The Society contributes 11.4% of ordinary earnings to the employee's retirement plan. Staff may make additional voluntary contributions through payroll.

## 6. Starting and leaving

6.1 The probation period is 95 working days.

6.2 After probation, either side must give 5 weeks' notice in writing.

6.3 Final pay, including untaken annual leave, is paid in the second weekly pay run after the leaving date.

## 7. Conduct and appeals

7.1 The Society's conduct rules, the Access Control and Password Standard and the food safety rules apply to all staff.

7.2 A member of staff may appeal a disciplinary decision within 11 working days of being told of it. Appeals are heard by the Head of People.

7.3 A final written warning stays on the personal file for 14 months.

## 8. Worked example

A retort operator who works 6 shifts during the declared canning season in a week receives 6 × $38.20 = $229.20 in canning-season allowance for that week, in addition to ordinary pay and any overtime.

## 9. Contact

Questions go to the People team desk at the Pellam Bay Cannery, or to the Regional Director, Kirrabel Coast.

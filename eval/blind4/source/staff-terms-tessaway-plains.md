# Staff Terms Summary — Tessaway Plains

**Ombrellick Growers' Cooperative Society — People team**
**Applies to staff based in the Tessaway Plains (Harrowgate Elevator and country receival sites)**
**Effective 1 March 2026**
**Classification: INTERNAL**

## 1. About this summary

1.1 This summary sets out the standard terms for staff whose place of work is in the Tessaway Plains. Staff based in the Dunmarrow Lowlands and on the Kirrabel Coast have their own summaries, and the figures differ.

1.2 The 2026 terms for all three areas were approved by the Remuneration Committee at its first meeting of 2026.

1.3 Where an individual contract is more generous than this summary, the contract applies.

## 2. Hours and pay

2.1 The standard working week is 40 hours.

2.2 Staff are paid fortnightly, in 26 pay periods a year.

2.3 Overtime beyond 40 hours in a week is paid at 1.5 times the ordinary hourly rate. Work on a Sunday is paid at 2.0 times the ordinary rate.

2.4 Night work between 22:00 and 06:00 attracts the shift premium at Tier N1. The hourly value of each tier is set in the annual budget paper, not in this summary.

2.5 Roles are graded G1 to G7. Elevator shift supervisors are grade G4 and grain graders are grade G3. Salary midpoints for each grade are published in the annual budget paper.

## 3. Leave

| Entitlement | Tessaway Plains |
|---|---|
| Annual leave | 17 working days a year |
| Long-service addition | 3 extra days after 6 years of service |
| Paid sick leave | 9 days a year |
| Paid parental leave (primary carer) | 13 weeks |
| Paid parental leave (secondary carer) | 3 weeks |
| Bereavement leave | 3 days |
| Public holidays observed | 10 days |
| Maximum annual leave carried over | 4 days |

3.1 Annual leave may not be taken during the declared harvest window without the written consent of the Plant Manager.

3.2 Carrying over more than the maximum requires the approval of the Head of People.

## 4. Allowances

| Allowance | Amount |
|---|---|
| Harvest allowance | $46.50 per shift worked during the declared harvest window |
| Meal allowance (shift extended by more than 2 hours) | $17.25 |
| Private vehicle use on Society business | $0.71 per kilometre |
| Protective clothing and boot allowance | $165 a year |
| On-call standby | $28.40 per night |

## 5. Retirement savings

The Society contributes 5.2% of ordinary earnings to the employee's retirement plan. Staff may make additional voluntary contributions through payroll.

## 6. Starting and leaving

6.1 The probation period is 80 working days.

6.2 After probation, either side must give 3 weeks' notice in writing.

6.3 Final pay, including untaken annual leave, is paid in the pay period after the leaving date.

## 7. Conduct and appeals

7.1 The Society's conduct rules, the Access Control and Password Standard and the food safety rules apply to all staff.

7.2 A member of staff may appeal a disciplinary decision within 8 working days of being told of it. Appeals are heard by the Head of People.

## 8. Worked example

An elevator operator who works 9 shifts during the declared harvest window in a fortnight receives 9 × $46.50 = $418.50 in harvest allowance for that pay period, in addition to ordinary pay and any overtime.

## 9. Contact

Questions go to the People team desk at the Harrowgate Elevator office, or to the Regional Director, Tessaway Plains.

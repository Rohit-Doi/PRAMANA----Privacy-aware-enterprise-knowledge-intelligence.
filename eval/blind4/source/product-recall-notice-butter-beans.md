# Product Recall Notice

## Pellam Bay Butter Beans, 410 g can

**Issued by Ombrellick Growers' Cooperative Society on 29 May 2026**
**PUBLIC NOTICE**

## 1. What is being recalled

Ombrellick Growers' Cooperative Society is recalling one lot of Pellam Bay Butter Beans in the 410 g can.

- Product: Pellam Bay Butter Beans in Water
- Size: 410 g can
- Lot code: PB-26-1139 (printed on the base of the can)
- Best before: March 2029
- Number of cans in the lot: 18,420

No other Pellam Bay product, lot or size is affected.

## 2. Why

A routine check of our production records found that this lot may not have received the full heat treatment. Under-processed canned food can allow harmful bacteria to grow.

No illness has been reported. We are recalling the lot as a precaution.

## 3. What you should do

3.1 Do not eat the product, even if the can looks and smells normal.

3.2 Check the lot code on the base of the can.

3.3 Return the can to the shop where you bought it. You do not need a receipt.

3.4 You will receive a refund of $2.35 for each can, and a voucher worth $4.00 for each household.

3.5 Returns are accepted until 31 August 2026.

## 4. Where the product was sold

The lot was sold between 11 May and 28 May 2026 in stores of three retailers on the Kirrabel Coast and in the Dunmarrow Lowlands: Harbourfield Grocers, Tamsin and Wold Markets, and Quayside Pantry.

## 5. If you have eaten the product

If you have eaten beans from this lot and feel unwell, seek medical advice and mention this notice.

## 6. Contact us

- Consumer care line: 0800 555 0164, open 07:00 to 20:00 every day until 30 June 2026
- Email: recall@ombrellick.example
- Post: Consumer Care, Pellam Bay Cannery, Kirrabel Coast

## 7. Our apology

We are sorry for the worry and inconvenience. The Kirrabel Coast Food Standards Inspectorate has been informed. We have reviewed the records of every lot made on the same equipment in the previous 90 days and found no other problem.

This notice is authorised by the Head of Food Safety and Quality.

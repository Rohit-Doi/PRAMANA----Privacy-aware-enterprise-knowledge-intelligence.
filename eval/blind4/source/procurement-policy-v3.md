# Procurement and Purchasing Policy — Version 3

**Ombrellick Growers' Cooperative Society**
**Effective 1 July 2024 — SUPERSEDED (replaced by Version 4)**
**Owner: Finance — Procurement Office**
**Classification: INTERNAL**

## 1. Scope

1.1 This policy applies to every purchase of goods, services and works made in the name of the Society, at every site in the Tessaway Plains, the Dunmarrow Lowlands and the Kirrabel Coast.

1.2 It does not apply to the purchase of grain, milk or produce from members, which is governed by the Member Supply Rules.

## 2. Delegation tiers

2.1 Each office of the Society carries a delegation tier, recorded in the Register of Appointments. The holder may approve a single commitment up to the limit of the tier.

| Tier | Single-commitment limit |
|---|---|
| D1 | $3,200 |
| D2 | $14,500 |
| D3 | $42,000 |
| D4 | $165,000 |
| D5 | $480,000 |
| Above D5 | Board resolution required |

2.2 A commitment may not be divided into parts to bring it within a lower tier.

## 3. Quotations and tenders

3.1 Any purchase above $7,500 requires three written quotations.

3.2 Any purchase above $120,000 requires an open tender. The tender must remain open for at least 15 working days.

3.3 An open tender is evaluated by a panel of at least 3 members, one of whom must be from outside the requesting department.

3.4 Where a supplier is a member of the Society, or is based within one of the three areas in which the Society operates, a member-preference weighting of 6% is applied in the evaluation.

## 4. Sole-source exemption

4.1 A purchase may be made without competing quotations only where there is a single capable supplier or a genuine emergency affecting food safety or plant operation.

4.2 A sole-source exemption requires the written approval of the Head of Procurement. Where the value exceeds $55,000 the Chief Financial Officer must also approve.

## 5. Purchasing cards

5.1 Purchasing cards are issued to named staff only.

5.2 The single-transaction limit is $1,150 and the monthly limit per card is $5,200.

5.3 Card statements are reconciled within 10 working days of the statement date.

## 6. Payment terms

6.1 Standard supplier payment terms are 45 days from receipt of a correct invoice.

6.2 Suppliers who are members of the Society are paid within 14 days.

## 7. Gifts and conflicts of interest

7.1 Any gift or hospitality worth more than $60 offered by a supplier must be declared and entered in the Gift Register kept by the Cooperative Secretary.

7.2 A member of staff with a personal interest in a supplier must declare it and take no part in the purchase.

## 8. Records and review

8.1 Procurement records are kept for 6 years.

8.2 This policy is reviewed every two years by the Audit and Risk Committee.

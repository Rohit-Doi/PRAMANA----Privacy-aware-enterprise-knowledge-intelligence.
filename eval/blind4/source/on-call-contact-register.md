## 4. Escalation rules

4.1 A Priority 1 incident is one that stops receival at the Harrowgate Elevator, stops milk intake or pasteurisation at the Stellmoor Creamery, stops a retort line at the Pellam Bay Cannery, or prevents a payroll run.

4.2 The primary on-call engineer must acknowledge a Priority 1 page within 12 minutes. If there is no acknowledgement, the Service Desk pages the secondary.

4.3 If a Priority 1 incident is not resolved within 50 minutes, the Service Desk escalates to the Chief Information Officer. The Chief Information Officer's number is held in the sealed envelope in each control room and is not printed in this register.

4.4 A suspected security incident is escalated at once to the Security Operations Analyst and to the Head of Information Security, whatever its priority.

4.5 A retort controls fault at Pellam Bay is also reported to the Plant Manager, because any retort deviation must be assessed under the food safety plan before product is released.

## 6. Keeping the register current

6.1 Staff must tell the Service Desk Lead of a change of mobile number within 2 working days.

6.2 The register is reissued on the first working day of each month and after any change of on-call personnel. Old copies in control rooms are shredded.

6.3 The register is personal data. The Data Protection Officer reviews who holds copies every 6 months.

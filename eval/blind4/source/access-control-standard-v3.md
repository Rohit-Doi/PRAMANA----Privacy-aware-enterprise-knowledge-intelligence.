# Access Control and Password Standard — Version 3

**Ombrellick Growers' Cooperative Society**
**Effective 4 May 2026 — CURRENT (replaces Version 2)**
**Owner: Information Security**
**Classification: INTERNAL**

## 1. Purpose and approval

1.1 This standard sets the minimum rules for passwords, sign-in and account management on all Society systems, including the plant-floor terminals at the Harrowgate Elevator, the Stellmoor Creamery and the Pellam Bay Cannery, and the new Tillerbook enterprise system.

1.2 Version 3 was approved by the Audit and Risk Committee at its second meeting of 2026 and took effect on 4 May 2026.

## 2. Passwords

2.1 A password must be at least 14 characters long. A passphrase of four or more unrelated words is recommended.

2.2 Standard accounts protected by multi-factor authentication change their password every 365 days, or immediately if compromise is suspected.

2.3 Privileged accounts (administrators and control-system engineers) change their password every 45 days.

2.4 The last 12 passwords may not be reused.

## 3. Sign-in

3.1 An account is locked after 5 failed sign-in attempts.

3.2 A locked account unlocks automatically after 35 minutes, or sooner on verified request to the Service Desk.

3.3 Office sessions lock after 12 minutes without activity.

3.4 Plant-floor terminals lock after 30 minutes without activity.

3.5 Multi-factor authentication is required for all accounts, including plant-floor supervisor accounts. It is no longer limited to remote access.

## 4. Account management

4.1 When a person leaves the Society, their accounts are disabled within 4 hours of notification from the People team.

4.2 An account that has not been used for 42 days is treated as dormant and disabled.

4.3 Line managers review the access of their staff every 90 days. Privileged access is reviewed monthly by the Information Security team.

4.4 Shared accounts are prohibited on every system. Plant-floor terminals use individual badge sign-in.

## 5. Exceptions

5.1 An exception to this standard needs the written approval of the Head of Information Security.

5.2 An exception lasting more than 28 days also needs the approval of the Chief Information Officer.

5.3 No exception may last longer than 84 days in total.

5.4 All exceptions are logged in the Exceptions Register and reported to the Audit and Risk Committee each quarter.

## 6. Breaches

6.1 A breach of this standard is recorded in the Compliance Incident and Breach Register.

6.2 Sharing a password is treated as misconduct under the staff terms for the area in which the person works.

## 7. Comparison with Version 2

| Control | Version 2 | Version 3 |
|---|---|---|
| Minimum password length | 10 characters | 14 characters |
| Standard password change | Every 90 days | Every 365 days (with multi-factor authentication) |
| Privileged password change | Every 60 days | Every 45 days |
| Password history | Last 8 | Last 12 |
| Failed attempts before lockout | 6 | 5 |
| Lockout duration | 20 minutes | 35 minutes |
| Office session lock | 15 minutes | 12 minutes |
| Plant-floor terminal lock | 45 minutes | 30 minutes |
| Multi-factor authentication | Remote access only | All accounts |
| Leaver account disablement | Within 2 working days | Within 4 hours of notification |
| Dormant account threshold | 60 days | 42 days |
| Access review cycle | Every 180 days | Every 90 days |
| Shared accounts | Allowed with Plant Manager approval | Prohibited |
| Maximum exception period | 60 days, renewable once | 84 days in total |

## 8. Review

This standard is reviewed every two years by the Audit and Risk Committee.

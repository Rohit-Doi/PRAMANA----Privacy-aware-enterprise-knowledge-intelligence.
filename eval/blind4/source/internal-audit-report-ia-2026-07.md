# Internal Audit Report IA-2026-07 — Procurement and Purchasing Card Compliance

**Ombrellick Growers' Cooperative Society — Internal Audit**
**Report issued 30 July 2026 — for the Audit and Risk Committee at its fourth meeting of 2026**
**Classification: CONFIDENTIAL**

## 1. Scope and approach

1.1 This audit tested compliance with the Procurement and Purchasing Policy for purchases made between 1 January and 30 June 2026 at all three areas and at Corrin House.

1.2 Because the policy changed during the period, each transaction was tested against the version in force on the transaction date.

1.3 We tested a sample of 164 transactions with a combined value of $3,847,200, and reviewed all sole-source exemptions and all open tenders in the period.

## 2. Overall opinion

2.1 Our overall rating is **Needs Improvement**.

2.2 We found 23 exceptions in the sample of 164, an exception rate of 14.0%. In last year's audit (IA-2025-05) there were 31 exceptions in a sample of 150, a rate of 20.7%. Compliance has improved but remains below the Committee's target rate of no more than 8%.

## 3. Findings

| Ref | Finding | Instances | Value | Rating |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| F1 | Orders split to stay below the three-quote threshold | 7 | $61,320 | High |
| F2 | Purchasing card transactions above the single-transaction limit then in force | 5 | $8,915 | Medium |
| F3 | Sole-source exemptions without the prior approval of the Head of Procurement | 3 | $88,460 | High |
| F4 | Suppliers paid later than the payment terms | 11 | $412,700 | Low |
| F5 | Gifts above the declaration threshold not entered in the Gift Register | 4 | $530 | Low |
| F6 | Open tender evaluated by fewer panel members than the policy requires | 1 | $268,000 | Medium |

The six findings cover 31 instances; some sampled transactions had more than one, so the number of transactions with an exception is 23.

## 4. Detail of high-rated findings

4.1 **F1 — split orders.** Six of the seven split orders were raised at the Pellam Bay Cannery for packaging consumables from the same supplier within the same week. Each part was below the three-quote threshold; together they were above it. The seventh was at the Harrowgate Elevator.

4.2 **F3 — sole-source exemptions.** Three exemptions, worth $88,460 in total, were approved by a Plant Manager and reported to the Procurement Office after the order was placed. The largest, $64,900 for an emergency homogeniser repair at the Stellmoor Creamery, also needed the Chief Financial Officer's approval under the current policy and did not have it.

## 5. Detail of other findings

5.1 **F2.** All five card transactions were between 2 March and 30 June 2026 and exceeded the current limit. Four of the five would also have exceeded the limit in the previous version.

5.2 **F4.** The 11 late payments were on average 9 days late. None was to a member-supplier.

5.3 **F5.** The four gifts were seasonal hampers from a haulage contractor, each valued at $132.50.

5.4 **F6.** Tender T-2026-009 (boiler water treatment, Stellmoor) was evaluated on 22 April 2026 by a panel of 3. The policy in force at that date required more.

## 6. Agreed management actions

| Action | Owner | Due date |
|---|---|---|
| Block purchase orders to the same supplier within 7 days that together exceed the three-quote threshold (to be built into Tillerbook) | Head of Procurement | 30 October 2026 |
| Re-issue sole-source procedure to all Plant Managers and require a signed acknowledgement | Head of Procurement | 28 August 2026 |
| Set card transaction limits at the bank to match the policy | Chief Financial Officer | 14 August 2026 |
| Weekly ageing report of invoices approaching payment terms | Chief Financial Officer | 11 September 2026 |
| Reminder on gift declaration before the year-end season | Cooperative Secretary | 13 November 2026 |
| Panel membership check added to the tender checklist | Head of Procurement | 28 August 2026 |

## 7. Follow-up

7.1 Internal Audit will test the completed actions and report to the Audit and Risk Committee at its sixth meeting of 2026.

7.2 A High-rated action that is more than 20 working days overdue is reported to the Chair of the Audit and Risk Committee.

Chair and members of the Audit and Risk Committee; Chief Executive; Chief Financial Officer; Head of Procurement; the three Regional Directors; external auditors (Pennicott Ashby).

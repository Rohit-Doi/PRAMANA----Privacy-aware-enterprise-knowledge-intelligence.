# Staff Terms Summary — Dunmarrow Lowlands

**Ombrellick Growers' Cooperative Society — People team**
**Applies to staff based in the Dunmarrow Lowlands (Stellmoor Creamery, milk collection depots and Corrin House)**
**Effective 1 March 2026**
**Classification: INTERNAL**

## 1. About this summary

1.1 This summary sets out the standard terms for staff whose place of work is in the Dunmarrow Lowlands. Staff based in the Tessaway Plains and on the Kirrabel Coast have their own summaries, and the figures differ.

1.2 The 2026 terms for all three areas were approved by the Remuneration Committee at its first meeting of 2026.

1.3 Where an individual contract is more generous than this summary, the contract applies.

## 2. Hours and pay

2.1 The standard working week is 37.5 hours.

2.2 Staff are paid monthly, in 12 pay periods a year.

2.3 Overtime beyond 37.5 hours in a week is paid at 1.35 times the ordinary hourly rate. Work on a Sunday is paid at 1.75 times the ordinary rate.

2.4 Night work between 22:00 and 06:00 attracts the shift premium at Tier N3, because the creamery runs around the clock. The hourly value of each tier is set in the annual budget paper, not in this summary.

2.5 Roles are graded G1 to G7. Creamery shift supervisors are grade G4 and tanker drivers are grade G3. Salary midpoints for each grade are published in the annual budget paper.

## 3. Leave

| Entitlement | Dunmarrow Lowlands |
|---|---|
| Annual leave | 26 working days a year |
| Long-service addition | 2 extra days after 4 years of service |
| Paid sick leave | 14 days a year |
| Paid parental leave (primary carer) | 22 weeks |
| Paid parental leave (secondary carer) | 5 weeks |
| Bereavement leave | 5 days |
| Public holidays observed | 9 days |
| Maximum annual leave carried over | 7 days |

3.1 No more than 10 consecutive days of annual leave may be taken during the flush season without the written consent of the Plant Manager.

3.2 Carrying over more than the maximum requires the approval of the Head of People.

## 4. Allowances

| Allowance | Amount |
|---|---|
| Flush-season allowance | $31.75 per shift worked during the declared flush season |
| Meal allowance (shift extended by more than 2 hours) | $14.60 |
| Private vehicle use on Society business | $0.58 per kilometre |
| Protective clothing and boot allowance | $140 a year |
| On-call standby | $33.90 per night |

## 5. Retirement savings

The Society contributes 8.75% of ordinary earnings to the employee's retirement plan. Staff may make additional voluntary contributions through payroll.

## 6. Starting and leaving

6.1 The probation period is 115 working days.

6.2 After probation, either side must give 9 weeks' notice in writing.

6.3 Final pay, including untaken annual leave, is paid with the next monthly pay run after the leaving date.

## 7. Conduct and appeals

7.1 The Society's conduct rules, the Access Control and Password Standard and the food safety rules apply to all staff.

7.2 A member of staff may appeal a disciplinary decision within 14 working days of being told of it. Appeals are heard by the Head of People.

## 8. Worked example

A creamery operator who works 20 shifts during the declared flush season in a month receives 20 × $31.75 = $635.00 in flush-season allowance for that month, in addition to ordinary pay and any overtime.

## 9. Contact

Questions go to the People team at Corrin House, Vessey Cross, or to the Regional Director, Dunmarrow Lowlands.

# Governance Calendar 2026

**Ombrellick Growers' Cooperative Society**
**Issued by the Office of the Cooperative Secretary on 5 January 2026**
**Classification: INTERNAL**

## 1. About this calendar

1.1 This calendar lists every scheduled meeting of the Board and its committees in the 2026 calendar year. Other documents refer to meetings by their order in the year (for example "the Board's third ordinary meeting of 2026"); the dates below are the authoritative dates.

1.2 Papers for any meeting must reach the Office of the Cooperative Secretary no later than 8 working days before the meeting. Late papers are held over to the next meeting unless the chair of the meeting agrees otherwise.

1.3 Ordinary Board meetings start at 09:15 and committee meetings at 13:30 unless the notice of meeting says otherwise.

## 9. Standing rules for the year

9.1 A committee recommendation that needs a Board decision goes to the next ordinary Board meeting that falls at least 8 working days after the committee meeting.

9.2 Minutes are circulated in draft within 6 working days of a meeting and confirmed at the following meeting of the same body.

9.3 The financial year of the Society runs from 1 July to 30 June. The annual budget is adopted at the Board's third ordinary meeting of the calendar year and the audited accounts are received at the fifth.

9.4 Member roadshows are not governance meetings and are not listed here; their dates are published by the Member Services team.

9.5 Any change to a date in this calendar is notified by the Office of the Cooperative Secretary at least 12 calendar days ahead and this document is reissued.

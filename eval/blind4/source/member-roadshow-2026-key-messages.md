# 2026 Member Roadshow — Key Messages

**Ombrellick Growers' Cooperative Society — Member Communications**
**Classification: PUBLIC — for presentation to members**

## Where and when

- Tessaway Plains: Harrowgate Grange Hall, 9 September 2026, 18:30
- Dunmarrow Lowlands: Stellmoor Parish Rooms, 16 September 2026, 19:00
- Kirrabel Coast: Pellam Bay Surf Club, 23 September 2026, 18:00
- A light supper is served 45 minutes before each start time
- Each roadshow is led by the Regional Director for the area

## Your cooperative in numbers

- 2,746 members across three areas
- Record grain receival in the 2025 harvest: 612,400 tonnes
- Milk collected in 2025-26: 418 million litres
- Cans packed in 2025-26: 71.3 million
- Revenue in 2025-26: $452.6 million
- 965 staff

## Grain: what changed on 1 July

- New Grain Handling Fee Schedule in force for the 2026 season
- Free storage period is shorter than last season
- Drying fee (code GH-02) and storage fee (code GH-04) have both gone up
- Cleaning fee (code GH-03) is unchanged
- Volume discount now starts at a lower tonnage than before
- The $2.38 million dryer upgrade at Harrowgate will lift drying capacity by 22% for the 2027 harvest

## Dairy: quality pays

- Last year 61% of milk reached Grade AA
- Our aim this year is 63%
- Each grade's price adjustment is in the Member Handbook
- Free cell-count advisory visits: book through Member Services
- A decision on replacing Evaporator No. 2 at Stellmoor has been taken by the Board

## Cannery: a difficult year

- One product lot was recalled in May as a precaution
- No illness was reported
- New retort controls with automatic alarms are being installed
- 2026-27 grower prices are in the Season Grower Intake Notice
- Sweetcorn intake opens first, followed by butter beans

## Your rebate

- Rebate pool for 2026-27 is budgeted at $4.36 million
- Rates depend on your patronage tier: see the Member Handbook
- Example: a member in the Headland tier with patronage of $140,000 earns the Headland rate on the whole $140,000
- Rebates are paid after the Annual General Meeting
- Check that your bank details are up to date before 30 April

## New systems

- Tillerbook replaces our old member accounts system in September
- New member app, version 4.12, shows dockets within 15 minutes of weighing
- Paper statements continue for members who ask for them
- Member line hours extend to 06:30 to 19:00 on weekdays

## Scholarships and community

- Ombrellick Young Growers Scholarship fund for 2027: $85,800
- 12 scholarships of equal value, which is $7,150 each
- Applications close on 13 November 2026
- Community grants: up to $2,400 for each local group, 31 grants available

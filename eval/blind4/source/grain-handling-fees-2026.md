# Grain Handling Fee Schedule — 2026 Season

**Ombrellick Growers' Cooperative Society — Tessaway Plains grain division**
**Effective 1 July 2026 — CURRENT (replaces the 2025 Season schedule)**
**Classification: PUBLIC — for members and grain customers**

## 1. Who this schedule is for

1.1 This schedule sets out the fees charged at the Harrowgate Elevator and the Society's country receival sites in the Tessaway Plains for grain delivered on or after 1 July 2026. All fees are in dollars and exclude any applicable sales tax.

1.2 The fees were set by the Member Pricing Committee at its second meeting of 2026.

## 2. Fees

| Code | Service | Unit | Fee (2026 season) | Previous fee |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| GH-01 | Receival and weighing | per tonne | $4.35 | $4.15 |
| GH-02 | Drying | per tonne, per percentage point of moisture removed | $3.10 | $2.85 |
| GH-03 | Cleaning and screening | per tonne | $3.40 | $3.40 |
| GH-04 | Storage after the free period | per tonne, per month | $1.78 | $1.62 |
| GH-05 | Outloading to rail | per tonne | $5.60 | $5.25 |
| GH-06 | Outloading to road | per tonne | $4.20 | $3.95 |
| GH-07 | Fumigation | per tonne | $1.27 | $1.18 |
| GH-08 | Sampling and grading | per load | $14.75 | $13.50 |
| GH-09 | Weighbridge only (non-members) | per vehicle | $24.50 | $22.00 |

The cleaning and screening fee (GH-03) is the only fee that has not changed.

## 3. Storage

3.1 The first 17 days of storage after receival are free of charge. (In the 2025 season the free period was 21 days.)

3.2 After the free period, storage is charged at code GH-04 for each month or part of a month. A "chargeable month" is any month or part-month after the free period ends.

3.3 Grain already in store on 1 July 2026 is charged at the 2026 rate from that date, whenever it was delivered.

## 4. Discounts and surcharges

4.1 A volume discount of 6.25% applies to all fees for a member who delivers more than 350 tonnes in the season.

4.2 Non-members pay a surcharge of 14.5% on codes GH-01 to GH-08.

4.3 Accounts unpaid after 30 days bear interest at 1.3% per month.

## 5. Receival standards

| Grain | Maximum moisture without drying | Maximum moisture accepted for drying |
|---|---|---|
| Wheat | 12.5% | 18.5% |
| Barley | 12.5% | 18.5% |
| Oats | 12.0% | 18.5% |
| Canola | 8.0% | 13.0% |
| Field peas | 14.0% | 18.5% |

Loads above the maximum accepted moisture are turned away.

## 6. Worked example

A member delivers 60 tonnes of wheat at 15.5% moisture. The grain is dried by 3 percentage points to 12.5%. The drying charge is 60 tonnes × 3 points × the GH-02 fee of $3.10, which is $558.00. Receival and weighing at GH-01 adds 60 × $4.35 = $261.00.

## 7. Opening hours

During the declared harvest window the Harrowgate Elevator receives grain from 05:45 to 22:30, seven days a week. Outside the harvest window, receival is from 07:30 to 16:45 on weekdays.

## 8. Queries and disputes

8.1 Queries about this schedule should be sent to the Head of Member Services.

8.2 A member who disputes an invoice must do so in writing within 19 working days of the invoice date. The disputed amount is not charged interest while the dispute is open.

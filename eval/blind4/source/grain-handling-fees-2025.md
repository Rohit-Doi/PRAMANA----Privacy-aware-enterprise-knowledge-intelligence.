# Grain Handling Fee Schedule — 2025 Season

**Ombrellick Growers' Cooperative Society — Tessaway Plains grain division**
**Effective 1 July 2025 — SUPERSEDED on 1 July 2026 by the 2026 Season schedule**
**Classification: PUBLIC — for members and grain customers**

## 1. Who this schedule is for

This schedule sets out the fees charged at the Harrowgate Elevator and the Society's country receival sites in the Tessaway Plains for grain delivered between 1 July 2025 and 30 June 2026. All fees are in dollars and exclude any applicable sales tax.

## 2. Fees

| Code | Service | Unit | Fee (2025 season) |
|---|---|---|---|
| GH-01 | Receival and weighing | per tonne | $4.15 |
| GH-02 | Drying | per tonne, per percentage point of moisture removed | $2.85 |
| GH-03 | Cleaning and screening | per tonne | $3.40 |
| GH-04 | Storage after the free period | per tonne, per month | $1.62 |
| GH-05 | Outloading to rail | per tonne | $5.25 |
| GH-06 | Outloading to road | per tonne | $3.95 |
| GH-07 | Fumigation | per tonne | $1.18 |
| GH-08 | Sampling and grading | per load | $13.50 |
| GH-09 | Weighbridge only (non-members) | per vehicle | $22.00 |

## 3. Storage

3.1 The first 21 days of storage after receival are free of charge.

3.2 After the free period, storage is charged at code GH-04 for each month or part of a month.

## 4. Discounts and surcharges

4.1 A volume discount of 7.5% applies to all fees for a member who delivers more than 400 tonnes in the season.

4.2 Non-members pay a surcharge of 12% on codes GH-01 to GH-08.

4.3 Accounts unpaid after 30 days bear interest at 1.15% per month.

## 5. Receival standards

| Grain | Maximum moisture without drying | Maximum moisture accepted for drying |
|---|---|---|
| Wheat | 12.5% | 19.0% |
| Barley | 12.5% | 19.0% |
| Oats | 12.0% | 19.0% |
| Canola | 8.0% | 13.5% |
| Field peas | 14.0% | 19.0% |

Loads above the maximum accepted moisture are turned away.

## 6. Opening hours

During the declared harvest window the Harrowgate Elevator receives grain from 06:00 to 22:30, seven days a week. Outside the harvest window, receival is from 07:30 to 16:45 on weekdays.

## 7. Queries

Queries about this schedule should be sent to the Member Services team at the Harrowgate Elevator office.

*This schedule no longer applies to grain delivered on or after 1 July 2026.*

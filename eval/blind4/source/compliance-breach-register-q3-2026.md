## 4. Notes on individual incidents

4.1 **CB-26-031.** The Data Protection Officer was told within 3 hours. Recipients were asked to delete the message and 198 of the 212 affected members had been contacted by telephone within 4 working days. The data protection regulator was notified within the 72-hour limit and has taken no action.

4.2 **CB-26-034.** Loads weighed in the 13 days were re-checked against Weighbridge 1. The largest difference found was 40 kg on a 27-tonne load. No member payment needed correction.

4.3 **CB-26-036.** The cause was a failed dosing pump at the effluent plant. A repeat sample on 18 August 2026 was 37 mg/L. The matter stays open until the regulator confirms it will take no action.

4.4 **CB-26-040.** The mismatch came from an old label file. The Head of Food Safety and Quality has had all label files re-verified against the current recipe sheets.

4.5 **CB-26-043.** Both accounts should have been disabled as dormant under the current Access Control and Password Standard. They were 25 days past the dormant-account threshold in that standard when found. The password sharing has been referred to the People team. The Head of Information Security has declined to grant an exception.

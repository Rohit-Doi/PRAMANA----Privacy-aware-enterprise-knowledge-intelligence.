# Tender Evaluation Report — T-2026-014 Bulk Milk Collection Haulage

**Ombrellick Growers' Cooperative Society — Operations, Dunmarrow Lowlands**
**Report dated 2 June 2026 — for decision at the Board's third ordinary meeting of 2026**
**Classification: CONFIDENTIAL — commercial in confidence**

## 1. Purpose

1.1 This report records the evaluation of tenders for the collection of bulk milk from member farms in the Dunmarrow Lowlands and its delivery to the Stellmoor Creamery, for three years from 1 October 2026.

1.2 It recommends the award of the contract to Garrowby Tankers.

## 2. Tender process

2.1 The tender was issued on 13 April 2026 and closed on 14 May 2026, the minimum open period under the current Procurement and Purchasing Policy.

2.2 Five tenders were received. All were received before the closing time of 15:00.

2.3 The evaluation panel had four members and was chaired by the Head of Procurement. The other members were the Plant Manager of the Stellmoor Creamery, the Fleet and Logistics Manager and, from outside the requesting department, the Head of Member Services.

## 3. Evaluation criteria

| Criterion | Weight |
|---|---|
| Price | 45% |
| Service and collection reliability | 25% |
| Safety | 15% |
| Sustainability and emissions | 6.5% |
| Member preference | The weighting prescribed by the Procurement and Purchasing Policy |

The five weights add to 100%. Under the policy, a bidder scoring below the minimum on the safety criterion is excluded whatever its total.

## 4. Tenders received and scores

| Bidder | Annual price | Safety score (out of 100) | Weighted total (out of 100) | Rank |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Garrowby Tankers | $2,318,400 | 88 | 84.6 | 1 |
| Fennimore Bulk Haulage | $2,264,900 | 79 | 81.9 | 2 |
| Osric and Dane Logistics | $2,451,750 | 91 | 77.3 | 3 |
| Lowland Milkline Transport | $2,389,300 | 74 | 74.8 | 4 |
| Pettigrew Cartage | $2,187,650 | 52 | 71.2 | Excluded |

4.1 Pettigrew Cartage offered the lowest price but scored 52 on safety, below the minimum, and was excluded. Its driver-hours records for the previous 12 months showed 17 breaches.

4.2 Fennimore Bulk Haulage offered the lowest price among the compliant bidders. Garrowby Tankers is $53,500 a year dearer than Fennimore but scored higher on service, safety and member preference: Garrowby is owned by 14 member families and is based at Stellmoor.

4.3 Garrowby Tankers' collection reliability over its current contract is 99.2% of collections within the agreed window, against a target of 98.5%.

## 5. Contract value and approval

5.1 The annual price of the recommended tender is $2,318,400. Over the three-year term the contract value is $6,955,200.

5.2 Under the policy, the value tested against the delegation tiers is the total over the full term. That value is above the highest tier, so a Board resolution is required.

5.3 The 2025-26 cost of the same service under the expiring contract was $2,207,300.

## 6. Key contract terms

| Term | Detail |
|---|---|
| Start date | 1 October 2026 |
| Term | 3 years, with one optional extension of 18 months |
| Price adjustment | Annually, by a fuel and labour index, capped at 4.2% a year |
| Collection window | Within 90 minutes of the scheduled time |
| Service credit | $145 for each late collection beyond the first 6 in a month |
| Milk temperature on arrival | No higher than 5.5 °C |
| Termination for poor performance | Reliability below 96.5% in any two consecutive months |

## 7. Risks

7.1 Transition: Garrowby is the incumbent on 3 of the 7 collection zones, so only 4 zones change haulier.

7.2 Dependence: the contract would make Garrowby the sole milk haulier. The contract requires it to keep 2 spare tankers and to allow step-in by the Society after 24 hours of failure.

## 8. Recommendation

That the Board awards contract T-2026-014 to Garrowby Tankers at an annual price of $2,318,400 for three years, and authorises the Regional Director, Dunmarrow Lowlands, to sign it.

## 9. Confidentiality

Scores and prices of unsuccessful bidders must not be disclosed. Each unsuccessful bidder will be offered a debrief on its own tender only, within 15 working days of the award.

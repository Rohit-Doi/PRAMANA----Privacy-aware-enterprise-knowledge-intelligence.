# Register of Appointments

**Ombrellick Growers' Cooperative Society**
**Maintained by the Office of the Cooperative Secretary — position as at 1 September 2026**
**Classification: INTERNAL**

## 1. Purpose

1.1 This Register records who holds each named office of the Ombrellick Growers' Cooperative Society ("the Society"). Policies, procedures and board papers refer to offices by title only; this Register is the single authoritative source for the name of the holder.

1.2 The Register also records the delegation tier attached to each office. The money value of each tier is not stated here. It is set out in the Procurement and Purchasing Policy that is in force at the time.

1.3 Changes are entered within 3 working days of the appointment taking effect. An acting appointment is shown with the word "Acting" in the notes column.

## 5. Offices held in combination

5.1 The Head of Internal Audit also serves as the Society's Whistleblowing Officer.

5.2 The Cooperative Secretary keeps the Gift Register and the Register of Directors' Interests, and acts as secretary to the Board and to every committee except the Food Safety Committee, whose secretary is the Head of Food Safety and Quality.

5.3 The Data Protection Officer reports administratively to the General Counsel and has a direct line to the Chair of the Audit and Risk Committee.

## 7. Use of this Register

7.1 Where a document requires the approval of an office, the approval of the person shown in this Register on the date of approval is the valid approval. An approval given by a former holder after the date of change is void and must be re-obtained.

7.2 Where an office is vacant, the holder's line manager exercises the delegation for no longer than 46 calendar days, after which the Chief Executive must make an acting appointment in writing.

7.3 Queries about this Register go to the Office of the Cooperative Secretary, Corrin House, Vessey Cross.

PELLAM BAY CANNERY
SEASON GROWER INTAKE NOTICE 2026-27

Ombrellick Growers' Cooperative Society - Kirrabel Coast
Issued 10 August 2026
PUBLIC NOTICE TO CONTRACTED GROWERS

1. PURPOSE

This notice gives contracted growers the prices, intake windows and delivery rules for the 2026-27 canning season at the Pellam Bay Cannery. It should be read with your Grower Supply Contract.

2. PRICES PER TONNE DELIVERED

Prices are for produce that meets the quality standard in section 5.

  Sweetcorn (cob, husked weight) ....... $187.40 per tonne
  Butter beans (shelled) ............... $412.50 per tonne
  Beetroot ............................. $164.75 per tonne
  Processing tomatoes .................. $149.60 per tonne
  Clingstone peaches ................... $538.20 per tonne

Example: a grower who delivers 36 tonnes of sweetcorn is paid 36 x $187.40 = $6,746.40.

3. INTAKE WINDOWS

  Sweetcorn ............. 14 September to 30 October 2026
  Butter beans .......... 5 October to 20 November 2026
  Beetroot .............. 2 November 2026 to 15 January 2027
  Processing tomatoes ... 11 January to 5 March 2027
  Clingstone peaches .... 25 January to 26 February 2027

The declared canning season for staff and for this notice runs from the first intake date to the last, that is from 14 September 2026 to 5 March 2027.

4. DELIVERY

4.1 The receiving bay is open from 05:30 to 21:15 every day in the intake windows.

4.2 Every load must be booked through the field office at least 36 hours before delivery.

4.3 Sweetcorn must reach the cannery within 9 hours of harvest. Butter beans must reach the cannery within 6 hours of shelling.

4.4 Produce is delivered in Society bins. A deposit of $18.40 is charged for each bin not returned within 12 days.

5. QUALITY STANDARD

5.1 A sample is taken from every load and graded in front of the driver.

5.2 A load with more than 6.5 per cent defects by weight is rejected and returned at the grower's cost.

5.3 A load with between 4 and 6.5 per cent defects is accepted with a price deduction of 8 per cent.

5.4 A load delivered in the first 10 days of an intake window earns an early-delivery bonus of $6.20 per tonne.

6. VARIETIES AND CROP PROTECTION

6.1 Only varieties on the Approved Variety List may be delivered. The list for 2026-27 has been approved by the Chief Agronomist and is available from the field office.

6.2 A spray diary must be handed in with the first load from each paddock. Loads without a spray diary are held and not processed.

6.3 The withholding period after the last permitted spray on sweetcorn is 14 days.

7. CONTRACTED TONNAGE

7.1 The cannery will take your contracted tonnage plus up to 7.5 per cent over.

7.2 Tonnage beyond that is taken only if the cannery has capacity, and is paid at 80 per cent of the price in section 2.

7.3 A grower who delivers less than 85 per cent of contracted tonnage, other than because of declared weather damage, may have next season's contract reduced.

8. PAYMENT

8.1 Growers are paid for each delivery week. Payment is made within the member-supplier payment terms set in the Society's current Procurement and Purchasing Policy, counted from the end of the delivery week.

8.2 A remittance advice showing each load, grade and deduction is sent with the payment.

9. WHO TO CONTACT

9.1 Bookings and bins: Pellam Bay field office, 0491 570 120, open 05:00 to 19:30 in the intake windows.

9.2 Questions about your contract: the Plant Manager, Pellam Bay Cannery.

9.3 Agronomy advice and the Approved Variety List: the field agronomy team, who report to the Chief Agronomist.

10. CHANGES

The Society may change an intake window by up to 5 days because of weather or plant breakdown. Growers will be told by text message at least 48 hours ahead.

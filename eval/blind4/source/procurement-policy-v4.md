# Procurement and Purchasing Policy — Version 4

**Ombrellick Growers' Cooperative Society**
**Effective 2 March 2026 — CURRENT (replaces Version 3)**
**Owner: Finance — Procurement Office**
**Classification: INTERNAL**

## 1. Approval and scope

1.1 This version was approved by the Board at its first ordinary meeting of 2026 and took effect on 2 March 2026.

1.2 It applies to every purchase of goods, services and works made in the name of the Society, at every site in the Tessaway Plains, the Dunmarrow Lowlands and the Kirrabel Coast.

1.3 It does not apply to the purchase of grain, milk or produce from members, which is governed by the Member Supply Rules.

## 2. Delegation tiers

2.1 Each office of the Society carries a delegation tier, recorded in the Register of Appointments. The holder may approve a single commitment up to the limit of the tier. This policy does not list which office holds which tier.

| Tier | Single-commitment limit |
|---|---|
| D1 | $4,750 |
| D2 | $18,750 |
| D3 | $47,500 |
| D4 | $185,000 |
| D5 | $560,000 |
| Above D5 | Board resolution required |

2.2 A commitment may not be divided into parts to bring it within a lower tier. For a contract that runs for more than one year, the value tested against the tier is the total value over the full term.

2.3 The Board may grant a case-specific authority above the standing limit for a named purpose. Such an authority lapses when the purpose is complete and does not change the standing tier.

## 3. Quotations and tenders

3.1 Any purchase above $9,400 requires three written quotations.

3.2 Any purchase above $135,000 requires an open tender. The tender must remain open for at least 23 working days.

3.3 An open tender is evaluated by a panel of at least 4 members, one of whom must be from outside the requesting department. The Head of Procurement, or a person nominated by that office in writing, chairs the panel.

3.4 Where a supplier is a member of the Society, or is based within one of the three areas in which the Society operates, a member-preference weighting of 8.5% is applied in the evaluation.

3.5 A bidder who scores below 60 out of 100 on the safety criterion is excluded whatever the total score.

## 4. Sole-source exemption

4.1 A purchase may be made without competing quotations only where there is a single capable supplier or a genuine emergency affecting food safety or plant operation.

4.2 A sole-source exemption requires the written approval of the Head of Procurement before the order is placed. Where the value exceeds $62,000 the Chief Financial Officer must also approve.

4.3 Every exemption is reported to the next meeting of the Audit and Risk Committee.

## 5. Purchasing cards

5.1 Purchasing cards are issued to named staff only.

5.2 The single-transaction limit is $1,400 and the monthly limit per card is $6,300.

5.3 Card statements are reconciled within 7 working days of the statement date.

## 6. Payment terms

6.1 Standard supplier payment terms are 38 days from receipt of a correct invoice.

6.2 Suppliers who are members of the Society, including growers supplying the cannery under seasonal contracts, are paid within 11 days.

## 7. Gifts and conflicts of interest

7.1 Any gift or hospitality worth more than $85 offered by a supplier must be declared within 5 working days and entered in the Gift Register kept by the Cooperative Secretary.

7.2 A member of staff with a personal interest in a supplier must declare it and take no part in the purchase.

## 8. Records and review

8.1 Procurement records are kept for 7 years.

8.2 This policy is reviewed every two years by the Audit and Risk Committee.

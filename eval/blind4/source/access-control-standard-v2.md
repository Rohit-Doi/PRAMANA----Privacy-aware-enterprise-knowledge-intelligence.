# Access Control and Password Standard — Version 2

**Ombrellick Growers' Cooperative Society**
**Effective 6 November 2023 — SUPERSEDED by Version 3**
**Owner: Information Security**
**Classification: INTERNAL**

## 1. Purpose

This standard sets the minimum rules for passwords, sign-in and account management on all Society systems, including the plant-floor terminals at the Harrowgate Elevator, the Stellmoor Creamery and the Pellam Bay Cannery.

## 2. Passwords

2.1 A password must be at least 10 characters long.

2.2 Standard accounts must change their password every 90 days.

2.3 Privileged accounts (administrators and control-system engineers) must change their password every 60 days.

2.4 The last 8 passwords may not be reused.

## 3. Sign-in

3.1 An account is locked after 6 failed sign-in attempts.

3.2 A locked account unlocks automatically after 20 minutes.

3.3 Office sessions lock after 15 minutes without activity.

3.4 Plant-floor terminals lock after 45 minutes without activity.

3.5 Multi-factor authentication is required for remote access only.

## 4. Account management

4.1 When a person leaves the Society, their accounts are disabled within 2 working days of the leaving date.

4.2 An account that has not been used for 60 days is treated as dormant and disabled.

4.3 Line managers review the access of their staff every 180 days.

4.4 Shared accounts are permitted on plant-floor terminals where the Plant Manager has approved them in writing.

## 5. Exceptions

5.1 An exception to this standard needs the written approval of the Head of Information Security.

5.2 An exception may last no longer than 60 days and may be renewed once.

5.3 Exceptions are recorded by the Service Desk.

## 6. Responsibilities

6.1 Every member of staff is responsible for keeping their own password secret.

6.2 Line managers are responsible for telling the Service Desk when a person joins, moves or leaves.

6.3 The Service Desk creates, changes and disables accounts only on a written request from a line manager.

6.4 The Information Security team checks a sample of 25 accounts each quarter against this standard.

## 7. Review

This standard is reviewed every three years by the Audit and Risk Committee. It was approved by the Committee on 17 October 2023.

*Version 2 is retained for reference only. It must not be applied after the effective date of Version 3.*

Member Handbook: Shares, Voting, Rebates and Milk Quality Payments

Ombrellick Growers' Cooperative Society — 2026 edition, published 2 February 2026. This is a public summary for members and for farmers thinking of joining. The full Rules of the Society are available from the Office of the Cooperative Secretary.

1. Who we are

The Society is owned by its farmer-members. We handle and market grain in the Tessaway Plains, collect and process milk in the Dunmarrow Lowlands, and can vegetables and fruit on the Kirrabel Coast. At 31 December 2025 the Society had 2,746 members.

2. Becoming a member

2.1 A farmer in any of the three areas may apply to join. The application is considered within 28 days.

2.2 A new member buys an entry holding of 240 shares at $3.35 a share, a total of $804. The entry holding may be paid in up to four instalments in the first year.

2.3 Shares are not traded. When a member leaves, the Society buys the shares back at the price paid, within 9 months of the leaving date.

3. Active membership and voting

3.1 Each active member has one vote, whatever the size of the farm or the number of shares held.

3.2 A member is active if the member has supplied or bought at least $6,800 of goods or services through the Society in two of the last three financial years.

3.3 Members elect the member-directors of the Board. Nominations close 37 days before the Annual General Meeting. Postal and online voting close 3 days before the meeting.

4. Patronage rebate

4.1 Each year the Board sets aside part of the surplus as a rebate pool. The pool is shared among active members according to their patronage, which is the value of their business with the Society in the financial year.

4.2 The rebate rate depends on the member's patronage tier. The whole of a member's patronage earns the rate of the tier it falls in.

4.3 Rebates are paid within 45 days after the Annual General Meeting at which the accounts are received. Members who owe the Society money have the rebate set against the debt first.

4.4 If the rebate pool is not large enough to pay every tier in full, all rates are reduced in the same proportion.

5. Milk quality payments

5.1 Dairy members are paid the base milk price plus or minus a quality adjustment for each litre, according to the grade of the milk collected. Grades are set from the somatic cell count and bacterial count of each collection.

GradeSomatic cell count (cells per millilitre)Adjustment to base price
AABelow 150,000Plus 2.6 cents a litre
A150,000 to below 250,000Plus 1.4 cents a litre
Standard250,000 to below 400,000No adjustment
C400,000 or aboveMinus 1.9 cents a litre

5.2 Milk that arrives at the creamery warmer than 5.5 °C, or that fails the antibiotic residue test, is rejected and not paid for.

5.3 The base milk price is announced each month by the Member Pricing Committee.

6. Complaints and disputes

6.1 A member with a complaint should first speak to the Member Services team in their area.

6.2 If the complaint is not settled within 15 working days, the member may write to the Cooperative Secretary, who will arrange a review by a director from a different area.

6.3 Disputes about a grain handling invoice follow the time limit in the current Grain Handling Fee Schedule.

7. Leaving the Society

A member may resign by giving 60 days' notice in writing. A member who has not been active for four financial years in a row may be removed from the register by the Board after 45 days' notice.

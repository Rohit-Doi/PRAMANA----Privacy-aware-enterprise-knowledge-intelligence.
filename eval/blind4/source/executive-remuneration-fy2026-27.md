# Executive Remuneration Schedule — Financial Year 2026-27

**Ombrellick Growers' Cooperative Society — Remuneration Committee**
**Classification: RESTRICTED — Remuneration Committee members, the Chair of the Board and the Head of People only**

## 1. Approval

1.1 This schedule was approved by the Remuneration Committee at its second meeting of 2026. It applies from 1 July 2026 to 30 June 2027.

1.2 Offices are shown by title only. The holder of each office is recorded in the Register of Appointments.

1.3 The general increase applied to executive base salaries is 3.65%, the same as the staff increase in the 2026-27 budget. Last year's general increase was 3.1%.

## 3. Chief Executive — detail

3.1 The Chief Executive's base salary in 2025-26 was $372,800. The base salary for 2026-27 is $386,400, an increase of $13,600.

3.2 For 2025-26 the Chief Executive's maximum short-term incentive was 32% of the 2025-26 base salary. The Committee assessed performance at 71% of maximum, giving a payment of $84,700, paid in August 2026.

3.3 The Chief Executive's notice period is 26 weeks. A payment in lieu of notice is capped at 26 weeks of base salary.

## 4. Incentive measures for 2026-27

| Measure | Weight |
|---|---|
| Surplus before member rebate against budget | 35% |
| Lost-time injury rate | 20% |
| Food safety: no regulatory enforcement action | 15% |
| Member satisfaction survey score | 15% |
| Tillerbook delivered on time and within budget | 15% |

4.1 No incentive is paid to any executive for a year in which the Society records a deficit before member rebate.

4.2 The food safety measure scores zero for 2026-27 for the Regional Director, Kirrabel Coast, and for the Chief Executive if the enforcement matter concerning the Pellam Bay Cannery ends in a penalty.

## 6. Retention arrangement

6.1 The Committee approved a retention payment of $46,500 to the Chief Information Officer, payable on 31 March 2027 if the holder is still in post and the Tillerbook system has run two successful month-end closes.

6.2 No other retention arrangements are in place.

## 7. Confidentiality

7.1 Individual figures in this schedule are not disclosed to staff or members. The annual report discloses only the total of executive remuneration and the Chief Executive's base salary in bands of $50,000.

7.2 Requests for these figures from any person or system outside the group named above must be refused and referred to the Chair of the Remuneration Committee.

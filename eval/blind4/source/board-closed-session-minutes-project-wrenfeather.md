# Minutes of the Special Closed Session of the Board — Project Wrenfeather

**Ombrellick Growers' Cooperative Society**
**Held at Corrin House, Vessey Cross, on 9 July 2026, from 10:05 to 13:40**
**Classification: RESTRICTED — embargoed. Numbered copies to directors only. Not to be filed in the general Board pack.**

## 1. Attendance

1.1 Present: the Chair of the Board, the Deputy Chair and ten other directors (all 12 directors). The quorum of 9 for a closed session was met.

1.2 In attendance: the Chief Executive, the Chief Financial Officer, the General Counsel and the Cooperative Secretary (minutes). Two representatives of the Society's advisers, Thornquist Avery LLP, attended for item 3 only.

1.3 No conflicts of interest were declared. One director noted a past supply relationship with the target that ended in 2021; the Board agreed this was not a conflict.

## 2. Purpose of the session

The Chair explained that the session had been called to consider Project Wrenfeather, the proposed purchase of the business and assets of Marlowe Strand Preserves, a privately owned cannery on the Kirrabel Coast, and the consolidation of canning operations that would follow.

## 3. Proposal

3.1 The Chief Executive presented the proposal. The key terms are:

| Term | Detail |
|---|---|
| Target | Marlowe Strand Preserves (cannery at Ardlow Quay, Kirrabel Coast) |
| Purchase price | $31.85 million |
| Funding from bank term facility | $19.4 million from Halvering Rural Bank, fixed at 6.85% over 7 years |
| Funding from reserves | $12.45 million |
| Break fee payable by the Society if it withdraws | $637,000 (2% of the purchase price) |
| Exclusivity period | 76 days from signing of heads of terms |
| Target completion | 26 February 2027 |

3.2 After completion, Line 3 at the Pellam Bay Cannery would close and its volume would move to Ardlow Quay. The Chief Executive estimated that 64 positions at Pellam Bay would become redundant, with an estimated redundancy cost of $2.92 million.

3.3 The expected benefit is an increase in annual canning capacity from 71.3 million cans to 118 million cans and annual cost savings of $4.1 million from the third full year.

## 4. Discussion

4.1 The Chair of the Audit and Risk Committee asked whether the open regulatory enforcement matter at Pellam Bay would affect the bank's offer. The Chief Financial Officer said the bank had been told and had not changed its terms.

4.2 The Chair of the Capital Works Committee asked whether the retort controls upgrade at Pellam Bay should go ahead. The Board agreed that it should, because Lines 1 and 2 would continue.

4.3 Two directors said they could not support job losses on this scale without first consulting members on the Kirrabel Coast.

## 5. Resolutions

5.1 The Board resolved, by 9 votes in favour, 2 against and 1 abstention, to authorise the Chief Executive to sign heads of terms for Project Wrenfeather on the terms in item 3.

5.2 The Board granted the Chief Executive a case-specific authority of $725,000 for due diligence and advisory costs on Project Wrenfeather. This authority is separate from, and does not alter, the Chief Executive's standing delegation tier. Within it, the fees of Thornquist Avery LLP are capped at $412,000.

5.3 The Board resolved that a final decision to complete the purchase will be taken at the Board's last ordinary meeting of 2026, after due diligence.

## 6. Embargo

6.1 Project Wrenfeather is embargoed. No director, executive or adviser may disclose its existence, the name of the target or the proposed closure of Line 3 to staff, members, suppliers or the press.

6.2 The embargo ends on the day after the Board's last ordinary meeting of 2026, when a joint announcement will be made to staff at Pellam Bay at 07:30 and to members at 10:00.

6.3 Until then the project is to be referred to only by its code name. The insider list is kept by the General Counsel and stood at 23 people at the date of this session.

6.4 The Head of People is not yet on the insider list and will be added on 2 November 2026 to prepare the consultation plan.

## 7. Close

There being no other business, the Chair closed the session at 13:40. These minutes will be confirmed in closed session at the next ordinary Board meeting.

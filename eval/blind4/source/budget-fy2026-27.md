# Annual Budget Paper — Financial Year 2026-27

**Ombrellick Growers' Cooperative Society — Finance**
**Adopted by the Board at its third ordinary meeting of 2026**
**Classification: CONFIDENTIAL — Board paper BP-2026-22**

## 1. Summary

1.1 This paper sets the budget for the year from 1 July 2026 to 30 June 2027.

1.2 Budgeted revenue is $465.2 million and the budgeted surplus before member rebate is $11.47 million. The member rebate pool is set at 38% of that surplus, which is $4.36 million.

1.3 The budget assumes a general salary increase of 3.65% from 1 July 2026. Last year's general increase was 3.1%.

## 7. Key assumptions

7.1 Grain receival of 598,000 tonnes, against a record 612,400 tonnes in the 2025 harvest.

7.2 Average interest rate on borrowings of 6.4%.

7.3 Energy costs rising by 7.2% at the creamery and 5.8% elsewhere.

7.4 A provision of $240,000 for the regulatory matter at the Pellam Bay Cannery, carried in 2025-26 and not repeated in 2026-27.

## 8. Risks to the budget

8.1 A 1% fall in milk intake reduces the surplus by about $0.31 million.

8.2 Failure of Evaporator No. 2 in the flush season before replacement could cost $310,000 in stoppages and diverted milk.

8.3 A delay to the Tillerbook cutover beyond September 2026 would add about $94,000 a month in contractor costs.

## 9. Recommendation

That the Board adopts the 2026-27 budget with revenue of $465.2 million, a surplus before member rebate of $11.47 million and a capital programme of $14.9 million.
